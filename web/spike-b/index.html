Engine preview: our own overworld Not the game, a test of how it could work. The world here is drawn by the page, with the real game's battles.
  • Walk and run like Emerald (hold B to walk).
  • Face a trainer and press A to battle.
  • Step on a rustling patch of grass (or a dark ripple on water) for a wild Pokémon, as in the game.
  • Spectate, then drag and pinch to see all of Hoenn and everyone in it.
