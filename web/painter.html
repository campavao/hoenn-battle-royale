Pick marks where the drop should put people: a town with any picks deals from them first, then its ordinary cells, then its doorsteps. Veto marks where nobody would ever stand (a yard behind a fence, a roof, a strip behind a building): it comes out of the drop, the ring and the bots. Click a cell to paint it, click again to clear. Drag to paint a rectangle; shift-drag to clear one. A pick has to be somewhere you can walk off from; a veto can go on anything feet could be on.
