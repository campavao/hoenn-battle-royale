# floor-dive: the floors you only reach by diving have something too (POK-413 review).
#
# floors.py walked warps and connections, and the way up out of the sea is a script
# (setdivewarp), so SEAFLOOR CAVERN, the SEALED CHAMBER and the ABANDONED SHIP's hidden
# corridors were never reached and stayed bare. DIVE is free in a match, so they are
# live. SEAFLOOR CAVERN ROOM1 (24:28) has no ball of its own: it is sRooms[153], cache
# key 0x8D99, at (12,8) -- (19,15) MAP_OFFSET in.
#
# The match goes on before the boot, so the room is laid out as a match's at its first load.
wait 30
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x1072
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 18 1C 0C 00 0A 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 24
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 28
shot room
# Row 0 is the DAY CARE chest (0x8E00), held all match; the cache is the next.
expect u16 gBrLoot+20 0x8D99
expect u16 gBrLoot+22 19
expect u16 gBrLoot+24 15
expect u8 gBrLoot+31 3
say floor dive ok
