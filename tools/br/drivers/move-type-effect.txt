# move-type-effect: the desktop's move menu says how a move lands (Cam's 2026-10-19
# play-test: "the one part where you gain an advantage on mobile is that you can see the
# type effectiveness on the moves"). pret's "TYPE/NORMAL" becomes "NORMAL ×0.5" in red,
# "×0.25" in red, "×0" in grey, "×1" plain; a move that does no damage keeps pret's line.
#
# menu-byte's patch battle, the TESTMON's POUND (NORMAL) top left and LEER beside it, and the foe's types (gBattleMons[1].types, +0x58+0x21) poked to what each case
# needs. The line is drawn again whenever the cursor lands on a move. gDisplayedStringBattle
# holds it: "NORMAL", a space at [6], then for a coloured one the colour code FC 04 at
# [7] with green 5 or red 7 at [9], and the × (B9) at [12].
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gBrPatch+16 1A 00
pokebytes gBrPatch+32 10 00
poke u8 gBrPatch+4 1
tap LEFT
wait 4
tap LEFT
wait 600
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 90
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
# ROCK: POUND is ×0.5, in red
pokebytes gBattleMons+0x79 05 05
tap A
wait 30
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 2
shot half
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle+6 0x00
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle+7 0xFC
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle+8 0x04
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle+9 7
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle+12 0xB9
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle+13 0xA1
# LEER does no damage: pret's TYPE/ line, its T first
tap RIGHT
wait 10
shot status
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle 0xCE
# ROCK and STEEL: ×0.25
pokebytes gBattleMons+0x79 05 08
tap LEFT
wait 10
shot quarter
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle+9 7
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle+16 0xA6
# GHOST: ×0, no colour
pokebytes gBattleMons+0x79 07 07
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap LEFT
wait 10
shot none
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle+7 0xB9
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle+8 0xA1
# GRASS: ×1, no colour
pokebytes gBattleMons+0x79 0C 0C
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap LEFT
wait 10
shot plain
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle+7 0xB9
expect u8 gDisplayedStringBattle+8 0xA2
