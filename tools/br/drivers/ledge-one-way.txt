# ledge-one-way: a ledge is jumped the way it faces and is a wall from every other side
# (HBR-62). Every ledge in Hoenn carries the collision bit, and the ROM asks
# ShouldJumpLedge before it looks at it (CheckForObjectEventCollision,
# field_player_avatar.c). export-world.py asked the collision first and wrote every ledge
# as a wall; bots/world.ts then let a trainer stand on one from any side. This pins the
# ROM's half of what the page now models.
#
# Route 101 (group 0, num 16): a south-facing ledge at (8,13), ground at (8,12) above it,
# (8,14) below it and (7,13) west of it. gBrOwnPos carries map coords + MAP_OFFSET (7).
wait 30
# BR_BOOT_MAP | TESTMON, male, Route 101, x=8, y=12: facing the ledge from above
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 10 08 00 0C 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gBrOwnPos 0
expect u8 gBrOwnPos+1 16
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+2 15
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+4 19
# Down onto it: the hop clears the ledge and lands two cells on, at (8,14).
hold DOWN 20
wait 60
shot jumped
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+2 15
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+4 21
# Back up into it from below: a wall. Long enough to walk two cells were it open.
hold UP 40
wait 30
shot below
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+2 15
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+4 21
# From the side, walking east along the ledge's row from (7,13): a wall too.
hold LEFT 16
wait 20
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+2 14
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+4 21
hold UP 16
wait 20
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+2 14
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+4 20
hold RIGHT 40
wait 30
shot side
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+2 14
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+4 20
say ledge one-way ok
