# spike-safari: spike B's Safari opening asks the hidden ROM for a wild battle as
# BR_WILD_ASKED_SAFARI (br_wild.h): the roll is the Zone's any-first-stage
# (BrZone_PickAny), outside the Safari phase, where the plain ask's BrZone_Pick rolls
# nothing. Which roll it was the driver cannot tell apart (any first stage can be a
# route's stand-in); what it checks is that the new state is taken, fights, and comes
# back DONE like a plain ask, and that a plain ask after it is plain again. The opening's
# battle is the Zone's own (BATTLE_TYPE_SAFARI, BALL/POKeBLOCK/GO NEAR/RUN) with the balls
# the page set and the POKeBLOCKs BR_BOOT_FLAG_SAFARIKIT gave. Then the drop: ASKED_PICK
# opens the fly map in pick mode and A sends the match's `pick`.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 91 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 D3 C9 CF FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 4
poke u8 gNumSafariBalls 30
# the Safari's eight POKeBLOCKs in the case
expectne u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x848 0
wait 2
# ROUTE 102 (0:17) tall grass, asked as the Safari's
pokebytes gBrWildAsk+1 00 11 02
poke u8 gBrWildAsk 4
wait 10
expect u8 gBrWildAsk 2
expectne u16 gBrWildAsk+4 0
wait 600
expectbits u16 gBattleTypeFlags 0x80 0x80
shot safari
tap B
wait 300
tap B
wait 300
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap A
wait 240
tap A
wait 300
# DONE, B_OUTCOME_RAN
expect u8 gBrWildAsk 3
expect u8 gBrWildAsk+7 4
# the same spot, the plain ask
poke u8 gBrWildAsk 1
wait 10
expect u8 gBrWildAsk 2
expectne u16 gBrWildAsk+4 0
wait 600
shot plain
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 4
say spike safari ok
# out of it through the plain battle's RUN (again if it failed), then the drop's fly map
tap B
wait 60
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap A
wait 240
tap A
wait 240
tap B
wait 60
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap A
wait 240
tap A
wait 240
tap B
wait 60
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap A
wait 240
tap A
wait 240
tap B
wait 60
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap A
wait 240
tap A
wait 240
expect u8 gBrWildAsk 3
poke u8 gBrWildAsk 6
wait 120
expect u8 gBrWildAsk 2
expect u8 gBrPick 1
shot pick
tap A
wait 30
# pick {seat 0, section LITTLEROOT TOWN (0)}, where the hidden field stands
expectmsg 24 u8 0 0
expectmsg 24 u16 1 0
say spike pick ok
