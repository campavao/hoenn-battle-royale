# gift-giver: a town's gift-giver (match/gifts.ts) is a one-item bag under a gift key
# (0xC000 | i, br_loot.h), standing as a person rather than lying as a ball. A on him
# takes it like any bag of one thing -- "A GIFT: LEFTOVERS!" -- and the PICKUP of his key
# takes him off every map.
#
# object-slots-near.txt's view: Route 103 from (9,8), so we stand at (16,15) facing south.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 12 09 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 9
# gift 1 (an OLD WOMAN), LEFTOVERS (200), on (16,16), the cell we face
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 00 12 00 01 01 C0 10 00 10 00 01 C8 00 01 00 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 11 gDecompressionBuffer 19
wait 30
expect u16 gBrLoot+0 0xC001
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 2
expect u16 gBrLoot+6 200
expectne u8 gBrLoot+12 0xFF
# slot 1, the first after ours: an OLD WOMAN (30) with the first loot local id
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x29 30
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x2C 0xC0
shot gift
tap A
wait 60
shot given
expectmsg 10 u16 1 0xC001
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 0
# Somebody else talks to another one first: their bare PICKUP takes him off our map too
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 00 12 00 01 02 C0 11 00 10 00 01 C8 00 01 00 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 11 gDecompressionBuffer 19
wait 30
expect u16 gBrLoot+0 0xC002
expectne u8 gBrLoot+12 0xFF
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 05 02 C0 00 00 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 10 gDecompressionBuffer 8
wait 10
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 0
expect u8 gBrLoot+12 0xFF
say gift giver ok
