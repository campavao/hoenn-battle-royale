# evo-learn-full: learning a move on evolution asks nothing (POK-441, Cam's 2026-10-19
# play-test: "applying an item or a move shouldn't need confirming"). The evolution
# scene's own learn, like the bag's, goes straight to the summary screen's "forget
# which": no "trying to learn... delete a move?" and its YES/NO, no "1, 2, and... Poof!".
#
# A TREECKO at Lv15 knowing four (POUND, LEER, ABSORB, QUICK ATTACK), written in the clear
# (personality and OT id 0, so the substructs are unencrypted, in GAEM order), and a RARE
# CANDY (68). Lv16 is TREECKO's PURSUIT, taught from the party screen (B: not learnt),
# then GROVYLE, whose own Lv16 move is FURY CUTTER (210): A there takes ABSORB's slot.
wait 30

wait 400

wait 60
tap START
wait 40
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap A
wait 120
# BAG > RARE CANDY > USE > TREECKO
tap A
wait 30
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 200
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 16
# "elevated to Lv. 16", its stats, then PURSUIT: the summary screen's "forget which" at
# once (party_menu.c, tm-teach-full); B there is "did not learn"
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 200
shot pursuit

wait 200
tap A

# the evolution, to "Congratulations! Your TREECKO evolved into GROVYLE!"

wait 200

# then FURY CUTTER: straight to the summary screen, the new move fifth, no YES/NO
wait 150

# A on POUND: FURY CUTTER in its slot and "GROVYLE learned FURY CUTTER!", no Poof
