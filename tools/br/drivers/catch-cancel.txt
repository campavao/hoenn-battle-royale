# catch-cancel: cancelling the release leaves the ball where it was (POK-294).
#
# Kanto's rule is that nothing ever leaves the match, and this is the other side of it
# from catch-release.txt: picking a ball up with a full party asks who gives way, and
# saying "nobody" used to destroy BOTH -- the mon in the ball and the ball itself, taken
# off the ground before the question was even asked.
#
# The party is filled by copying the test Treecko into all six slots (100 bytes a mon),
# so the pickup really does go down the full-party path rather than a poked flag.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
copy gPlayerParty+100 gPlayerParty 100
copy gPlayerParty+200 gPlayerParty 100
copy gPlayerParty+300 gPlayerParty 100
copy gPlayerParty+400 gPlayerParty 100
copy gPlayerParty+500 gPlayerParty 100
wait 20
# A ball on the cell we are standing on: key 0x0501, TREECKO (277) at level 5, kind 1
# (BR_LOOT_MON), not spawned yet (objId 0xFF). Loot coords are the outer space, and the
# boot leaves us on (12,16) there.
pokebytes gBrLoot 01 05 0C 00 10 00 15 01 00 09 05 01 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
poke u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 1
wait 60
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 0
tap A
wait 120
shot asked
# parked, and the release question is up
expect u8 gBrCatch 1
tap A
wait 90
shot picker
# ...and we say nobody. B out of the party menu.
tap B
wait 120
tap B
wait 120
shot cancelled
expect u8 gBrCatch 0
# The ball is still there: nothing was taken, and the row still holds the TREECKO.
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 0
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 1
expect u16 gBrLoot 0x0501
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 1
expect u16 gBrLoot+6 277
say catch cancel ok
