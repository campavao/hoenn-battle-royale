# ring-wide: on a wide screen the field's ring is 64x32 tiles on 512x256 BGs, and every
# cell of it holds the map (include/br/br_field.h, THE WIDE RING). The page asks for it
# by writing the columns it shows left of the LCD into gBrFieldShown before the field's
# BGs are set up, as here before the warp. tools/br/ring-wide.py (ring-tall.py --wide)
# rebuilds each cell from the layout and compares all 32 x 16 metatiles of all three
# layers.
#
# The walk is ring-tall's Rustboro City with the sideways legs long enough to wrap the
# 64-column ring: landed at (21,25), 4 steps down, a turn and 17 up to row 11, a turn and
# 16 right along it to (38,11), a turn and 16 left back to (21,11), then up and over the
# connection into Route 115, to (21,77). A step left must have its new column in its
# slot from the first frame, a step up its new top row's 32 columns; a step right leaves
# the far column's slot holding the column behind it until the step is done, and a step
# down the far row's. Past the connection the ring's columns beyond pret's were drawn from
# Rustboro's grid: at rest on Route 115 they must be Route 115's.
# checked-by: tools/br/ring-wide.py
