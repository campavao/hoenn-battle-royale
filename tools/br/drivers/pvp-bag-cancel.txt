# pvp-bag-cancel: back out of the bag in a duel and the fight goes on (POK-312).
#
# The ticket read: "open the BAG from the action menu, press B without picking anything.
# The battle never comes back" -- gBattleCommunication[0] stuck at 3 for ever, the action
# menu never redrawn, and gBattleControllerExecFlags holding 0x20000011: a link buffer
# transfer queued for the peer that nothing would ever acknowledge. It reproduced every
# time, and it was the driver.
#
# A page reads the mailbox out ring every frame. pvp-bag.txt did not, so a couple of
# thousand frames of block traffic filled the ring, BrNetlink_SendBlock could not hand
# another block over, gBrNetlink.pendingLen stuck, IsLinkTaskFinished() was false for ever
# and the link battle froze mid-turn. Nothing to do with the bag, the peer, or the
# loopback standing in for it. The harness fails a run that stalls that way now, rather
# than passing while the ROM is dead (tools/br/harness/harness.c).
#
# So this is the cancel with the ring read the way a page reads it: the action menu is
# back, nothing is left queued over the link, and the turn after it is played to damage.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
# CHALLENGE: type 5, framed len 7 = hdr(04 00 00) + challenger 00, opponent 01, nonce 0
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 05 07 04 00 00 00 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 600
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 200
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
# Undamaged going in. Not exactly 20: the test mon's IVs come off the RNG at boot, so
# anything that shifts a frame on the way in rolls a different TREECKO (shot-clock.txt
# carries the same line).
expectge u16 gBattleMons+0x28 19
# FIGHT is on the left, BAG on the right. 3 is STATE_WAIT_ACTION_CASE_CHOSEN: the choice
# was taken and the bag is opening over the battle.
tap RIGHT
wait 30
tap A
wait 150
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 3
shot bag
# One B, nothing picked.
tap B
wait 180
# 2 is STATE_WAIT_ACTION_CHOSEN: the action menu is up again and waiting on this battler.
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
shot back
# ...and nothing is stuck over the link. The top nibble of the exec flags is the
# "dispatched to a controller, not yet acknowledged" set; the whole 0x20000011 wedge lives
# up there, so anything at or below 0xFFFF is a battle whose bookkeeping is square.
expectle u32 gBattleControllerExecFlags 0xFFFF
# ...with nothing waiting on a ring slot either.
expect u8 gBrNetlink+9 0
# The turn still plays: FIGHT, the first move, and it lands. Both sides swing (a loopback
# peer mirrors us), so our own mon comes out of the turn with less than it went in with,
# and the next choice opens.
tap LEFT
wait 20
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 400
expectle u16 gBattleMons+0x28 18
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
shot next-turn
say pvp bag cancel ok
