# cache-solid: a house's cache stops a step even when A would not take it (Cam's 10-19
# play-test, #21: "Looks like I'm able to walk through this Poke Ball" -- a SAFEGUARD he
# already carried). A ball A cannot take is walked over so a dropped one never walls off a
# one-wide passage (loot-solid.txt), but a cache sits where floors.py put it, out of the
# way, so it is solid whatever it holds.
#
# house-cache.txt's room: PETALBURG CITY's HOUSE1 under seed 0x12345678, the cache TM24
# THUNDERBOLT at the map's (5,4); the boot leaves us on (3,6). We carry a TM24 already, so A would
# say "Already have" and leave it.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 08 02 03 00 06 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x12345678
wait 30
expect u16 gBrLoot 0x8D21
expect u16 gBrLoot+6 312
# a TM24 in the TMs pocket, its count the balls' (bag counts are kept encrypted)
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x690 312
copy *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x692 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x652 2
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 3
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 6
# two east along the carpet, to the cell under the cache
hold RIGHT 16
wait 20
hold RIGHT 16
wait 20
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 5
# north twice: one step, then the cache stops us
hold UP 16
wait 20
hold UP 16
wait 20
shot stopped
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 5
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 5
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 3
say cache solid ok
