# zone-items: the Zone's item balls are dealt from the match seed (POK-261, Kanto
# v0.49.0), one per area, with a one-in-eight chance one of them is a Master Ball. The
# opening's second decision is whether to spend two minutes catching or to go and look,
# and it only exists if there is something to find.
#
# Seed 0x0C00 drops this ROM in SOUTHWEST (safari-opening.txt's own cell), and the ball
# for that area is at (31,21) -- so the loot table has to hold six and one of them has to
# be on this map. We stand at (8,5), so it is out of view and holds no object slot yet
# (POK-330 #48, object-slots.txt).
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x00000C00
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 02 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gBrMatch 1
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 2
# six balls on the ground, row 3 of them on this map (26, 2), and none spawned
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 6
expect u8 gBrLoot+68 26
expect u8 gBrLoot+69 2
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 0
# and the first row is an item ball (kind 3), not a mon or a bag
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 3
# Machines are in the table (POK-279). Kanto's weighting is "a strong TM most often",
# and it is what makes the MOVES row a real choice -- without it the only machines a
# match holds are the eight HMs GiveTheRunOfHoenn deals, and a menu of CUT and FLASH is
# not a menu. Seed 0x0C00 deals three of the six: TM02 DRAGON CLAW into area 1, TM50
# OVERHEAT into area 2, TM22 SOLARBEAM into area 5.
expect u16 gBrZone+30 290
expect u16 gBrZone+32 338
expect u16 gBrZone+38 310
# and nothing outside TM01..TM50 got in by being adjacent to them in the item table
expectge u16 gBrZone+30 289
expectle u16 gBrZone+30 338
shot zone
say zone items ok
