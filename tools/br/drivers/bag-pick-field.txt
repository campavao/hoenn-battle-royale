# bag-pick-field: the field's BAG takes the page's pick too (Cam, 11-02: no old ROM screens).
# The page writes the pocket and the item and goes to BAG on the START menu unseen; the bag
# opens on that pocket, takes the row and USE by itself, whatever row USE is in its context
# menu. A match's POTION heals the whole party with no one to pick (br_items.c).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
# A SUPER POTION in ITEMS behind a POTION, key zeroed, and the lead hurt.
poke u32 *gSaveBlock2Ptr+0xAC 0
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0x0D
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x562 2
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x564 0x16
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x566 3
poke u16 gPlayerParty+86 1
# START, down to BAG (POKeMON, BAG), the pick, and A.
tap START
wait 30
tap DOWN
wait 10
poke u8 gBagPosition+5 0
poke u16 gBrBag 0x16
tap A
waitfor u32 gMain+4 CB2_BagMenuRun+1 200
wait 120
shot used
# One SUPER POTION gone, the POTION untouched, the pick spent, the lead healed.
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x566 2
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x562 2
expect u16 gBrBag 0
expectge u16 gPlayerParty+86 2
say bag pick field ok
