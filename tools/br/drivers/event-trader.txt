# event-trader: an event's placed NPC (speed-up 6b). The EVENT says where its trader
# stands and what he offers; every ROM puts him there, A on him asks, the party menu picks
# one of ours and he swaps it for his at our rung. He trades once, with whoever gets to
# him first, and then leaves every map. Cancelling the menu trades nothing, and a
# fainted one is refused.
#
# catch-release's boot: one TREECKO, standing on (12,16) facing south.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
expect u8 gBrEvents+16 0
# EVENT 0: legendary 0xB000, its trader on this map at (12,17), the cell we face, with a
# LAPRAS (131)
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 00 10 00 B0 00 09 0C 00 11 00 83 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 32 gDecompressionBuffer 13
wait 30
expect u8 gBrEvents+16 1
expect u16 gBrEvents+20 12
expect u16 gBrEvents+22 17
expect u16 gBrEvents+24 131
expect u8 gBrEvents+27 9
expectne u8 gBrEvents+52 0xFF
shot trader
# A asks; YES; then B out of the party menu: nothing traded
tap A
wait 90
shot offer
tap A
wait 30
tap A
wait 90
shot picker
tap B
wait 120
expect u8 gBrEvents+18 0
# a fainted one is refused: HP is the unencrypted u16 at 86 in struct Pokemon
poke u16 gPlayerParty+86 0
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 30
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 120
shot fainted
expect u8 gBrEvents+18 0
poke u16 gPlayerParty+86 20
# again, and pick the TREECKO this time
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 30
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 120
shot traded
expect u8 gBrEvents+18 1
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
expectmsg 7 u16 2 131
# ...and he is used up for the whole room: our PICKUP of his key (0xB00F) went out, and
# he has left the map
expectmsg 10 u16 1 0xB00F
expect u8 gBrEvents+52 0xFF
tap A
wait 30
expect u8 gBrEvents+18 1
shot again
# EVENT 1's trader on the same cell, and somebody else trades with him first: their
# PICKUP of 0xB01F takes him off our map too
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 10 10 B0 00 09 0C 00 11 00 83 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 32 gDecompressionBuffer 13
wait 30
expectne u8 gBrEvents+53 0xFF
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 03 1F B0 00 00 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 10 gDecompressionBuffer 8
wait 10
expect u8 gBrEvents+18 3
expect u8 gBrEvents+53 0xFF
# a new match's first EVENT takes him off the map
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x5678
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 10 10 B0
mailsend gBrMailbox 32 gDecompressionBuffer 5
wait 10
expect u8 gBrEvents+16 2
expect u8 gBrEvents+18 0
expect u8 gBrEvents+52 0xFF
expect u16 gBrEvents+24 0
say event trader ok
