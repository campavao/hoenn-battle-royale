# story-regi-smash: DESERT RUINS's sealed chamber opens on A at its wall, as Rock Smash
# would (br_story.c, BrStory_Tick). No HM is asked for: there are none to teach.
#
# FLAG_SYS_REGIROCK_PUZZLE_COMPLETED (0x8B0) is gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1386 bit 0. The wall is
# (7..9,19..20) and its middle bottom the warp up into the chamber; we boot under it at
# (8,21) on DESERT RUINS (24:6).
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 8
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 18 06 08 00 15 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 24
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 6
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1386 0x01 0x00
# Shut: walking at it goes nowhere.
hold UP 20
wait 30
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 21
shot sealed
tap A
wait 30
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1386 0x01 0x01
shot opened
hold UP 12
wait 150
# Through the warp, in the chamber at (8,11).
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 6
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 11
shot chamber
say story regi smash ok
