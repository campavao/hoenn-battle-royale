# grows-up: the party grows up with the rung (POK-290).
#
# Cam, live 2026-09-17: "Pokemon are not automatically evolving as time goes on." A
# Zigzagoon caught in the Zone was still a Zigzagoon at Lv75 in the last ring -- while a
# bot's team walked its evolution chain as it was dealt. At rung 75 you fought their
# SWAMPERT with your MUDKIP.
#
# It can't hang off a level-up, because nothing in a match levels up: no battle awards
# EXP and the rung writes the level straight on. So it happens in the lift.
#
# The lift teaches nothing (Cam, 2026-10-13: "the rung no longer teaches moves"): moves
# come off the ground as TMs, and MOVES offers the rest. So the SCEPTILE at the end still
# knows the POUND and LEER the TREECKO had.
#
# Ring phase 5 is the top of sLadder: rung 75, seventy levels in one step, which is two
# evolutions for a TREECKO (16 GROVYLE, 36 SCEPTILE).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
# The nickname is the one thing on a party mon that is NOT encrypted -- BoxPokemon keeps
# it in the clear at +8 -- so it is how a driver reads a species without a battle open.
# TREECKO, in the Gen 3 charmap, and level 5.
expect u8 gPlayerParty+8 0xCE
expect u8 gPlayerParty+9 0xCC
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 5
poke u8 gBrRing+1 5
poke u8 gBrRing 1
wait 120
shot grown
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 75
# SCEPTILE: S C E P T I L E. A mon nobody renamed is called after its species, so the
# name had to travel with the evolution or a SCEPTILE would answer to TREECKO.
expect u8 gPlayerParty+8 0xCD
expect u8 gPlayerParty+9 0xBD
expect u8 gPlayerParty+10 0xBF
expect u8 gPlayerParty+11 0xCA
expect u8 gPlayerParty+12 0xCE
# and the rung itself moved
expect u8 gBrLevels 75
# Into a fight, where gBattleMons is decrypted: species at +0, the four moves at +0x0C.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 03 BC C9 CE 01 15 01 05 13 00 13 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 CE CC BF C5 C9
mailsend gBrMailbox 23 gDecompressionBuffer 106
wait 10
# Seat 31's ghost on our map (out of our eyeline), then its CHALLENGE: a challenge
# comes from a trainer standing on our map (POK-330 #24).
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 01 00 09 14 00 08 00 01 01 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 400
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 200
shot fight
expect u16 gBattleMons 279
# POUND (1) and LEER (43), and nothing else: the rung taught it nothing on the way up.
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x0C 1
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x0E 43
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x10 0
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x12 0
say grows up ok
