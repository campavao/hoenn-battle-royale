# pvp-bag-clock: THE BAG IS NOT A HIDING PLACE (POK-292).
#
# Kanto's README: "The clock does not stop for an open bag: leave it open past the same
# thirty seconds and the bag closes itself." Ours stopped dead. BrBattle_ShotTick is
# called from the action menu and the move menu and from nowhere else, so a duellist who
# opened the BAG -- or the party screen the POKeMON row opens -- froze the clock for as
# long as they liked and held the other player there. A better stall than the one the
# shot clock was written to close, and it arrived with POK-207 opening the bag in a duel.
#
# pvp-bag.txt is the door itself: that the bag opens at all. This is the clock over it.
# shot-clock.txt is the clock in the open, where it forfeits.
#
# What this pins is the watchdog: the shot clock's own counter FROZEN while the bag is
# up, BrBattle_TickStall's counter running in its place, and the bag off the top of the
# stack afterwards without a button being touched. It stops there rather than following
# the forfeit through, because a loopback peer cannot service a link transfer that starts
# while this ROM is inside the bag menu and the fight wedges -- see POK-312, which is
# about that wedge and not about this clock.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 05 07 04 00 00 00 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 600
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 200
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
# The action menu has been counting: shotFrames is up but nowhere near the thirty
# seconds, and nothing has timed out.
expectge u16 gBrBattle 1
expectle u16 gBrBattle 1799
expect u16 gBrBattle+2 0
# FIGHT is on the left, BAG on the right.
tap RIGHT
wait 30
tap A
wait 150
# 3 is STATE_WAIT_ACTION_CASE_CHOSEN: the bag is open over the battle. Nothing is
# pressed from here -- every press after this line is the watchdog's own.
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 3
shot bag
# ...and the shot clock has stopped, which is the bug it is named after. Twenty-eight
# more seconds pass and its counter is still where the action menu left it -- a couple of
# hundred frames, nowhere near the 1800 it needs. BrBattle_ShotTick is not called from
# inside a bag and never was.
wait 1700
expectle u16 gBrBattle 400
# The clock that IS running is the watchdog's, and it is nearly out.
expectge u16 gBrBattle+10 1700
shot still-in-the-bag
wait 400
# Out of time. B was pressed for us: the bag is off the top of the stack (the watchdog
# only counts while a screen the battle put up is up, so a zero here IS the bag closing),
# and the flag that says this turn's clock has already gone is set, so the menu it backs
# out to does not get a fresh thirty seconds.
expect u8 gBrBattle+9 1
expect u16 gBrBattle+10 0
shot backed-out
say pvp bag clock ok
