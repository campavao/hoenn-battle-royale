# spike-teach: spike B's page hands a held item (or a TM) off the ground the way a
# match's pickup does (br_wild.h's BR_WILD_ASKED_TEACH): the ask arms the teach and
# answers DONE, and once the item reaches the bag the bag's GIVE screen opens on the
# field ("Give to which POKeMON?"). A gives it to TREECKO: the bag is empty again.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 D3 C9 CF FF
wait 400
wait 2
# ITEM_LEFTOVERS (200)
poke u16 gBrWildAsk+4 200
poke u8 gBrWildAsk 5
wait 2
expect u8 gBrWildAsk 3
# the GIVE, as the page's: into the items pocket (key 0, so the count is plain)
poke u32 *gSaveBlock2Ptr+0xAC 0
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 200
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x562 1
wait 120
shot give
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
shot given
tap A
wait 120
tap A
wait 120
shot back
# TREECKO holds it: the items pocket's slot is empty again
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0
say spike teach ok
