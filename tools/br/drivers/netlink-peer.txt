# netlink-peer: a link battle takes blocks from its own peer and nobody else
# (POK-330 #20).
#
# A BT carries its sender's seat (the bridge stamps it), and HandleBt never looked at
# it. A page that lost track of its opponent -- a rejoin mid-fight builds a new Bridge
# with none -- sends its blocks to the whole room, and every other link battle in that
# room delivered them as its own peer's and desynced.
#
# Seat 2 (placed out of our eyeline) challenges us and says nothing yet, so the start
# exchange sits waiting for its header: gBattleCommunication[MULTIUSE_STATE] is 2.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 01 00 09 14 00 08 00 01 01 02
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 300
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink+2 2
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
shot waiting
# A header from seat 5, which is somebody else's fight: seat, seq u16, len u16, data.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 05 01 00 04 00 00 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 6 gDecompressionBuffer 9
wait 30
expect u16 gBrNetlink+14 0
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
# The same block from seat 2 is the one it was waiting for: the exchange moves on to
# the parties.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 01 00 04 00 00 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 6 gDecompressionBuffer 9
wait 30
expect u16 gBrNetlink+14 1
expectge u8 gBattleCommunication 3
# Seat 2 goes out, and the fight is ours (out-peer.txt): back to the field.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02
mailsend gBrMailbox 9 gDecompressionBuffer 1
wait 200
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
expect u8 gBrNetlink+11 1
shot back
say netlink peer ok
