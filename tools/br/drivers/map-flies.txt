# map-flies: MAP is a way out, not just a look (POK-278). ring-map.txt opens the same
# screen and closes it; the play-test opened it with a bird in the party, pressed A on a
# town, and nothing happened -- "I used MAP from the main menu with a pokemon that could
# fly, but clicking on the location did not fly me there".
#
# Boot flags 0x40 is BR_BOOT_FLAG_TESTFLY: a SWELLOW that knows FLY, drivers only.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 C1 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 2
# A dropped match with a ring, the same as ring-map's.
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrRing 1
poke u8 gBrRing+1 1
poke u8 gBrRing+2 4
poke u8 gBrRing+3 5
poke u8 gBrRing+4 4
# We are in Littleroot (map 0:9).
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 9
tap START
wait 40
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap A
wait 240
shot map
expect u8 gBrMap 1
# The cursor opens on where we are standing (LITTLEROOT TOWN). Two steps north is
# OLDALE TOWN -- somewhere else, so landing there is the assertion.
tap UP
wait 30
tap UP
wait 30
shot cursor
# A on a town you can fly to is a flight now, not a way of closing the map.
tap A
wait 400
shot flying
wait 400
shot landed
expect u8 gBrMap 0
expect u8 gBrPick 0
# Oldale Town is map 0:10: somewhere we were not.
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 10
# ...and we are somewhere, on a map, with the controls back: START opens.
tap START
wait 60
shot menu
say map flies ok
