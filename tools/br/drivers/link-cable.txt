# link-cable: the LINK CABLE evolves a trade evolution from the BAG, the way a stone
# evolves its own (Cam, 2026-10-13: "a Link Cable item so that folks could evolve pokemon
# that can today only evolve via trade"). br_items.h, BrItems_CableTarget.
#
# Boots with no party (mode 1). Our own KADABRA (species 64) is put on our tile as a
# ball keyed to our seat, so taking it back does not finish a trade on the way in
# (loot-trade-evo is that half); then a LINK CABLE (52) by GIVE, and BAG > LINK CABLE >
# USE > KADABRA. LITTLEROOT (map 0:9), the boot at (5,8): our tile is (12,16).
wait 30
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 0
# key 1 (seat 0, ours), (12,16), KADABRA, map 0:9, level 30, kind 1 (BR_LOOT_MON)
pokebytes gBrLoot 01 00 0C 00 10 00 40 00 00 09 1E 01 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
# still KADABRA: "KADABRA" in the nickname, which is not encrypted
expect u8 gPlayerParty+8 0xC5
expect u8 gPlayerParty+9 0xBB
expect u8 gPlayerParty+10 0xBE
# one LINK CABLE, into the ITEMS pocket (SaveBlock1 +0x560, the id in the clear)
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 34 00 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 30 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 60
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 52
# START, down to BAG, in; the LINK CABLE is first, USE is first, the KADABRA is first.
tap START
wait 40
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap A
wait 120
shot bag
tap A
wait 30
shot submenu
tap A
wait 60
shot party-able
tap A
wait 120
shot evolving
# the evolution scene, then "Congratulations!" and "evolved into ALAKAZAM!"
wait 900
shot evolved
hold A 4
wait 120
hold A 4
wait 120
hold A 4
wait 120
shot back
# ALAKAZAM: A L A K A Z A M
expect u8 gPlayerParty+8 0xBB
expect u8 gPlayerParty+9 0xC6
expect u8 gPlayerParty+10 0xBB
expect u8 gPlayerParty+11 0xC5
expect u8 gPlayerParty+12 0xBB
expect u8 gPlayerParty+13 0xD4
expect u8 gPlayerParty+14 0xBB
expect u8 gPlayerParty+15 0xC7
# and the cable is used up
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0
say link cable ok
