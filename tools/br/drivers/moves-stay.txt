# moves-stay: MOVES hands the list back after a teach (POK-301).
#
# Cam, live: "when you apply a move to a Pokemon it kicks you out of the MOVES screen. It
# should stay in that screen until they back out themselves, and also get rid of the
# '1, 2, 3' and 'learned' text." So a teach is a keypress now, not a trip.
#
# Proving it needs a second teach with no second trip: two TMs go on the ground under the
# player, both are taken, and ONE MOVES session teaches both. If the screen still exited
# after the first, the presses for the second would land on the party menu and the second
# TM would still be in the pocket. The cursor is not moved between them either -- the list
# rebuilds one row shorter, so the machine below slides up under the cursor.
#
# See moves-spend.txt for the boot, the loot-coordinate and TM-pocket conventions.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 5
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B0 0
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B4 0
# TM06 TOXIC (294) then TM10 HIDDEN POWER (298), both on the tile we are standing on
pokebytes gBrLoot 01 00 0C 00 10 00 26 01 00 09 00 03 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
pokebytes gBrLoot+20 02 00 0C 00 10 00 2A 01 00 09 00 03 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
wait 60
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 90
shot took-both
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 2
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B0 294
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B4 298
# MOVES on the party screen
tap START
wait 40
tap A
wait 80
tap A
wait 40
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap A
wait 240
shot list
# CUT, STRENGTH, FLASH, ROCK SMASH, TOXIC, HIDDEN POWER, CANCEL -- four down to TOXIC,
# the first machine in the list that is a TM rather than an HM
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap DOWN
wait 20
shot on-toxic
tap A
wait 150
tap A
wait 150
shot back-on-the-list
# TOXIC is out of its slot (RemoveBagItem leaves the hole rather than compacting) and the
# list is a row shorter, so HIDDEN POWER has slid up under the cursor. No ceremony ran and
# no screen was left: teach it from here, same two presses, cursor untouched.
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B0 0
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B4 298
tap A
wait 150
tap A
wait 150
shot taught-both
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B0 0
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B4 0
# and every HM survived both teaches
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x690 339
say moves stay ok
