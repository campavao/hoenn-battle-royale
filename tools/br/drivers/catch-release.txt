# catch-release: a parked catch with a full party brings up the party picker in the
# overworld; choosing a slot puts the catch there -- and the one that gave up its slot
# lands on the ground as a ball anybody can take (POK-294). Kanto's rule, from its
# README: "The released Pokemon lands as a ball at your feet, claimable by anyone --
# trading up leaves a trace." Nothing ever leaves a match.
#
# Mechanism only: the park comes from a copy of the test Treecko, the "full party" check
# is bypassed by setting pending.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
# park a copy of party[0] and mark it pending
copy gBrPendingCatch gPlayerParty 100
poke u8 gBrCatch 1
wait 90
shot prompt
expect u8 gBrCatch+1 1
# through the message, into the party menu, pick slot 0
tap A
wait 60
shot picker
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 90
expect u8 gBrCatch 0
expect u8 gBrCatch+1 0
shot done
# ...and the old one is on the ground rather than gone. One piece of loot on this map,
# one release counted.
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 1
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA4 1
# Its key is in the released space -- BR_LOOT_KEY_FREED (0x4000) | seat 0 | first drop --
# so it can never collide with an elimination's (seat << 8) or a trainer's (0x8000).
expect u16 gBrLoot 0x4000
# ...and it is BESIDE us rather than under us: the boot leaves us on (12,16) in the outer
# coords loot uses, and the first spill offset out from there is (13,16).
expect u16 gBrLoot+2 13
expect u16 gBrLoot+4 16
# nothing was picked back up by the presses that chose the slot
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 0
# kind 1 is BR_LOOT_MON, and it is the TREECKO that gave up the slot (species 277).
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 1
expect u16 gBrLoot+6 277
say catch release ok
