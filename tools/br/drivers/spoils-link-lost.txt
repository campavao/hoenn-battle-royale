# spoils-link-lost: the other side of spoils-link.txt. Beaten in a link fight, our team is
# the winner's to put down round their feet, so the spill we send going out is our bag
# alone. Before, both ROMs would have dropped the team -- ours where we stood and the
# winner's round them -- and whichever ball was taken first came back from the other.
# The same loopback session, with OUR TREECKO left 1 HP.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 600
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 200
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
# battler 0's HP (gBattleMons row 0, +0x28)
poke u16 gBattleMons+0x28 1
# FIGHT, POUND
tap A
wait 30
tap A
# a link battle's text runs itself
wait 900
wait 900
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
expect u8 gBrNetlink+11 2
# out, and the spill that went with it: no team, the bag
expect u8 gBrMatch 3
expectmsg 11 u8 0 0
expectmsg 11 u8 3 0
expectmsg 11 u8 4 1
expectmsg 11 u16 5 0x00FF
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 1
shot lost
say spoils link lost ok
