# ghost-seat31: the last seat's ghost is not the player (POK-315).
#
# Ghost local ids were BR_GHOST_LOCAL_ID_BASE (0xE0) + seat, and 0xE0 + 31 is 255:
# LOCALID_PLAYER, the id the engine uses to mean YOU. Seat 31 is dealt in an ordinary
# match (Cam's roster read CAM, P25..P31), and its ghost was, to a good part of the
# engine, the player -- and solid, since BrGhosts_Insubstantial stops short of 255 on
# purpose. The base is 0xC8 now: 0xC8..0xE7, clear of the loot balls and the player.
#
# ghost-walkthrough.txt's shape, with seat 31 in the tile north of us instead of seat 1:
# walking through it is what a ghost lets you do and the player's own id never would.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 10 07 00 0F 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 16
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+2 0x0E
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+4 0x16
# PLACE seat 31 (0x1F) at (0x0E,0x15), facing down
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 1F 01 00 10 0E 00 15 00 01 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 60
# seat 31's row is present and its ghost is on the map (objId at +9 is not BR_NO_OBJ)
expect u8 gBrSeats+0x1F0 1
expectne u8 gBrSeats+0x1F9 0xFF
shot facing-them
hold UP 26
wait 20
shot walked-through
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+2 0x0E
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+4 0x13
say ghost seat31 ok
