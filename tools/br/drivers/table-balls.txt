# table-balls: a painted room holds Cam's ball and nothing on its tables. The tables of a notable
# building carried up to two more (Cam's 2026-10-19 play-test, #8, sTableBalls); Cam painted every
# room's ball by hand (11-01, room-paint.json) and, 11-22: "Yeah just use item spots that I had
# painted by hand". Every notable building is painted, so sTableBalls is empty.

# DEVON CORP. 2F (map 11:1) is sRooms[143]: its cache, key 0x8D8F, painted at (5,4) -- (12,11)
# MAP_OFFSET in -- and nothing else: the desks at (7,4) and (10,7) that held 0xAC12 and 0xAC13
# are bare. The boot puts us at (7,3), above the first desk.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 0B 01 07 00 03 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x1072
wait 30
expect u16 gBrLoot 0x8D8F
expect u16 gBrLoot+2 12
expect u16 gBrLoot+4 11
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 3
# no row a table ball's key (0xACxx)
expectne u8 gBrLoot+21 0xAC
expectne u8 gBrLoot+41 0xAC
expectne u8 gBrLoot+61 0xAC
expectne u8 gBrLoot+81 0xAC
expectne u8 gBrLoot+101 0xAC
expectne u8 gBrLoot+121 0xAC
expectne u8 gBrLoot+141 0xAC

# A at the desk below: nothing there to take
hold DOWN 1
wait 20

wait 120
# no row a table ball's key (0xACxx)
expectne u8 gBrLoot+21 0xAC
expectne u8 gBrLoot+41 0xAC
expectne u8 gBrLoot+61 0xAC
expectne u8 gBrLoot+81 0xAC
expectne u8 gBrLoot+101 0xAC
expectne u8 gBrLoot+121 0xAC

# no row a table ball's key (0xACxx)
expectne u8 gBrLoot+21 0xAC
expectne u8 gBrLoot+41 0xAC
