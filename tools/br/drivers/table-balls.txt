# table-balls: a notable building's tables, desks and counters carry up to three balls
# (Cam's 2026-10-19 play-test, #8): the floor's cache and up to two more (br_balls.c,
# sTableBalls, written by tools/br/floors.py). Each sits on a table top and is taken by
# pressing A at it from the floor beside it.
#
# DEVON CORP. 2F (map 11:1) is sRooms[143]: its cache, key 0x8D8F, on the desk at (11,4),
# and sTableBalls[18] and [19], keys 0xAC12 and 0xAC13, on the desks at (7,4) and (10,7)
# -- MAP_OFFSET in, (18,11), (14,11) and (17,14). The boot puts us at (7,3), above the
# first desk, facing down at it.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 0B 01 07 00 03 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x1072
wait 30
expect u16 gBrLoot 0x8D8F
expect u16 gBrLoot+2 18
expect u16 gBrLoot+4 11
expect u16 gBrLoot+20 0xAC12
expect u16 gBrLoot+22 14
expect u16 gBrLoot+24 11
expect u8 gBrLoot+31 3
expect u16 gBrLoot+40 0xAC13
expect u16 gBrLoot+42 17
expect u16 gBrLoot+44 14
expect u8 gBrLoot+51 3
shot desks
# A at the desk takes its ball: under seed 0x1072 it is TM16 LIGHT SCREEN (304), so the
# teach screen, B to keep it, and the PICKUP of its key
expect u16 gBrLoot+26 304
hold DOWN 1
wait 20
tap A
wait 120
shot teach
tap B
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
expectmsg 10 u16 1 0xAC12
expect u8 gBrLoot+31 0
expect u8 gBrLoot+51 3
shot taken
# Somebody else takes the third -- seat 1, PICKUP of key 0xAC13 -- and it goes from here
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0A 0B 08 00 00 01 13 AC 00 00 00 00 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 20
expect u8 gBrLoot+51 0
# ...and up the stairs and back down them: the cache comes back, the two taken ones do not
hold LEFT 80
wait 20
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 2
hold UP 60
wait 200
expectne u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 1
shot upstairs
hold UP 40
wait 200
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 1
expect u16 gBrLoot 0x8D8F
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 3
expect u8 gBrLoot+31 0
expect u8 gBrLoot+51 0
shot back
say table balls ok
