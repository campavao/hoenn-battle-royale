# spectate-follow-door: a watcher goes through a door with the trainer it follows, not
# after them (Cam's 2026-10-27 play-test, HBR-69: "going from area to area ... it lags
# behind somewhat. Should be instantaneous"). Their PLACE on the far side comes only after
# their own door, fade and load -- about 70 frames after the STEP onto the door tile --
# and the cut used to start there. Now the STEP onto a tile that warps whoever steps on it
# takes us through the same warp at once, and their PLACE finds us where they come out.
#
# LITTLEROOT (0:9). Seat 1 below the house door at (12,15), object coords (0C,10); the
# door is (0C,0F) and goes to the house's 1F (1:0), coming out at (0F,0F) as the boot's own
# walk through it does.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 0C 00 10 00 02 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 10
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 14 04 01 00 00 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 90
expect u8 gBrSpectate+8 1
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 9
# STEP up onto the door tile: we are through it before their PLACE says so
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 03 0B 08 00 00 01 01 0C 00 0F 00 00 09
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 40
shot ahead
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 1
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 0
# ...and their PLACE inside, a second later, is ridden where it lands, with no cut back
# to the town in between
wait 30
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 0
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x10D8 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 01 00 0F 00 0F 00 02 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 4
wait 60
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 1
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 0
expectge u8 gBrSpectate+9 1
shot inside
say spectate follow door ok
