# spectate-retry: a TURN the out ring has no room for goes out when there is room, not
# never (POK-330 #12). The publisher took the turn's bytes off the record and then
# ignored BrWire_Send's FALSE, so a spectator's replay lost them for good: it read the
# next turn's bytes as this one's, or waited on them until the page reloaded. duel.txt's
# proxy fight is the publisher here -- no netlink, so a full ring holds up nothing but
# the stream.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 02 01 01 15 01 05 13 00 13 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 CE CC BF C5 C9 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 1B 01 05 14 00 14 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 C7 CF BE C5 C3 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 01 4B 00 01 0D 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 27 gDecompressionBuffer 210
wait 300
expect u8 gBrDuel+1 1
poke u32 gRngValue 3
# the bstart is out; no turn has been
expect u8 gBrSpectate+4 1
expect u16 gBrSpectate 0
# The page stops taking slots, and the ring is full: 64 slots between tail and head.
drain 0x0
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0A 0
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x08 64
# Through the intro to the first choices, which both AIs make at once.
tap A
wait 20
tap A
wait 20
tap A
wait 20
tap A
wait 20
tap A
wait 20
tap A
wait 20
tap A
wait 20
tap A
wait 20
tap A
wait 20
tap A
wait 20
# The turn was tried and refused (the ring counts the pushes it turned away), and
# nothing is counted as having got out.
expect u16 gBrMailbox+0x08 64
expectge u32 gBrMailbox+0x14 1
expect u16 gBrSpectate 0
# The page takes slots again, and the held turn goes out: a TURN for battle 1 | 2 << 8
# is in the ring, where the old ROM had none to show for the fight so far.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 3
expectge u16 gBrSpectate 1
expectmsg 19 u16 0 0x0201
say spectate retry ok
