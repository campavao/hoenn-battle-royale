# mart-stones: the evolution stones are sold in Lilycove, and the MOON STONE costs
# money (POK-309).
#
# Cam: "it looks like all of the stones are available in Pokemarts. I think this is
# incorrect... there is a town that has essentially a big department store. That
# location should be what sells the stones, not just all Pokemarts. And the Moon Stone
# for sure should not be sold for zero dollars."
#
# mart-no-stones.txt is the other half: an ordinary mart at the top rung, with none.
#
# DEPARTMENT STORE 2F is map 13:17; the left clerk stands at (7,6), so boot on (7,7)
# and face her. Indoors the boot lands on the cell it names -- the one-tile step is the
# door-exit animation and there is no door here.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 0D 11 07 00 07 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 700
shot arrived
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 13
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 17
# the item pocket is empty before we buy anything (SaveBlock1 +0x560, four bytes a slot,
# item id in the clear)
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0
hold UP 10
wait 20
tap A
wait 120
tap A
wait 150
shot shelf
tap A
wait 90
shot list-top
hold DOWN 150
wait 40
shot list-bottom
# The shelf's tail is the POKe DOLL (POK-293) and then FIRE, WATER, THUNDER, LEAF, SUN,
# MOON STONE, all at 2100 -- the frame above is where the price is read. Down to the
# MOON STONE and buy one.
tap DOWN
wait 15
tap DOWN
wait 15
tap DOWN
wait 20
shot on-moon-stone
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 120
tap A
wait 150
shot bought
tap A
wait 60
tap B
wait 60
# It is in the bag: SaveBlock1 +0x560 is the first ITEMS slot, four bytes, and the item
# id is in the clear (only the quantity is scrambled). 94 is ITEM_MOON_STONE.
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 94
say mart stones ok
