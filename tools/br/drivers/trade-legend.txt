# trade-legend: a legendary trades like any mon (Cam, 2026-10-23: "just treat it like any
# pokemon since it would drop if they died too"). He keeps the LATIAS and offers it to the
# next trainer, and he still stands as himself, not as the LATIAS (br_loot.c Spawn: home
# keys first).
#
# house-trade's room and seed: PETALBURG CITY's HOUSE1 under 0x1072, a KADABRA trader.
# A LATIAS on our own cell (row 0, over the room's cache) is taken into the party, then
# copied over the TREECKO in slot 0, the one the party menu offers first.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 08 02 03 00 06 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x1072
wait 30
hold DOWN 90
wait 200
hold UP 40
wait 200
expect u16 gBrLoot+20 0x8C21
expect u16 gBrLoot+26 64
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 3
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 8
# seat 3's LATIAS (407, level 30) at our feet: (3,8) + MAP_OFFSET, map 8:2, kind 1
pokebytes gBrLoot 00 03 0A 00 0F 00 97 01 08 02 1E 01 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
wait 30
tap A
wait 120
tap A
wait 60
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 2
copy gPlayerParty gPlayerParty+100 100
# to him, as house-trade walks
hold UP 16
wait 20
hold LEFT 16
wait 20
hold LEFT 16
wait 20
hold UP 34
wait 20
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 1
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 5
hold UP 1
wait 20
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 60
# the ask, and no host behind the harness: after BR_CLAIM_FRAMES the trade goes ahead
wait 660
shot traded
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 2
expect u16 gBrLoot+20 0x8C21
expect u16 gBrLoot+26 407
expect u8 gBrLoot+31 1
expectmsg 10 u16 1 0x8C21
expectmsg 10 u16 4 407
say trade legend ok
