# Cam: "I tried taking the ferry to lilycove but it softlocked me since it put me on the
# boat but then there was no way off". The way off is the sailor by the gangway at (1,11)
# on SS TIDAL's corridor (25:41), who warps you ashore once the cabin bed has made land.
# He is a SAILOR with no flag and no ferry script, so the match's strip (br_strip.c) took
