# give-after-out: a bag's GIVE that lands after we went out goes back down as the bag, in
# nobody's pockets (HBR-88 review). Our own drop is read out of the pockets a bag at a
# time when the ring is full (SendBag), so a GIVE poured in between would send a row twice
# or not at all.
#
# bag-take-all.txt's ground: seat 3's bag on the cell we face, taken, then out before its
# GIVE (ORAN BERRY x2) arrives.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0B 14 11 00 00 03 00 09 00 01 FF 03 0C 00 11 00 00 64 00 00 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 1
tap A
wait 20
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 1
expectmsg 10 u16 1 0x03FF
poke u8 gBrMatch 3
# GIVE [count 1] (ORAN BERRY x2)
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 8B 00 02
mailsend gBrMailbox 30 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 40
# not in the pocket, and back on the ground as seat 3's bag with its berries
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x790 0
expectmsg 11 u16 5 0x03FF
expectmsg 11 u8 11 1
expectmsg 11 u16 12 139
expectmsg 11 u8 14 2
say give after out ok
