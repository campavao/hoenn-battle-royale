# ghost-surf-cull: a surfing ghost the engine culls takes its SURF blob with it (Cam's
# 2026-10-27 play-test: "random floating surf things", one "duped when he surfaced").
# Walking away, the camera's move culls the ghost's object (RemoveObjectEventsOutsideView)
# before BrGhosts_Tick hears it is out of view, so Despawn found no object and left its
# blob on the sea, riding a freed object.
#
# ROUTE 107 (map 0:22), surfing from (20,10) as ghost-surf.txt, seat 2 (BRENDAN) on the sea
# nine tiles east: in the object box, off the screen.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 16 14 00 0A 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrMatch+1 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 03 19 0C 3C 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
# PLACE seat 2 on 0:22 at (29,10), facing west, alive, skin 0
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 02 0E 0B 00 00 02 01 00 16 1D 00 0A 00 03 01 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 90
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x74 0xCA
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x71 2
# seat 2's blob is sprite 59, riding object 3 (data[2] at +0x32). The slot follows from every
# sprite allocated before it: a new mark, weather or field effect in this setup moves it.
expectbits u8 gSprites+0xFEA 0x01 0x01
expect u8 gSprites+0xFDE 3
# surf west to the shore: the box leaves seat 2 behind
hold LEFT 140
wait 30
shot away
# the camera's cull took object 3, and the seat let go of it...
expectbits u8 gObjectEvents+0x6C 0x01 0x00
expect u8 gBrSeats+41 0xFF
# ...and its blob went with it, rather than staying on the sea
expectbits u8 gSprites+0xFEA 0x01 0x00
say ghost surf cull ok
