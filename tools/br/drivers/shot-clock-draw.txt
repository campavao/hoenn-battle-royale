# shot-clock-draw: the drawn shot clock is top-right in both menus (POK-231). A loopback
# engage battle; screenshots of the action menu (bg0 scrolled 20) and the move menu
# (scrolled 40) both show the seconds top-right. No pixel assert -- the harness reads
# memory, not the screen -- so this is a capture driver for a human/agent eyeball.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# the eyeline only fires in a started match (gBrMatch.phase = BR_PHASE_PLAY)
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 0F 00 10 00 03 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 860
shot action
tap A
wait 40
shot move
say shot clock draw ok
