# release-retake: your own released ball picked back up is still yours (POK-330 #28).
#
# Kanto's BR-24: a ball that changed hands finishes the trade -- KADABRA, MACHOKE,
# GRAVELER and HAUNTER evolve on the way into somebody ELSE's party -- and your own ball
# picked back up does not. A released mon's key is BR_LOOT_KEY_FREED | seat << 8 | n, and
# WasOurs compared the whole high byte (0x40 | seat) with our seat, so it never matched:
# release a KADABRA to make room, walk back, take it, and it came back an ALAKAZAM. A free
# trade evolution on demand.
#
# The KADABRA comes from our own spill key first (seat 0's, so no evolution), then the
# catch ticket's release flow lets it go, then we take it back.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
# SPILL: seat 0 (us), map 0:9, one KADABRA (64) at level 30 on our own cell (12,16),
# keyed 0x0003 -- seat 0's own space, so it is ours and does not evolve
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0B 11 0E 00 00 00 00 09 01 03 00 0C 00 10 00 40 00 1E 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 20
tap A
wait 30
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 2
# "TOOK KADABRA!": the box line, 'K' (0xC5) after "TOOK "
expect u8 gBrHud+0x153 0xC5
wait 120
# A catch with nowhere to go: a copy of the TREECKO, pending, and the party picker
copy gBrPendingCatch gPlayerParty 100
poke u8 gBrCatch 1
wait 90
tap A
wait 60
shot picker
# down to slot 1, the KADABRA, and let it go
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 90
expect u8 gBrCatch 0
shot released
# it is on the ground beside us, keyed in the released space: FREED | seat 0 | first
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 1
expect u16 gBrLoot 0x4000
expect u16 gBrLoot+6 64
expect u16 gBrLoot+2 13
expect u16 gBrLoot+4 16
wait 120
# face it and take it back
tap RIGHT
wait 20
tap A
wait 30
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 0
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 3
# "TOOK KADABRA!" again -- it was "TOOK ALAKAZAM!", 'A' (0xBB)
expect u8 gBrHud+0x153 0xC5
expect u8 gBrHud+0x154 0xBB
expect u8 gBrHud+0x155 0xBE
shot retaken
say release retake ok
