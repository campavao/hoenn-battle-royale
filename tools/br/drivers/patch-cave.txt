# patch-cave: a patch on a cave floor is a shadow too (review of #19: the darkened puff
# of dust was the floor's own colour, and gone between puffs).
#
# GRANITE CAVE 1F (map 24:7, wild header 0x10) under match seed 4: from (10,10) patch 2,
# at (9,10) -- (0x10,0x11) with the +7 -- is in view and drawn. (Patch 5 was, until the
# floor's trader took a cell from the deal, POK-413.)
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 18 07 0A 00 0A 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 4
wait 120
expect u16 gBrPatch 0x1807
expect u16 gBrPatch+2 0x10
expect u8 gBrPatch+4 8
expect u16 gBrPatch+20 0x10
expect u16 gBrPatch+36 0x11
expectne u8 gBrPatch+7 64
shot cave
say patch cave ok
