# lab-closed: Professor Birch's lab is shut for the match. Boot onto the tile below the
# lab door (Littleroot 7,16), lean north into it, and be refused with a line on the
# bottom box instead of warped into a room with Birch, the rival and the aide in it.
#
# lab-control.txt is the other half: the same walk into Brendan's house, which is not on
# the closed list, still opens. Without it this driver passes on a player who never moved.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 07 00 11 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 9
# the step onto the door tile is eight frames at a run; the refusal is the next press.
hold UP 30
wait 4
shot refused
# the box was drawn, so the door said something rather than the walk quietly failing.
# BrHud_Box gives it 90 frames, hence the check here and not after the long hold below.
expectge u8 gBrHud+0x178 1
# and leaning on it for another two seconds still does not get in
hold UP 120
wait 20
shot still-out
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 9
say lab closed ok
