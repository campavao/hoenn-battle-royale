# spectate-follow-seam: a watched trainer walks over a map seam, and so does the camera
# (2026-10-06 play-test: "a bot walked out of Rustboro north and jumped somewhere into the
# next route"). Their first step onto the next map comes as a PLACE in that map's numbers,
# which despawned the ghost and warped the spectator after it, fade and all. Now the
# ghost walks that step (br_ghosts.c's CrossedSeam), the camera rides it over, and the
# camera's own crossing loads the route the way walking does.
#
# Littleroot (0:9) and Route 101 (0:16) are both 20x20, Route 101 above at offset 0, so
# Littleroot's (10,0) has Route 101's (10,19) one step north of it.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 9
# seat 1 on Littleroot at (10,2) -- 17, 9 with MAP_OFFSET -- facing north
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 11 00 09 00 02 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 10
# FOLLOW seat 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 14 04 01 00 00 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 30
expectge u8 gBrSpectate+9 1
# the view is centred on them: pos (10,2)
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0 10
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 2
shot on-them
# two steps north, to Littleroot's top row
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 03 0B 08 00 00 01 02 11 00 08 00 00 09
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 20
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x10D8 03 0B 08 00 00 01 02 11 00 07 00 00 09
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 4
wait 30
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 0
# ...and over the seam: PLACE on Route 101 (0:16) at (10,19), 17, 26 with MAP_OFFSET
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1118 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 10 11 00 1A 00 02 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 5
wait 4
shot mid-seam
# no warp asked for
expect u8 gBrSpectate+14 0
wait 30
# on Route 101 by the camera's crossing, centred on them, still riding the ghost
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 16
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0 10
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 19
expect u8 gBrSpectate+14 0
expectge u8 gBrSpectate+9 1
shot over
# and on up the route, an ordinary step
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1158 03 0B 08 00 00 01 02 11 00 19 00 00 10
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 6
wait 30
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 18
expectge u8 gBrSpectate+9 1
shot route
say spectate follow seam ok
