# spectate-follow-warp: following a seat on another map warps there, once (POK-247).
# FollowTick runs before the warp's task every frame; it used to call DoWarp again each
# frame, which began a new fade the frame the last one ended -- the task never saw one
# ended -- and played SE_EXIT every frame: an out player watching somebody on another
# map sat on a black screen with the sound chopped at 60 Hz. Now one warp, latched
# (gBrSpectate.warpWait, +14) until the map load.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 9
# seat 1 placed on ROUTE 101 (group 0, num 16) at (10, 10) -- 17, 17 with MAP_OFFSET --
# facing south: type 2, len 14 = hdr(0b 00 00) + seat 01, hasMap 01, group 00, num 10,
# x 11 00, y 11 00, facing 01, status 01, sprite 01
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 10 11 00 11 00 01 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 10
expect u8 gBrSeats+16 1
expect u8 gBrSeats+19 0x10
# FOLLOW seat 1: type 20 (0x14), len 4 = hdr(01 00 00) + seat
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 14 04 01 00 00 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 10
expect u8 gBrSpectate+8 1
# the warp is under way and latched: asked once, not once a frame
expectge u8 gBrSpectate+14 100
shot warping
# the fade, the old map's music and the load: on their map inside two seconds
wait 110
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 0x10
expect u8 gBrSpectate+14 0
# and on their ghost once it has spawned there
wait 60
expectge u8 gBrSpectate+9 1
shot followed
say spectate follow warp ok
