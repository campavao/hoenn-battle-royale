# surf-map-fade: START > MAP while surfing, then DOWN toward the shore during the map's
# fade (Cam, 2026-10-18: surfing on land). HideStartMenu hands the controls back, the
# fade (BrField_Leave) ran with them, a step ashore started the dismount jump, the field
# went mid-jump, and CB2_ReturnToField rebuilt the avatar from its sprite -- the surfing
# one -- on a land tile, which it then walked on its blob. The leave locks the controls
# and lets a step under way finish, so the player is still on the water when the map
# opens and back on it, surfing, when it closes.
# ROUTE 104 (0:19), boot at (33,22) land; (33,21) is sea.
wait 30
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
# Boot mode 21: TESTSWIM's MUDKIP could learn SURF (br_hm.h).
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 21 00 00 13 21 00 16 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
hold UP 20
wait 120
# surfing at (33,21)
expectbits u8 gPlayerAvatar 0x08 0x08
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 21
tap START
wait 40
# the MUDKIP: POKeMON, BAG, MAP, PLAYER, EXIT
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap A
wait 8
hold DOWN 40
wait 200
expect u8 gBrMap 1
tap B
wait 200
shot back-from-map
# still on the water, still surfing: the DOWN never became a step
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0 33
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 21
expectbits u8 gPlayerAvatar 0x08 0x08
# ...and the shore is still a proper dismount: ashore, on foot
hold DOWN 20
wait 120
shot ashore
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 22
expectbits u8 gPlayerAvatar 0x09 0x01
say surf map fade ok
