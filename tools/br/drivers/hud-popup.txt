# hud-popup: the map-name popup comes and goes in place, and leaves the HUD alone
# (2026-10-05 play-test: "our banner is connected to the route UI in the top left, and
# since that UI slides down when entering a route, so do our banners... very jarring";
# "it has a red highlight within"). The popup slid all of BG0, the HUD's layer too, and
# loaded its sign's colours over palette 14, the frame's -- every HUD box went red-framed
# while it showed. The HUD's frame is drawn from its own copy in palette 13 now.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrHud+0x00 7
poke u16 gBrHud+0x02 125
wait 30
# the corner is up, its frame's top-left cell (19,0) on palette 13
expectbits u8 gBrHud+0x09 1 1
expect u16 0x0600F826 0xD214
dump 0x050001A0 32
# seat 1 on ROUTE 101; following them warps there and the popup says ROUTE 101
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 10 11 00 11 00 01 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 10
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 14 04 01 00 00 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 150
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 0x10
# the popup's sign is up (its window is cells 1..10 of rows 1..3)
wait 40
shot popup-up
# palette 14 is the sign's now; 13, the frame's, is as it was
dump 0x050001C0 32
expect u16 0x050001A0 0x330C
expect u16 0x050001BE 0x2909
# the corner did not move and kept its colours: its frame is still palette 13, and the
# frame's colours are what they were before the popup came
expect u16 0x0600F826 0xD214
expectbits u8 gBrHud+0x09 1 1
wait 200
shot popup-gone
say hud popup ok
