# spill-own-full: the biggest first bag a SPILL carries, 102 bytes -- six mons, eight
# rows and a seven-letter name (HBR-88 review). SendBag's buffer was a byte short of it in
# #166, which read the bag header as seven bytes and left out the item count.
#
# spill-own-items.txt's walk-out with TREECKO copied into all six slots, the other five
# fainted, so the fog's last one down is the lead; the name ABCDEFG; ITEMS POTION..MAX
# POTION (13..20) one of each, so the dealt POKe BALLs go in a second bag.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400

expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
copy gPlayerParty+100 gPlayerParty 100
copy gPlayerParty+200 gPlayerParty 100
copy gPlayerParty+300 gPlayerParty 100
copy gPlayerParty+400 gPlayerParty 100
copy gPlayerParty+500 gPlayerParty 100

poke u16 gPlayerParty+0x11E 0
poke u16 gPlayerParty+0x182 0
poke u16 gPlayerParty+0x1E6 0
poke u16 gPlayerParty+0x24A 0
poke u8 gPlayerPartyCount 6
pokebytes *gSaveBlock2Ptr+0 BB BC BD BE BF C0 C1 FF
poke u32 *gSaveBlock2Ptr+0xAC 0
pokebytes *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0D 00 01 00 0E 00 01 00 0F 00 01 00 10 00 01 00 11 00 01 00 12 00 01 00 13 00 01 00 14 00 01 00
pokebytes *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x650 04 00 05 00
poke u16 gPlayerParty+0x56 2
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 01 19 0C 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1

wait 30
expect u8 gBrMatch 3
# the first bag: six mons, hasBag at 58, key 0x00FF at 59, eight rows at 66..89, the
# money at 90, then the name, seven bytes at 95..101
expectmsg 11#2 u8 3 6

expectmsg 11#2 u16 59 0x00FF
expectmsg 11#2 u8 65 8
expectmsg 11#2 u16 87 20
expectmsg 11#2 u8 94 7
expectmsg 11#2 u8 95 0xBB
expectmsg 11#2 u8 101 0xC1
