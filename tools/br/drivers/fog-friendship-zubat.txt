# fog-friendship-zubat: friendship rises as the fog closes (Cam, 2026-11-10: "raise
# friendship and beauty levels of pokemon while they are in your party as fog closes in
# (similar to level up) so that pokemon that evolve via those methods or moves that get
# stronger via those work"). A ZUBAT taken at rung 5 is as fond of you as any catch is
# (70); each ring that closes adds 50 -- 120, 170, 220, then the 255 cap -- so GOLBAT
# becomes CROBAT at the third lift (rung 50, where friendship first reaches the 220
# FRIENDSHIP_EVO_THRESHOLD), not the first. Before this, GrowUp asked as if every mon
# were at 255, so CROBAT came at rung 30 and RETURN never got stronger.
#
# Friendship is encrypted on a party mon; tools/br/fog-friendship.py decrypts the dumps.
# checked-by: tools/br/fog-friendship.py
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 0
# SPILL from seat 2: one ZUBAT (41) at level 5 on the cell we face, taken at rung 5
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0B 11 0E 00 00 02 00 09 01 00 02 0C 00 10 00 29 00 05 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 20
tap A
wait 30
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
# Z U B A T
expect u8 gPlayerParty+8 0xD4
expect u8 gPlayerParty+9 0xCF
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 5
say stage 0 rung 5
dump gPlayerParty 100
# ring 1 (the match's first, tier 0, the rung it began at): nothing is warmer yet. Real
# matches go 1 then 2, so the step must not fire here too.
poke u8 gBrRing+1 1
poke u8 gBrRing 1
wait 200
say stage 5 ring 1
dump gPlayerParty 100
# ring 2: rung 15
poke u8 gBrRing+1 2
wait 200
expect u8 gBrLevels 15
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 15
say stage 1 rung 15
dump gPlayerParty 100
# ring 3: rung 30 -- GOLBAT (22) but friendship is only 170: still no CROBAT
poke u8 gBrRing+1 3
wait 200
expect u8 gBrLevels 30
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 30
# G O L B A T
expect u8 gPlayerParty+8 0xC1
expect u8 gPlayerParty+9 0xC9
expect u8 gPlayerParty+10 0xC6
say stage 2 rung 30
dump gPlayerParty 100
# ring 4: rung 50 -- friendship 220, CROBAT
poke u8 gBrRing+1 4
wait 200
expect u8 gBrLevels 50
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 50
# C R O B A T
expect u8 gPlayerParty+8 0xBD
expect u8 gPlayerParty+9 0xCC
expect u8 gPlayerParty+10 0xC9
say stage 3 rung 50
dump gPlayerParty 100
# ring 5: rung 75 -- the cap
poke u8 gBrRing+1 5
wait 200
expect u8 gBrLevels 75
say stage 4 rung 75
dump gPlayerParty 100
say fog friendship zubat ok
