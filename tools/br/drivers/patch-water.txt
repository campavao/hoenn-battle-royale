# patch-water: a patch on water is a dark blue shadow under the surface that ripples now and
# then, as the grass ones rustle (POK-356, POK-357). A ripple alone, in the palette's darker
# blues, was all but lost on Hoenn's sea (review of #19); the shadow carries it.
#
# ROUTE 107 (map 0:22, wild header 0x1C, water only) under match seed 4 deals eight
# patches; from (20,10) patch 7, at (23,13) -- (0x1E,0x14) with the +7 -- is in view and
# drawn. The shot is checked by eye; the byte says it is drawn at all.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 16 14 00 0A 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 4
wait 120
expect u16 gBrPatch 0x0016
expect u16 gBrPatch+2 0x1C
expect u8 gBrPatch+4 8
expect u16 gBrPatch+30 0x1E
expect u16 gBrPatch+46 0x14
expectne u8 gBrPatch+12 64
shot water
say patch water ok
