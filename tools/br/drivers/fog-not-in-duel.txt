# fog-not-in-duel: the fog does not reach into a fight between contestants (POK-262).
# The other half of fog-in-battle.txt, and the half that matters for fairness: a duel
# decided by whose map the ring happens to be over is not a duel.
#
# A loopback netlink battle (the same setup run-roll.txt uses), then the ring closes
# somewhere else, and nothing bleeds for as long as the fight lasts.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 0F 00 10 00 03 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 900
# in the duel
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
expect u16 gBrRing+10 0
# the fog closes on Littleroot: RING phase 1, centre (25,12), r 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 01 19 0C 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 600
shot duel
# two and a half bleed ticks' worth of nothing
expect u16 gBrRing+10 0
expect u8 gBrRing+7 0
say fog not in duel ok
