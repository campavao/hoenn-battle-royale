# trainer-team: a beaten trainer drops THEIR WHOLE TEAM, not just their lead (POK-291).
#
# Kanto's rule, from its README: "Kanto's trainers drop their teams too (at the rung they
# fought at), which gives PvE a point beyond levels -- and means a route can be picked
# over." Ours dropped party[0] with a hard-coded count of 1, so clearing a route was worth
# about a third of what it is worth there.
#
# trainer-drop.txt walks into Josh in Rustboro Gym, who carries exactly one Pokemon -- so
# it could never have caught this, and did not. HALEY on Route 104 (31,24) carries two.
#
# OUTDOORS on purpose. A spill needs somewhere to put each ball: NextCell walks thirteen
# offsets around the trainer and skips anything the metatile grid calls solid, so inside
# Rustboro Gym -- a maze of walls -- a three-mon team still only had room for one ball,
# and the driver read that as the loop not working. A route has room.
#
# He spots US -- POK-259's ambush -- rather than being talked to. Ivan on Route 104 was
# the first pick and carries three, but his sight range is 0, so the only way in is a
# conversation, and A on a trainer did not start one from a driver.
#
# HALEY faces LEFT with a sight range of 7, so the boot lands two tiles to her LEFT --
# into her line, not behind her, which is the mistake Devan cost.
#
# Route 102 was the first pick and is no good for this: several trainers there reach the
# same tile, so WHICH one ambushed varied run to run, and a two-mon trainer and a one-mon
# trainer make this pass and fail at random. Haley's nearest neighbour, Winston at
# (21,25), has a sight of 3 and is eight tiles away.
#
# The boot lands ON the cell it names out here: the one-tile step south Littleroot's boot
# makes is a DOOR-EXIT animation, not something every warp does.
wait 30

wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrMatch+1 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 03 19 0C 3C 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
expect u8 gBrLevels 30
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 0

# Both of their Pokemon are on the ground, each on its own cell, and they are gone.
#
# `count` is the table and is the assertion that matters. `spawned` is only the balls
# whose object events exist right now, and Emerald keeps object events near the camera --
# a ball two tiles the wrong side of the screen edge is on the ground and not spawned, so
# that one is >= 1 rather than == 2 (the same thing npcout-peer.txt had to learn).
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 2
expectge u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 1
expectge u8 gBrLoot+0xA3 1
# ...and they are HALEY's. A key is `0x8000 | (partyIndex << 11) | trainerId`, so trainer
# 604 gives 0x825C for the lead and 0x8A5C for the second. Without this the driver passes
# on any two-mon trainer who wanders into range, which is how it read as flaky before.
expect u16 gBrLoot+0 0x825C
expect u16 gBrLoot+20 0x8A5C
say trainer team ok
