# win-mid-fight: the match is decided while we are in a fight, and the parade still runs
# (10-19 play-test #27: "P1 WINS" went up mid-battle, and the results came ~50 s later,
# at the page's 60 s give-up, with no Hall of Fame in between).
#
# The link control for win-bot-fight.txt: a link fight already ended on its peer's OUT
# (br_netlink.c's TickPeerOut) before 10-19 #27, and BR_RUN_OVER leaves it to that.
#
# out-peer.txt's session: we challenge seat 1 with loopback on, so the fight is real up
# to the first choice. Then seat 1 goes out (the fog took them) and the page says we won.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
# CHALLENGE: challenger 00 (us), opponent 01, nonce 0
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 600
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 200
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
expect u8 gBattleOutcome 0
shot menu
# OUT: seat 1, then RESULT: seat 0 (us), outcome 0 -- the win, in the order the host
# sends them.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 9 gDecompressionBuffer 1
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 16 gDecompressionBuffer 2
wait 300
shot after
# BR_PHASE_WIN (4): the parade is on, a few seconds after the fight, not a minute.
expect u8 gBrMatch 4
say win mid fight ok
