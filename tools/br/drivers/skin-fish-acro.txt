# skin-fish-acro: an NPC look fishes and rides the ACRO BIKE in its own sprite (POK-411,
# PR #72 review). Both used to swap it to pret's BRENDAN or MAY for the duration, and the
# look has no rod or wheelie frames: the rod's casts and the wheelies stand, facing,
# and putting the rod away no longer waits on an animation that loops (it hung).
#
# hm-bike-surf.txt's spot: ROUTE 104 at (36,22), the sea to the north. PICNICKER, skin 7.
wait 30
poke u8 gBrMySkin 7
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 01 00 13 24 00 16 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 19
# face the water, then the OLD ROD (262) on SELECT
tap UP
wait 20
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x496 262
tap SELECT
wait 90
shot cast
expect u8 gObjectEvents+5 32
wait 200
shot waiting
# no A on the bite: it gets away, and the rod goes away -- the wait that used to hang
wait 120
shot got-away
# one A for "It got away..." -- another, facing the sea, would ask to SURF
hold A 4
wait 200
shot after-fish
expect u8 gObjectEvents+5 32
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
# the ACRO BIKE (272) on SELECT, facing away from the water (a bike into water surfs,
# hm-bike-surf.txt), a step, then B held: the wheelie
tap DOWN
wait 20
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x496 272
tap SELECT
wait 60
expectbits u8 gPlayerAvatar 0x04 0x04
expect u8 gObjectEvents+5 32
hold LEFT 16
shot acro
hold B 60
shot wheelie
expect u8 gObjectEvents+5 32
hold LEFT 16
wait 30
shot after-acro
expect u8 gObjectEvents+5 32
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
say skin fish acro ok
