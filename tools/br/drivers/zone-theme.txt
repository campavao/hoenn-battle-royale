# zone-theme: a seed that is already there when the opening begins still gets its
# "A <TYPE> MATCH!" line. zone-pool.txt's seed arrives in a START after the boot, with
# the opening already on; a solo page writes its seed into gBrMatch before the boot, and
# a room's START comes before the warp into the Zone. Both of those reach
# BrMatch_BeginSafari with the seed in hand, and it dealt the pool before it set the
# phase: SayTheme saw PHASE_NONE and said nothing, and dealtFor made every later deal a
# no-op. No solo match and no room announced its theme.
#
# zone-pool.txt's seed, so the deal is its GRASS match.
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x12345678
# boot mode 2 = the Safari Zone
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 02 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 BD BB C7 FF
# The boot lands about 200 frames on, and a ticker line is up for 180 field frames
# after that (BR_HUD_LINE_FRAMES): this looks in the middle of its time on screen.
wait 260
expect u8 gBrMatch 1
# dealt at the boot, from the seed that was waiting for it
expect u32 gBrZone 0x12345678
expect u16 gBrZone+4 283
# ...and said: the ticker's first line is "A GRASS MATCH!" (A, space, G, R)
expectge u8 gBrHud+0x0A 1
expect u8 gBrHud+0x46 0xBB
expect u8 gBrHud+0x47 0x00
expect u8 gBrHud+0x48 0xC1
expect u8 gBrHud+0x49 0xCC
wait 30
shot theme
say zone theme ok
