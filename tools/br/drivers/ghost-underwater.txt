# ghost-underwater: under the sea a ghost rides one picture, as the diving player does
# (Cam's 2026-10-27 play-test: "when underwater or surfacing, sometimes there is
# artifacting around the head"). By the tile alone the seabed stood them up and the
# seaweed sat them on a SURF blob, so every step between the two swapped picture and blob.
#
# ROUTE 126 (map 0:41) boots on the sea above UNDERWATER ROUTE 126 (0:51). Seat 2 (skin 5)
# is on the seabed at (47,17); we FOLLOW them down, and they walk south onto the seaweed.
wait 30
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 21 00 00 29 28 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch+1 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 03 19 0C 3C 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 02 0E 0B 00 00 02 01 00 33 2F 00 11 00 01 01 05
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 5
# FOLLOW seat 2
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 14 04 01 00 00 02
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 200
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 51
expect u8 gBrSeats+41 1
shot seabed
# on the bare seabed their blob is already under them: sprite 59, riding object 1
expectbits u8 gSprites+0xFEA 0x01 0x01
expect u8 gSprites+0xFDE 1
# STEP south twice, onto the seaweed
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x10D8 03 0B 08 00 00 02 01 2F 00 12 00 00 33
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 4
wait 16
expectbits u8 gSprites+0xFEA 0x01 0x01
expect u8 gSprites+0xFDE 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1118 03 0B 08 00 00 02 01 2F 00 13 00 00 33
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 5
wait 30
shot seaweed
expectbits u8 gSprites+0xFEA 0x01 0x01
expect u8 gSprites+0xFDE 1
say ghost underwater ok
