# see-through: the battle over the map, an experiment (2026-10-05 play-test: "disable
# the white battle background so the battle overlays the map").
#
# The page asks with gBrSeeThrough[0]; a battle then draws its backdrop and the
# environment's sky and ground stripes pure blue, BR_SEE_THROUGH_KEY, which the page's
# filter makes transparent so the field it draws under the picture shows through. The
# platforms keep their colours (2026-10-07 play-test: "the circles ... still visible").
# [1] counts down from 2 every battle VBlank, so the page keys the picture only while a
# battle draws it.
# bot-fight.txt's card and challenge.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrSeeThrough 1
expect u8 gBrSeeThrough+1 0
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 03 BC C9 CE 01 15 01 05 13 00 13 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 CE CC BF C5 C9
mailsend gBrMailbox 23 gDecompressionBuffer 106
wait 10
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 01 00 09 14 00 08 00 01 01 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 300
expect u8 gBrBotFight+2 1
expectge u8 gBrSeeThrough+1 1
# the backdrop and the stripes (BG palette 2 colours 1, 11-15; 3: 1, 15) are the key;
# the platforms' (2: 2, 3; 3: 2) are not, and BG3 (DISPCNT bit 11) is still on
expect u16 0x05000000 0x7C00
expect u16 0x05000042 0x7C00
expect u16 0x05000056 0x7C00
expect u16 0x0500005A 0x7C00
expect u16 0x0500005E 0x7C00
expect u16 0x05000062 0x7C00
expect u16 0x0500007E 0x7C00
expectne u16 0x05000044 0x7C00
expectne u16 0x05000046 0x7C00
expectne u16 0x05000064 0x7C00
expectbits u16 0x04000000 0x0800 0x0800
shot battle
