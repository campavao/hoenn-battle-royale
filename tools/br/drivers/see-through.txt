# see-through: the battle over the map, an experiment (2026-10-05 play-test: "disable
# the white battle background so the battle overlays the map").
#
# The page asks with gBrSeeThrough[0]; a battle then draws no terrain (BG3 off in
# DISPCNT) and a pure blue backdrop, BR_SEE_THROUGH_KEY, which the page's filter makes
# transparent so the field it draws under the picture shows through. [1] counts down
# from 2 every battle VBlank, so the page keys the picture only while a battle draws it.
# bot-fight.txt's card and challenge.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrSeeThrough 1
expect u8 gBrSeeThrough+1 0
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 03 BC C9 CE 01 15 01 05 13 00 13 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 CE CC BF C5 C9
mailsend gBrMailbox 23 gDecompressionBuffer 106
wait 10
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 01 00 09 14 00 08 00 01 01 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 300
expect u8 gBrBotFight+2 1
expectge u8 gBrSeeThrough+1 1
# the backdrop is the key, and BG3 (DISPCNT bit 11) is off
expect u16 0x05000000 0x7C00
expectbits u16 0x04000000 0x0800 0
shot battle
