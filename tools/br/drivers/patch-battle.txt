# patch-battle: grass meets nothing until you step on a patch you can see (Cam,
# 2026-10-13: "no step encounters anywhere (grass, caves, water). Darkened indicators
# you can see instead").
#
# ROUTE 102 (map 0:17, wild header 1) under match seed 4 deals eight patches. Two are on
# screen from the boot cell (20,9): (18,9) and (18,12), map coordinates +7 in gBrPatch.
# A step west onto plain grass meets nothing; the next lands on (18,9), patch 1, and the
# battle starts -- on the first steps after a map load, which used to be immune. Its key, 0x2000 | 1 << 3 | 1, goes out
# as a PICKUP so every ROM takes it off, and after the battle it is not drawn again.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 4
# a slice of the map a frame (br_patch.c), then drawn
wait 30
expect u16 gBrPatch 0x0011
expect u16 gBrPatch+2 1
expect u8 gBrPatch+4 8
expect u16 gBrPatch+18 0x19
expect u16 gBrPatch+34 0x10
expectne u8 gBrPatch+6 64
shot patches
# one step west, onto plain grass at (19,9): nothing
tap LEFT
wait 4
tap LEFT
wait 250
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 0
expect u16 gObjectEvents+0x10 26
drain gBrMailbox
# and one more, onto the patch
tap LEFT
wait 250
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 4
expectmsg 10 u16 1 0x2009
expectmsg 10 u8 3 0
shot wild
say patch battle ok
