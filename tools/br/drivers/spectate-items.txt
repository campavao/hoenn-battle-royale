# spectate-items: a spectator's replay plays a fighter's bag and a POKe DOLL (POK-330
# #12). Emerald bans items in a recorded link battle, and the ban wrote 0xFF over the
# replay's read cursor: the replay of a fighter's potion froze on that turn for good. A
# DOLL rode in RUN's return value, which the record never carried, so the replay said
# "Can't escape!" and then waited on a turn that was never coming.
#
# spectate-watch.txt's setup, with a second mon for the challenger at 5 HP on the bench.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 78 56 34 12 0E 00 00 00 BD BB C7 FF 00 00 00 00 BD BB C8 FF 00 00 00 00 00 01 02
copy gDecompressionBuffer+29 gPlayerParty 100
copy gDecompressionBuffer+129 gPlayerParty 100
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer+215 05 00
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer+229 01
copy gDecompressionBuffer+230 gPlayerParty 100
mailsend gBrMailbox 18 gDecompressionBuffer 330
wait 10
expect u8 gBrSpectate+5 1
# Turn one. The challenger (battler 0) drinks a POTION into its benched mon: USE_ITEM, then
# 0x0D, 0 (the id, seven bits a byte), slot 1, move 0. The peer (battler 1) is on the
# opponent's side, so its item is an X ATTACK with its AI's reading of it: type 4
# (AI_ITEM_X_STAT), flags 1 (X ATTACK).
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer+400 00 01 00 05 01 0D 00 01 00 01 05 01 4B 00 04 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer+400 16
wait 1500
shot items
# The benched mon took the potion: 5 HP and 20 more is all of it (19 or 20, by IVs).
expectge u16 gPlayerParty+186 19
# The peer's X ATTACK went up a stage on the replay too: ATK is statStages[1] at +0x19.
expect u8 gBattleMons+0x71 7
expect u8 gBrSpectate+5 1
# Turn two: the challenger RUNs with a POKe DOLL (RUN, BR_RUN_DOLL); the peer FIGHTs.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer+400 00 01 00 02 03 01 01 03 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer+400 11
wait 600
# The replay honoured the doll: one run, one getaway (this ROM's own counters, which the
# replay's HandleAction_Run reaches like a fighter's).
expect u16 gBrBattle+4 1
expect u16 gBrBattle+6 1
wait 600
# ...and it ended, as the fight did, with the viewer back on the field.
expect u8 gBrSpectate+5 0
say spectate items ok
