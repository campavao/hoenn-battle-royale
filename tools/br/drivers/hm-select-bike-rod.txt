# hm-select-bike-rod: SELECT rides the bike on dry land even with the rod registered (Cam's
# 2026-10-19 play-test: "select does not automatically bring out my bike"). The phone's
# FISH button registers the rod, and SELECT used to cast it again wherever you stood.
# BrHm_SelectBike lets a rod come first only facing water.
#
# hm-select-bike's spot: ROUTE 104 (map 0:19) at (33,22), facing south onto land; the
# match bag has the OLD ROD (BrLevels_GiveStartingBag) and both bikes.
wait 30
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 13 21 00 16 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 19
# the OLD ROD (262) registered
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x496 262
tap SELECT
wait 60
shot select-bike-rod
# on the MACH BIKE, registered in the rod's place
expectbits u8 gPlayerAvatar 0x02 0x02
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x496 259
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
say select bike over rod ok
