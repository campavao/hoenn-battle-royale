# peek-sign: the map-name sign and the spectator's peek box never draw over each other
# (Cam's 2026-10-14 play-test, POK-370: "you can see an artifact if both of them are
# open at the same time" -- the ROUTE 118 sign cut into the box's corner, and its erase
# took the box's cells with it). OpenPeek takes a sign that is up down first, and a sign
# due while the box is up waits in Task_MapNamePopUpWindow's print wait until it closes.
#
# hud-popup's follow: seat 1 on ROUTE 101, and we warp there to watch. The box's cell
# (2,3), inside both the sign's cells and the box's, is read off BG0's tilemap.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrHud+0x00 7
poke u16 gBrHud+0x02 125
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 10 11 00 11 00 01 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 10
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 14 04 01 00 00 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
# On ROUTE 101 with its sign still to come: open the box before it prints
wait 100
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 0x10
tap START
wait 2
expect u8 gBrSpectate+0x0A 1
# the box's own tile there (0xF01A: palette 15, tile 0x01A -- its interior), when the
# sign would have printed, while it would have shown, and after it would have gone
expect u16 0x0600F8C4 0xF01A
wait 40
expect u16 0x0600F8C4 0xF01A
wait 100
expect u16 0x0600F8C4 0xF01A
wait 60
shot box-kept
expect u16 0x0600F8C4 0xF01A
expect u8 gBrSpectate+0x0A 1
# closed: the sign it held comes now
tap START
wait 60
shot sign-after
expect u8 gBrSpectate+0x0A 0
expect u16 0x0600F8C4 0xE11C
# ...and with that sign up, the box opened over it takes it down first: the sign's
# erase two seconds on would otherwise clear the box's cells
tap START
wait 2
expect u8 gBrSpectate+0x0A 1
expect u16 0x0600F8C4 0xF01A
wait 200
shot box-over-sign
expect u16 0x0600F8C4 0xF01A
