# ring-preview: the MAP shows the next ring (Cam, 2026-10-18): "if an area is going to be
# covered in fog, for the round prior it will have it slowly blinking purple... and there
# should be a white circle around where the fog is actually going to close in on".
#
# The host's RING carries the next radius after the elapsed seconds (hasNext, next). On
# the map the cells this ring keeps and the next does not get a tile of their own after
# the fog's, which the fly map's VBlank turns on and off (BrMap_BlinkFog): whole, with no
# dithered steps between, and no circle (Cam, 2026-10-18: "just hide the dots and big
# white circle").

wait 400
# RING phase 3, centre (12,7), r 9, next 7, naming LITTLEROOT: payload seat 00 phase 03
# sx 0C sy 07 r 09 placeLen 0A + the name + hasElapsed 00, elapsed 00 00, hasNext 01,
# next 07 = 21 bytes; with the frame (15 00 00), 0x18
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 18 15 00 00 00 03 0C 07 09 0A C6 C3 CE CE C6 BF CC C9 C9 CE 00 00 00 01 07
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
expect u8 gBrRing+4 9
expect u8 gBrRing+32 7
# START, MAP (two below the top in a match)
poke u8 gBrMatch 2

wait 40
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap A
wait 240
expect u8 gBrMap 1
# BG1's tilemap is 0x0600F000, the grid from its cell (1,2). Along the centre's row: the
# centre (12,7) and (19,7), seven out, are the frame's own; (20,7) and (21,7), which this
# ring keeps and the next does not, are the blink tile 0x06 in the fog's palette 10;
# (22,7), ten out, is the fog's 0x05.
expect u16 0x0600F25A 0x1000
expect u16 0x0600F268 0x1000
expect u16 0x0600F26A 0xA006
expect u16 0x0600F26C 0xA006
expect u16 0x0600F26E 0xA005
expect u16 gBrMap+2 6

# fog for the first 48 frames of each 96, clear for the rest -- every pixel colour 1 or
# none, never a dot pattern

expect u32 0x0600C0DC 0x11111111
