# engage-settle-out: engage-settle.txt up to the settle, and then the seat goes out (the
# fog, or a closed tab) while its ghost is still walking. No link opens to it -- that was
# ten seconds of black and a forfeit, waiting on a hello nobody sends -- and we walk again.
#
# Cam's play-test (2026-10-05): "make sure what one person is seeing in the overworld
# ahead of time is the same as the other". Two things let the screens disagree. The
# challenger kept walking through BR_ENGAGE_WAIT (90 frames) and went into the fight from
# somewhere the other screen never drew it; and the fight started with the opponent's
# ghost still playing steps the wire had already given it. Now the challenge freezes us,
# and the fight starts only once the ghost has caught up (BrEngage_Settle).
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+2 12
# Seat 1 at (15,16) facing WEST on Littleroot, three tiles east of us: engage.txt's spot.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 01 00 09 0F 00 10 00 03 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
wait 20
expect u16 gBrEngage+4 1
expect u8 gBrEngage+7 1
# The wait: we try to walk off west, and stay on the cell we challenged from.
hold LEFT 40
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+2 12
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+4 16
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
# The steps they took before our challenge reached them, landing late: five EAST, to
# (20,16). Eighty frames of walking for the ghost, past the end of our wait.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 04 10 00 10 00 00 09
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 8
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 04 11 00 10 00 00 09
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 8
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 04 12 00 10 00 00 09
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 8
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 04 13 00 10 00 00 09
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 8
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 04 14 00 10 00 00 09
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 8
wait 60
# The wait is over and the ghost is still walking: settling with seat 1, no link yet.
expect u8 gBrEngage+7 0xFF
expect u8 gBrEngage+11 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
expect u16 gBrSeats+20 20
shot settling
# Seat 1 goes out mid-settle.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 9 gDecompressionBuffer 1
wait 10
expect u8 gBrEngage+11 0xFF
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
wait 120
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
# Free again: walking west moves us.
hold LEFT 24
expectle u16 gBrOwnPos+2 11
shot free
say engage settle out ok
