# ring-tall: the field's ring is 32x64 tiles on 256x512 BGs, and every cell of it holds
# the map (POK-329). tools/br/ring-tall.py rebuilds each cell from the layout, the
# tilesets' metatiles and layer types -- what DrawMetatile writes -- and compares all
# 32 x 16 metatiles of all three layers' heap buffers, at the rotation the scroll
# registers give, on every record below. See include/br/br_field.h, THE RING.
#
# Rustboro City's column 21 is a clear walk from row 0 to row 39 with no grass, no
# trainer and nobody wandering into it, and from (21,11) to (21,39) the ring's 32 rows
# (the player's -11..+20) are all inside the map. Landed at (21,25): 14 steps down, a
# turn and 28 up (the tile offset wraps the 64-row ring more than once), 6 right and 6
# left along row 11, then up and over the connection into Route 115, to (21,77).
#
# Each step from standing, facing its way, starts on the first frame the direction is
# held: the two frames of `hold` are the step's first and second (watched), the dump
# after `wait 2` is its middle (the camera 8 pixels in), and the one after `wait 8` is
# at rest. A step up must have its new top row in its slot from the first frame, a step
# left its new column's 32 rows; a step down leaves the new bottom row's slot holding
# the row behind it until the step is done, and a step right its far column. Past the
# connection the ring's rows beyond pret's were drawn from Rustboro's grid, where the
# ones more than 7 above its edge are border: at rest on Route 115 they must be Route
# 115's rows. A turn (hold 9: eight frames to face, then the step) is checked at rest.
# The parent's 256x256 BGs fail on every record (BGxCNT size 0).
# checked-by: tools/br/ring-tall.py
