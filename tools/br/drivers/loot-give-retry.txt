# loot-give-retry: the rest of a bag the out ring has no room for goes back on the ground
# when there is room, and not before (POK-331 #6).
#
# loot-give-full.txt puts what did not fit back down as a SPILL. HandleGive ignored
# BrWire_SendLarge's FALSE and put the bag on our own ground anyway, so on a full ring
# the room never heard of it: the page keeps a bag's contents only from its SPILL, and
# whoever took it next got no GIVE. The ROM now holds the SPILL and sends it from
# BrLoot_Tick, and the bag lands here in the same frame it goes out.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# the ITEMS pocket, all thirty slots: POTION..BLACK FLUTE (13..42), one of each
pokebytes *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0D 00 01 00 0E 00 01 00 0F 00 01 00 10 00 01 00 11 00 01 00 12 00 01 00 13 00 01 00 14 00 01 00 15 00 01 00 16 00 01 00 17 00 01 00 18 00 01 00 19 00 01 00 1A 00 01 00 1B 00 01 00 1C 00 01 00 1D 00 01 00 1E 00 01 00 1F 00 01 00 20 00 01 00 21 00 01 00 22 00 01 00 23 00 01 00 24 00 01 00 25 00 01 00 26 00 01 00 27 00 01 00 28 00 01 00 29 00 01 00 2A 00 01 00
# seat 3's bag on our own tile: key 0x03FF, (12,16), map 0:9, kind 2 (BR_LOOT_BAG),
# objId BR_NO_OBJ, money 1200 (0x4B0)
pokebytes gBrLoot FF 03 0C 00 10 00 00 00 00 09 00 02 FF 00 00 00 B0 04 00 00
wait 60
tap A
wait 20
# "FOUND 1200!", and the bag's pickup is out: the row is gone from our ground.
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 1
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 0
# The page stops taking slots, and the ring is full: 64 slots between tail and head.
drain 0x0
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0A 0
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x08 64
# GIVE [count 3] (ORAN BERRY x2) (NUGGET x1) (TM06 x1): the NUGGET needs a slot the
# ITEMS pocket does not have.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 03 8B 00 02 6E 00 01 26 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 30 gDecompressionBuffer 10
wait 10
# The berries and the TM are in, and the box says the rest did not fit...
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x790 139
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B0 294
expect u8 gBrHud+0x14D 32
# ...but nothing got out, so the NUGGET is not on our ground either: the old ROM put it
# there with no SPILL behind it.
expect u16 gBrMailbox+0x08 64
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 0
wait 30
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 0
# The page takes slots again, and the held SPILL goes out: under our seat, no mons, the
# bag 0x03FF on (12,16) with the NUGGET in it...
drain gBrMailbox
wait 3
expectmsg 11 u8 0 0
expectmsg 11 u8 3 0
expectmsg 11 u8 4 1
expectmsg 11 u16 5 0x03FF
expectmsg 11 u16 7 12
expectmsg 11 u16 9 16
expectmsg 11 u8 11 1
expectmsg 11 u16 12 110
expectmsg 11 u8 14 1
# ...and lands on our own ground as it does: a bag with the cash already gone.
expect u16 gBrLoot 0x03FF
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 2
expect u16 gBrLoot+2 12
expect u16 gBrLoot+4 16
expect u32 gBrLoot+16 0
shot sent
say loot give retry ok
