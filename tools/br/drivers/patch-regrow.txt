# patch-regrow: a taken patch grows back when the ring moves on a phase (Cam, 2026-10-14:
# "Patches can regrow over time"; br_patch.c, Regrow on a new gBrRing.phase).
#
# patch-battle.txt's ROUTE 102 under seed 4: patch 1 at (18,9) is drawn from the boot
# cell. Seat 1's PICKUP of its key, 0x2009, takes it off and it stays off for the rest
# of phase 1. The RING for phase 2 brings it back, drawn. A PICKUP read in the same
# frame as phase 3's RING takes it for phase 3 (Settle before the mark). Phase 4 brings
# it back again, and a step onto it is a battle that sends its PICKUP.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# a lift here picks its own four: the fifth-move prompt is learn-ask.txt's (POK-400)
poke u8 gBrAsk+7 1
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 4
# RING phase 1, centre 25,12, r 30 (inside everywhere)
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 01 19 0C 1E 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
expect u8 gBrRing+1 1
expectne u8 gBrPatch+6 64
# PICKUP [seat 1, key 0x2009, the whole piece]
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 0A 0B 08 00 00 01 09 20 00 00 00 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 10
expect u8 gBrPatch+6 64
# ...and still gone a while into the same phase
wait 120
expect u8 gBrPatch+6 64
shot taken
# RING phase 2: everything taken in phase 1 grows back
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 02 19 0C 1E 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 10
expect u8 gBrRing+1 2
expectne u8 gBrPatch+6 64
shot regrown
# RING phase 3 and seat 1's PICKUP in one frame: grown back, then taken, in that order
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x10D8 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 03 19 0C 1E 00 00
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1118 0A 0B 08 00 00 01 09 20 00 00 00 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 5
wait 10
expect u8 gBrRing+1 3
expect u8 gBrPatch+6 64
# RING phase 4: back again
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1158 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 04 19 0C 1E 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 6
wait 10
expect u8 gBrRing+1 4
expectne u8 gBrPatch+6 64
# west twice, as patch-battle.txt: plain grass, then the patch
drain gBrMailbox
tap LEFT
wait 4
tap LEFT
wait 250
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 0
expect u16 gObjectEvents+0x10 26
tap LEFT
wait 250
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 4
expectmsg 10 u16 1 0x2009
shot wild
say patch regrow ok
