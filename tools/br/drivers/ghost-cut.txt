# ghost-cut: a ghost walking into a cuttable tree cuts it down first (2026-10-12 match
# video: a bot walked straight through one). The page lets any seat cross a tree, as
# everybody carries the HMs (bots/brain.ts canCut); the ROM shows the cut.
#
# Petalburg Woods' tree at (19,11) (FLAG_TEMP_12, which removeobject sets). We boot
# three tiles south of it and put seat 1 one tile south of it, facing north.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 18 0B 13 00 0E 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1272 0x04 0x00
# PLACE seat 1 at map (19,12), outer (26,19) -- PLACE and STEP carry outer coords, map
# + MAP_OFFSET (ghost-walkthrough.txt) -- facing north (2), skin 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 18 0B 1A 00 13 00 02 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeats+16 1
expectne u8 gBrSeats+25 0xFF
shot placed
# STEP north into the tree, outer (26,18)
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 03 0B 08 00 00 01 02 1A 00 12 00 18 0B
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 12
# the tree is being cut: the step waits on it, and the tree is still there
expect u8 gBrSeats+26 1
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1272 0x04 0x00
shot cutting
wait 80
# cut and removed, and the ghost walked on into its tile
expect u8 gBrSeats+26 0
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1272 0x04 0x04
shot cut
# The second tree, (19,10) (FLAG_TEMP_11), and a ghost that will not wait for it: six
# more STEPs north behind the one into the tree overflow the queue, and the ghost snaps
# to where the roster has it, past the tree. The cut it started still finishes: no
# invisible tree left standing.
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 03 0B 08 00 00 01 02 1A 00 11 00 18 0B
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 4
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1272 0x02 0x00
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x10D8 03 0B 08 00 00 01 02 1A 00 10 00 18 0B
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1118 03 0B 08 00 00 01 02 1A 00 0F 00 18 0B
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1158 03 0B 08 00 00 01 02 1A 00 0E 00 18 0B
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1198 03 0B 08 00 00 01 02 1A 00 0D 00 18 0B
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x11D8 03 0B 08 00 00 01 02 1A 00 0C 00 18 0B
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1218 03 0B 08 00 00 01 02 1A 00 0B 00 18 0B
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 9
wait 4
expect u8 gBrSeats+26 0
wait 80
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1272 0x02 0x02
shot snapped-past
