# ring-far-column: the ring's sixteenth column after a step right (POK-319). Emerald's
# slice redraw draws columns pos.x..pos.x+14 and leaves the sixteenth stale; the band
# past the LCD shows it, so BrField_Tick draws metatile column pos.x+15 into it -- all 32
# of the ring's rows since POK-329 (include/br/br_field.h, THE RING).
#
# From Littleroot (5,9) one step right puts pos at (6,9) with the ring's x tile offset at
# 2, so the sixteenth slot is tile columns (2 + 30) % 32 = 0..1 of every row of BG2's
# tilemap (0x0600C800, the middle layer): u32 at 0x0600C800 + row * 64. The values are
# metatile (21, 9 + row/2)'s, read off a whole-map draw booted straight at (6,9), where
# that metatile sits in tile columns 30..31 (u32 at 0x0600C83C + row * 64): rows 12..15
# are the edge of a building, not the plain grass the stale column 20 would show. The
# same address tells the whole story: metatile 5 at rest, still metatile 5 mid-step,
# metatile 21 once the step is done (BrField_Tick draws it on the completing frame).
# Tile rows 32..33 (0x0600D000) are ring row pos.y+16, past pret's sixteen: the old
# column there is the map's west edge (0x20092180), the far one a path, 0x20032002 --
# what the boot at (6,9) has in tile columns 30..31 there. In the parent's 256-row ring
# that address is not BG2's map, and it is 0.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
say pos-at-rest
dump *gSaveBlock1Ptr 4
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 5
say rest-slot-0-row-12-is-metatile-5
expect u32 0x0600CB00 0x20092180
# Ten frames in (hold N is N+1 frames; the first eight face and start the step) the tile
# offset has advanced, so slot 0 is now also "slot 30", and it must STILL hold metatile 5:
# the scroll lags the tile by 12 pixels and those are on screen. Drawing the far column
# here was the "jitter on the top left" of Cam's 2026-09-18 phone play-test.
expect u32 0x0600D000 0x20092180
hold RIGHT 9
say mid-step-camera
dump gFieldCamera+16 4
expectne u32 gFieldCamera+16 0
expect u32 0x0600CB00 0x20092180
expect u32 0x0600D000 0x20092180
wait 40
say pos-after-a-step-right
dump *gSaveBlock1Ptr 4
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 6
say far-column-rows-12-15
dump 0x0600CB00 4
dump 0x0600CB40 4
dump 0x0600CB80 4
dump 0x0600CBC0 4
expect u32 0x0600CB00 0x628C628B
expect u32 0x0600CB40 0x629C629B
expect u32 0x0600CB80 0x52515250
expect u32 0x0600CBC0 0x52615260
say far-column-row-16
expect u32 0x0600D000 0x20032002
expect u32 0x0600D040 0x20022003
say ok
