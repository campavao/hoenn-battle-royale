# pvp-bag: the bag works against a person (Kanto POK-207). Emerald bans items in every
# link battle -- Nintendo's own link rules, not a limit of the cable -- and our PvP is
# a link battle, so a contestant who could drink a potion against a bot could not
# against a player. Two kinds of fight, two sets of rules, one match.
#
# netlink-loop.txt's own session: CHALLENGE with loopback on, so our blocks come back
# as the peer's and a real link battle starts. Then FIGHT is passed over for BAG.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
# CHALLENGE: type 5, framed len 7 = hdr(04 00 00) + challenger 00, opponent 01, nonce 0
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 05 07 04 00 00 00 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 600
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
expect u8 gWirelessCommType 5
# Through the intro to the action menu (POK-269's rule: tap, never hold -- a held
# button is one press).
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 200
# gBattleCommunication[0] is 2 (STATE_WAIT_ACTION_CHOSEN) once the engine is actually
# waiting on this battler's choice -- press before that and the taps go nowhere.
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
shot menu
# FIGHT is on the left, BAG on the right.
tap RIGHT
wait 30
tap A
wait 120
# 3 is STATE_WAIT_ACTION_CASE_CHOSEN: the choice was taken and the bag is opening.
# 6 (STATE_SELECTION_SCRIPT) is where the engine parks a battler it has just told
# "Items can't be used now.", which is what this used to be.
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 3
# The frame shows the ITEMS pocket over the battle. It is empty, because the driver's
# boot block hands out no bag -- what is on trial here is the door, not the stock.
shot bag
say pvp bag ok
