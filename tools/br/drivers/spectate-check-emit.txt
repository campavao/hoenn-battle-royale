# spectate-check-emit: a fighter sends its sum for each turn (POK-376) in a TURN of its
# own as the turn ends: C0 03, the ended turn's number, the sum low byte first. It goes
# before the next turn is chosen, so a watcher knows the fighter is a turn ahead (HBR-63).
# spectate-turn.txt's loopback battle, two turns: the first turn's actions, turn 1's sum
# alone, then the second turn's actions.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 05 07 04 00 00 00 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 900
expect u8 gBrSpectate+4 1
tap A
wait 40
tap A
wait 200
expect u16 gBrSpectate 1
expect u16 gBrSpectate+2 10
wait 900
expect u8 gBattleResults+0x13 1
# turn 1 ended with both mons species 0x115, at 16 HP each (the walk-up's shorter "!",
# POK-444, starts the fight on another frame, so other rolls)
expect u16 gBattleMons 0x115
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x28 16
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x58 0x115
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x80 16
expect u16 gBrSpectate 2
expect u16 gBrSpectate+2 10
expectmsg 19 u8 2 0xC0
expectmsg 19 u8 3 3
expectmsg 19 u8 4 1
# 2 * 0x115 * 31 + 16 + 16
expectmsg 19 u16 5 0x4336
tap A
wait 40
tap A
wait 200
expect u16 gBrSpectate 3
# action bytes only: the check is not counted with them
expectge u16 gBrSpectate+2 20
expectle u16 gBrSpectate+2 22
shot turn2
say spectate check emit ok
