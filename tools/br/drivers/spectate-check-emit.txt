# spectate-check-emit: a fighter sends its sum for each turn (POK-376), after the next
# turn's actions in that TURN: C0 03, the ended turn's number, the sum low byte first.
# spectate-turn.txt's loopback battle, two turns. The first TURN has nothing after its
# actions -- no turn had ended -- and the second ends with turn 1's sum.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 05 07 04 00 00 00 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 900
expect u8 gBrSpectate+4 1
tap A
wait 40
tap A
wait 200
expect u16 gBrSpectate 1
expect u16 gBrSpectate+2 10
wait 900
expect u8 gBattleResults+0x13 1
# turn 1 ended with both mons species 0x115, at 11 and 16 HP
expect u16 gBattleMons 0x115
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x28 11
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x58 0x115
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x80 16
tap A
wait 40
tap A
wait 200
expect u16 gBrSpectate 2
# action bytes only: the check is not counted with them
expectge u16 gBrSpectate+2 20
expectle u16 gBrSpectate+2 22
expectmsg 19 u8 13 0xC0
expectmsg 19 u8 14 3
expectmsg 19 u8 15 1
# 2 * 0x115 * 31 + 11 + 16
expectmsg 19 u16 16 0x4331
shot turn2
say spectate check emit ok
