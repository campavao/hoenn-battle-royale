# hold-give: an item with a hold effect off the ground asks who should hold it (Cam,
# 2026-10-27: "We should prompt to give an item to a pokemon when picked up (only items
# that have hold effects)"). br_loot.c's Take arms BrMoves_HoldSoon, and once the item is
# in the bag BrMoves_Tick opens the bag's own GIVE screen; giving it goes back to the
# field with it held.
#
# A LUCKY PUNCH (0xDE) as an item ball on the cell we face, as tm-teach puts its TM.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0
pokebytes gBrLoot 01 00 0C 00 10 00 DE 00 00 09 00 03 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
wait 60
tap A
wait 150
shot give
# taken, in the bag, and "Give to which POKeMON?" is up on its own
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 1
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0xDE
expectne u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
# TREECKO, then A past "TREECKO was given the LUCKY PUNCH to hold."
tap A
wait 90
shot given
tap A
wait 120
# out of the bag, and back on the field with the controls ours
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
shot field
hold RIGHT 20
wait 30
expectne u16 gBrOwnPos+2 12
say hold give ok
