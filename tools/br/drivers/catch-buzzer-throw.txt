# catch-buzzer-throw: the buzzer waits for a ball already in the air (POK-308).
#
# Cam threw a SAFARI BALL as the clock hit 0:01 and the wild mon's health box came
# apart: the bar's own sprite left alive beside the ball with its frame gone. The forced
# exit skips the text waits (POK-261, so a Safari battle does not sit on one line with
# the fog closing) -- and skipping them WHILE THE THROW IS STILL PLAYING runs the
# battle's ending through the middle of the animation that tears the box down.
#
# Cam's own call on the scope: "it's fine for the Safari one to end when the timer is
# done, but the UI piece is really kind of the weird part." So it still ends; it just
# does not end mid-throw.
#
# catch-buzzer.txt is the buzzer arriving between throws, which is the case POK-261 was
# about. This one drops the clock to zero WHILE a ball is in the air.
# freeze -- "we both just disappeared. And then I was frozen here"). Cam's catch was at
# 2:50 of a match whose opening was over by then, so the ring was already up: the
# battle ends into BrMatch_SafariOver, which ends the opening and puts the drop map in
# front of you. What this asks is whether any of that happens at all, or whether the
# battle's own ending leaves the game somewhere with nothing to press.
#
# catch-pages.txt is the same catch without the ring. Everything down to the fourth
# throw is its script, so the two only differ in what is true outside the battle.
#
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x00000C00
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 02 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gBrMatch 1
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 0
expect u8 gBrMatch+1 0
poke u8 gBrMatch+1 1
poke u16 gBrMatch+12 240
hold DOWN 140
hold LEFT 100
hold RIGHT 70
hold LEFT 70
hold RIGHT 70
hold LEFT 70
wait 40
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
# The ball goes up...
tap A
wait 20
shot in-the-air
# ...and the clock runs out under it. clockLeft is gBrMatch+12.
poke u16 gBrMatch+12 0
wait 60
shot mid-throw
wait 120
shot after-throw
wait 240
# Both boxes are gone rather than half-drawn -- the wild mon's and the SAFARI BALLS
# counter. This is the frame Cam's bug was in.
shot boxes-gone
wait 600
# ...and the buzzer still closed it, with nothing pressed: phase 2 is BR_PHASE_DROP.
# It takes longer than an idle buzzer does, because the throw plays out first.
expectge u8 gBrMatch 2
say catch buzzer throw ok
