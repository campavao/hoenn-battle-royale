# legend-catch: only a catch keeps a legendary (POK-410, br_legend.c). legend-battle.txt
# is the run, which puts it back; this is a MASTER BALL thrown at it from the phone's bag
# (bag-pick-ball.txt's way in): it joins the party, and nothing goes back on the ground --
# its event's mark stays gray.
#
# Event 0's LATIAS (407) under key 0xB000 at (12,17), the cell the boot leaves us facing.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x1234
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 20 08 05 00 00 00 00 1F 00 B0
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 0B 11 0E 00 00 01 00 09 01 00 B0 0C 00 11 00 97 01 14 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 20
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 1
# OBJ_EVENT_GFX_LATIAS
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x4D 187
shot standing
tap A
waitfor u32 gMain+4 BattleMainCB2+1 600
# BattleSetup_StartLatiBattle: BATTLE_TYPE_LEGENDARY, not a REGI's
expectbits u32 gBattleTypeFlags 0x6000 0x2000
expect u8 gBrEvents+17 1
wait 300
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x58 407
tap B
wait 300
tap B
wait 300
# MASTER BALL x1 in POKe BALLS (SaveBlock1 +0x650), the key zeroed, then what the page
# does: BALLS_POCKET, MASTER BALL, BAG
poke u32 *gSaveBlock2Ptr+0xAC 0
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x650 1
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x652 1
poke u8 gBagPosition+5 1
poke u16 gBrBag 1
drain gBrMailbox
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap A
wait 600
shot caught
tap B
wait 120
tap B
wait 120
tap B
wait 120
tap B
wait 120
waitfor u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1 900
wait 60
shot back
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 2
# the mark stays gray, and nothing was put back: no row holds it, nothing stands here
expect u8 gBrEvents+17 1
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 0
expect u8 gBrLoot+0x0B 0
say legend catch ok
