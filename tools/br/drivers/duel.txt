# duel: two bots meeting is a real battle, not a coin flip (POK-238). The host's tab
# boots a second, hidden copy of this ROM and hands it two parties; this driver is that
# instance. Side A goes into gPlayerParty, side B into gEnemyParty, both sides are
# played by the AI (battle_controllers.c), and the fight is entered exactly the way a
# bot fight is -- the earlier attempt at this ticket wrote its own entry and stalled on
# the intro for ever.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# DUEL: seat 1 vs seat 2, one mon each -- a level 5 TREKO and a level 5 MUDKI, both
# with POUND, so the fight is short and somebody has to lose it.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 02 01 01 15 01 05 13 00 13 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 CE CC BF C5 C9 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 1B 01 05 14 00 14 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 C7 CF BE C5 C3 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 27 gDecompressionBuffer 204
wait 10
# both parties are in, and this ROM knows whose they are
expect u8 gBrDuel 1
expect u8 gBrDuel+2 1
expect u8 gBrDuel+3 2
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
# the tick starts it on the next overworld frame
wait 300
expect u8 gBrDuel+1 1
shot duel-intro
