# duel: two bots meeting is a real battle, not a coin flip (POK-238). The host's tab
# boots a second, hidden copy of this ROM and hands it two parties; this driver is that
# instance. Side A goes into gPlayerParty, side B into gEnemyParty, both sides are
# played by the AI (battle_controllers.c), and the fight is entered exactly the way a
# bot fight is -- the earlier attempt at this ticket wrote its own entry and stalled on
# the intro for ever.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# DUEL: seat 1 vs seat 2, one mon each -- a level 5 TREKO and a level 5 MUDKI, both
# with POUND, so the fight is short and somebody has to lose it -- and a bag each
# (POK-237): an X ATTACK for A, a POTION for B. Emerald keeps one trainer's worth of
# items, so the ROM swaps the right list in before each side decides.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 02 01 01 15 01 05 13 00 13 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 CE CC BF C5 C9 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 1B 01 05 14 00 14 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 C7 CF BE C5 C3 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 01 4B 00 01 0D 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 27 gDecompressionBuffer 210
wait 10
# both parties are in, and this ROM knows whose they are
expect u8 gBrDuel 1
expect u8 gBrDuel+2 1
expect u8 gBrDuel+3 2
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
# both bags read off the tail, nothing spent yet
expect u8 gBrDuel+7 1
expect u8 gBrDuel+8 1
expect u16 gBrDuel+0xC 0x4B
expect u16 gBrDuel+0x14 0x0D
expect u8 gBrDuel+9 0
# the tick starts it on the next overworld frame
wait 300
expect u8 gBrDuel+1 1
# Pin the dice. Two level-5 mon with POUND is a coin flip, and until POK-298 the coin was
# being tossed by the overworld: an NPC with a random movement type calls Random() every
# few frames, so how many people were standing on Littleroot decided who won this fight.
# Hiding the story's NPCs changed the count and flipped it. Seeded here rather than at the
# mailsend because a battle has no wandering NPCs in it, so from this frame on the run is
# a pure function of the seed. 3 is the smallest one where B takes it.
poke u32 gRngValue 3
shot duel-intro

expect u8 gBrDuel+1 0
# ...with an outcome, which is what rides home as BR_MSG_DRESULT: 2 is B_OUTCOME_LOST,
# meaning the party in gEnemyParty -- side B, the MUDKI -- took it. Deterministic off the
# seed above; without it this line is a coin flip dressed as an assertion.
expect u8 gBattleOutcome 2
# and the proxy did NOT treat its own side losing as going out of a match
expect u8 gBrMatch 2
# A's X ATTACK went in on the first turn -- an X item is a first-turn item to Emerald's
# AI -- and that is what rides home in the DRESULT for the page to take off its bag.
expect u8 gBrDuel+9 1
