# moves-spend: a TM taught from the MOVES list is spent; an HM is not (POK-279).
#
# Emerald's own rule, party_menu.c's Task_LearnedMove: `if (item < ITEM_HM01)
# RemoveBagItem(item, 1)`. Kanto's too (lib/moves.lua -- "a TM is spent by teaching; an
# HM is a tool, not a consumable"). The relearner had no idea machines existed, so the
# rule had to be carried in with them.
#
# A TM06 TOXIC is put on the ground under our feet rather than walked to: the Zone's
# balls are one per area and the nearest is twenty tiles from any landing cell.
#
# Loot coordinates are the outer space ObjectEvent.currentCoords uses -- map data plus
# MAP_OFFSET -- and the boot's own warp lands a tile south of the cell it names, the way
# stepping out of a door does. So the block says Littleroot (5,8), the player stands on
# (5,9), and that is (12,16) here. gBrOwnPos is where to read it back.
#
# The bag is read straight out of SaveBlock1: the TM pocket is at +0x690, four bytes a
# slot, and an ItemSlot's id is plain (only the quantity is scrambled). GiveTheRunOfHoenn
# deals HM01..HM08 into slots 0..7, so a picked-up TM lands in slot 8 at +0x6B0.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 5
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x690 339
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B0 0
# key 1, x 12, y 16, item 294 (TM06 TOXIC), map 0:9, level 0, kind 3 (BR_LOOT_ITEM),
# objId BR_NO_OBJ, then pad and money
pokebytes gBrLoot 01 00 0C 00 10 00 26 01 00 09 00 03 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
wait 60
tap A
wait 90
shot took
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 1
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B0 294
# MOVES on the party screen
tap START
wait 40
tap A
wait 80
tap A
wait 40
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap A
wait 240
shot offered
# CUT, STRENGTH, FLASH, ROCK SMASH, TOXIC, CANCEL -- four down to the machine that is
# a TM rather than an HM
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap DOWN
wait 20
shot onit
tap A
wait 120
tap A
wait 240
shot asked
tap A
wait 240
tap A
wait 180
shot learned
# The TM is gone from the pocket and every HM is still in it.
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B0 0
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x690 339
say moves spend ok
