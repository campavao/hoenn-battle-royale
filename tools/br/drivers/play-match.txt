# play-match: web/e2e/play.spec.ts's match on the ROM alone (POK-327) -- the Safari
# opening from a seeded boot, a wild battle and a ball thrown, the drop map and a pick,
# the landing, YOUNGSTER CALVIN talked to and beaten, the fog's bleed, and the OUT. The
# drivers that pin each beat on its own (catch-pages, safari-drop, trainer-drop,
# ring-outside, gym-out) never chained them; this does, with play.spec's own numbers, so
# the page's spec and this one are the same match, one with a screen and one without.
#
# What the page does here is done by hand: the seed goes into gBrMatch before the boot
# (app.ts, solo), and START, CLOCK, LAND and RING are written into the mailbox as the
# director would. Every fight is called the way the drivers call theirs. A shot a step.
#
# SEED 5376 deals seat 0 cell 21 of br_match.c's sSafariCells: SAFARI ZONE SOUTHEAST
# (15,14), tall grass. `#safari=60&fog=20`. `#land=MAP_ROUTE102,36,16`: three tiles right
# of CALVIN's column (data/maps/Route102/map.json, (33,14), local id 2).
