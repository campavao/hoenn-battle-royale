# wanderer-kept: a wanderer the ROM lets go comes back where it was, not at home
# (2026-10-07 play-test: "npcs flashing on the screen when moving around").
#
# pret despawns a person once both its tile and its home are outside a box that reaches
# ~40px above and ~72px below the LCD -- inside the picture the page shows on a phone. The
# page then draws it from its template, at home, up to a tile or two from where it stood,
# and the ROM respawns it there too: a jump on screen. br_field.c BrField_KeepWhereLeft
# moves the template to the tile it was let go on, so both pick it up where it was.
#
# Booted in SLATEPORT (map 0:1) at (16,44), two tiles north of local id 15, a
# WANDER_AROUND man at home (16,46). Templates are SaveBlock1 + 0xC70, 0x18 each, x/y at +4:
# id 15 is the 15th, at +0xDC0. The run is deterministic: he wanders to (17,45).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 01 10 00 2C 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 1
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0xDC0 15
# In view: his template is still home.
expect u32 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0xDC4 0x002E0010
wait 900
shot wandered
# Walk north until he is let go.
hold UP 200
wait 30
expectle u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 30
shot left-behind
# His template is where he stood, not (16,46).
expect u32 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0xDC4 0x002D0011
# Back down: he is made again where he stood, and still wanders round his home
# (BrField_KeepHome), not round that spot -- his range never walks off. Object slot 5
# (0x24 each): local id at +8, initialCoords at +0xC, currentCoords at +0x10, both
# MAP_OFFSET (7) in.
hold DOWN 120
wait 2
shot back
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0xBC 15
expect u32 gObjectEvents+0xC0 0x00350017
expect u32 gObjectEvents+0xC4 0x00340018
say wanderer kept ok
