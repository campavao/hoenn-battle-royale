# drop-sea-tie: on the drop, a cell between two equally near sections keeps the name
# already in the box (POK-352), so it does not flip back and forth as the cursor
# passes. West from SAFARI ZONE (16,2), (12,2) is one from ROUTE 119 (11,2) and one from
# ROUTE 120 (13,2); the cursor comes from ROUTE 120, and row order alone would say 119.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 82 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0E 15 12 00 00 78 56 34 12 01 78 00 78 00 00 00 00 19 0A 00 28 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 4
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 0D 06 03 00 00 00 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 200
expect u8 gBrPick 1
tap LEFT
wait 30
tap LEFT
wait 30
tap LEFT
wait 30
tap LEFT
wait 30
shot route-120-held
tap A
wait 60
# MAPSEC_ROUTE_120
expect u16 gBrPick+12 35
expect u8 gBrPick 1
say drop sea tie ok
