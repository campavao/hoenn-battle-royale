# hof-name: a Hall of Fame card names a mon once (HBR-52). Emerald's line is
# "NICKNAME/SPECIES" with the gender glued on, so the 10-20 play-test's champion read
# "FERALIGATR/FERALIGATR" and a glyph that looked like "$". hof-watcher's parade with
# the second mon named after its species: mon-1.png reads "TREECKO" and the glyph a
# space off, no "/TREECKO". The name is checked by that frame alone: no byte holds the
# printed line. The expects are hof-watcher's: the parade still runs.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# seat 1 placed two tiles east of us, wearing skin 4 (HIKER)
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 0E 00 0F 00 03 01 04
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 10
expect u8 gBrSeats+17 4
# their party: two mons -- species 0x0115 Lv12 named TREKO, and the same at Lv14
# named ZIGGY, a different personality each (+28) so the genders differ
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 02 15 01 0C 22 00 38 00 00 01 00 23 00 47 00 14 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 CE CC BF C5 C9
copy gDecompressionBuffer+102 gDecompressionBuffer+2 100
poke u8 gDecompressionBuffer+104 14
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer+130 01 00 00 00
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer+132 00 00 00 00
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer+138 07 CE CC BF BF BD C5 C9
mailsend gBrMailbox 7 gDecompressionBuffer 202
wait 10
# FAME: seat 1, named STEVEN -- in a match (a FAME in the lobby is last match's, dropped)
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer+0x400 01 06 CD CE BF D0 BF C8
mailsend gBrMailbox 33 gDecompressionBuffer+0x400 8
waitfor u8 gBrMatch 4 120
wait 330
shot mon-1
# The un-nicknamed TREECKO first, in its own colours: a row without BR_MON_SHINY is never shiny (HBR-22, the
# play-test's parade was all shiny). OBJ palette 1 is its pic's; colour 4 is 0x29BF shiny.
expect u16 0x05000228 0x42E9
wait 200
shot mon-2
wait 400
shot team
waitfor u8 gBrMatch 4 1
wait 200
shot champion
# ...and no A: a watcher's last screen moves on by itself (BrHof_LetGo), four seconds in
waitfor u8 gBrMatch 5 600
wait 120
shot after
expect u8 gBrMatch 5
# and it is let go of: a second FAME after the parade would parade again, not crash
say hof name ok
