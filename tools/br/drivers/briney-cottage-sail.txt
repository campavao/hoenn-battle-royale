# briney-cottage-sail: from his cottage MR. BRINEY sails to DEWFORD, with no call on the way
# (2026-10-07 review of the play-test fix).
#
# The cottage's sail is Route 104's (data/maps/Route104/scripts.inc), which rings DAD on
# the POKENAV first unless FLAG_ENABLE_NORMAN_MATCH_CALL is set: ten seconds of a match on
# a call. The boot sets it. Booted in the cottage (17:0) at (5,5) as briney-cottage is;
# the hide flags are SaveBlock1 +0x1270 (see briney-ferry for the bits).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 11 00 05 00 05 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 17
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 0
# FLAG_ENABLE_NORMAN_MATCH_CALL, 0x132: bit 2 of +0x1296.
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1296 0x04 0x04
hold UP 16
wait 20
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 60
shot where-bound
# The cursor starts on EXIT; up to DEWFORD.
tap UP
wait 10
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
shot after-a
wait 600
shot boarding
wait 1200
shot ashore-dewford
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
shot landed
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 11
expectne u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x14C8 0
# At Dewford: its man and boat shown, the cottage's and Route 109's hidden.
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x12CC 0xF8 0x68
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x12CD 0x01 0x01
say briney cottage sail ok
