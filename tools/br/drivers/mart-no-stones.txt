# mart-no-stones: an ordinary Poke Mart sells no evolution stones, at any rung
# (POK-309). The control for mart-stones.txt -- read the two frames together, because a
# screenshot of a shelf without stones on it proves nothing on its own.
#
# Oldale Town's Mart is map 2:4 and its clerk stands at (1,3), so boot on (1,4). The
# ring is pushed to phase 5 first, which is the top of the ladder and the shelf that
# used to carry all six stones.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 02 04 01 00 04 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 700
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 2
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 4
poke u8 gBrRing+1 5
poke u8 gBrRing 1
wait 120
# the top rung, so this is sMart4 -- the shelf with the MASTER BALL on it
expect u8 gBrLevels+2 4
hold UP 10
wait 20
tap A
wait 120
tap A
wait 150
tap A
wait 90
shot list-top
hold DOWN 150
wait 40
# The whole shelf, and the bottom of it is the POKe DOLL and CANCEL rather than six
# stones. The doll is on EVERY shelf including the first (POK-293): it is the only way
# out of a fight you do not want, and an escape you can only buy in one town is an
# escape most matches never have.
shot list-bottom
# One up from CANCEL is the doll. Buy it and find it in the bag: 80 is ITEM_POKE_DOLL,
# and SaveBlock1 +0x560 is the first ITEMS slot with the id in the clear.
tap UP
wait 20
shot on-doll
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 120
tap A
wait 150
tap A
wait 60
tap B
wait 60
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 80
say mart shelf ok
