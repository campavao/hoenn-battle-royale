# moves-bag: MOVES on a mon with nothing to remember says where the moves ARE (POK-279).
#
# Play-test: "I had a Wingull and when I clicked on MOVES from the Party screen, it
# kicked me out and said there were no moves. If I went to my bag manually though and
# used FLY, it was available to teach." Both true -- the relearner only ever offers
# level-up moves, and a machine is taught from the bag -- and nothing on screen joined
# them up.
#
# moves-row.txt is the same row on a mon that HAS something to remember: a Treecko at
# the L30 rung. This one leaves it at L5, where the relearner has nothing, and the bag
# has the HMs every match is dealt (GiveTheRunOfHoenn).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 5
tap START
wait 40
tap A
wait 80
tap A
wait 40
shot menu
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap A
wait 120
# The relearner never opened -- there was nothing to open it for -- and the ticker is
# holding the line that says so.
shot said
expect u8 gBrCatch+2 0xFF
say moves bag ok
