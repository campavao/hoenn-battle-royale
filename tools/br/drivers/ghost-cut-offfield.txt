# ghost-cut-offfield: leaving the field in the middle of a ghost's cut (reviewer, PR #12).
# The cut's bits now live through a screen off the field (a battle, the bag): a battle
# keeps the objects, and a slot SweepCutting finds no longer cutting only loses its bit.
# Either way the tree must come down once we are back, and the ghost walk on -- not an
# invisible tree left solid with every ghost held at it.
#
# ghost-cut.txt's tree, (19,11) on Petalburg Woods (FLAG_TEMP_12). A battle comes too
# slowly to land mid-cut (its intro plays on the field); the bag leaves within ~20 frames.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 18 0B 13 00 0E 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# seat 1 one tile south of the tree, facing it (outer coords), then its step into it
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 01 18 0B 1A 00 13 00 02 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
wait 20
expectne u8 gBrSeats+25 0xFF
# START open first, so its first entry (a screen of its own) is one press away; the
# cut is about 40 frames and the fade off the field about 20
tap START
wait 10
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 02 1A 00 12 00 18 0B
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 8
wait 2
expect u8 gBrSeats+26 1
tap A
wait 60
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1272 0x04 0x00
shot off-field
tap B
wait 60
tap B
wait 120
shot back
# back on the field: the cut played again and the tree is gone, the ghost through
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1272 0x04 0x04
expect u8 gBrSeats+26 0
