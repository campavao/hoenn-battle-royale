# bag-items: a bag hands over its contents, not just its cash (POK-280).
#
# Kanto's rule (README, "Knock someone out and their BAG hits the ground where they
# fell"): items AND money, the whole bag in one press. Ours gave the cash and left the
# items lying there -- ParseSpill skips the item rows on purpose, because the ROM's loot
# table has room for eight pieces on a map and none at all for what is inside one.
#
# So the page keeps them, as it has for the whole match (match/loot.ts), and gives them
# over when it sees our own seat take the bag. This is the ROM's half of that: BR_MSG_GIVE
# arrives and the items are in the bag.
#
# The bag is read straight out of SaveBlock1 -- the ITEMS pocket at +0x560, the TM pocket
# at +0x690, four bytes a slot, ids in the clear (only the quantity is scrambled).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# nothing in the ITEMS pocket yet; the eight HMs fill TM slots 0..7, so 8 is free
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x690 339
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B0 0
# a bag on our own tile: key 1, (12,16), species 0, map 0:9, level 0, kind 2
# (BR_LOOT_BAG), objId BR_NO_OBJ, pad, then money 1200 (0x4B0)
pokebytes gBrLoot 01 00 0C 00 10 00 00 00 00 09 00 02 FF 00 00 00 B0 04 00 00
wait 60
tap A
wait 20
# "FOUND 1200!" -- the bag's money, which read FOUND 0 on every bag in the game until
# 017142fc9. The box lives ninety frames, hence the short wait.
shot found
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 1
# GIVE: type 30 (0x1E), framed len 10 = hdr(07 00 00) + count 02, then
# (POTION 0x000D x2), (TM06 TOXIC 0x0126 x1)
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 1E 0A 07 00 00 02 0D 00 02 26 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
# both arrived, in their own pockets
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 13
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B0 294
say bag items ok
