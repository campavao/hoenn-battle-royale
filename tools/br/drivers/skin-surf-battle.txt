# skin-surf-battle: a battle on the water leaves us surfing, and the shore lets us off.
# Cam's 2026-10-20 report: "if you exit a battle on water, you can't exit the water to
# go back on land". The way back from a battle rebuilds the avatar's state from its
# sprite (SetPlayerAvatarObjectEventIdAndObjectId), and with an NPC look every state
# wears the one walking sprite, so it came back ON_FOOT on the sea: no blob, and the
# shore an elevation it could not climb.
#
# skin-field's spot and look: ROUTE 104 (map 0:19) at (33,22), the sea north of
# (36,22), a PICNICKER with the boot's test mon. win-wild-fight's patch, moved onto
# the water at (36,20). Boot flag 0x20 (TESTSWIM): a MUDKIP, so the party could SURF (#7).
wait 30
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrMySkin 7
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 A1 01 00 13 21 00 16 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 19
expect u8 gObjectEvents+5 32
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x496 259
tap SELECT
wait 60
# The cut tree at (35,22) is back (#7): the TREECKO cuts it on the way, and we go on
hold RIGHT 40
wait 150
hold RIGHT 24
wait 30
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0 36
hold UP 24
wait 150
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 21
expectbits u8 gPlayerAvatar 0x0A 0x08
shot surfing
# one patch, one tile further out (coordinates carry the map's +7)
pokebytes gBrPatch+16 2B 00
pokebytes gBrPatch+32 1B 00
poke u8 gBrPatch+4 1
hold UP 24
wait 600

wait 300
tap B
wait 300
