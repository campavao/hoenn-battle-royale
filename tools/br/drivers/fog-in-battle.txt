# fog-in-battle: a wild fight is not somewhere to hide from the fog (POK-262). Kanto's
# rule, v0.3.1. Without it you step outside the ring, pick a fight with the grass, and
# the clock stops mattering.
#
# The ring is turned on AFTER the encounter starts on purpose: the bleed counter is
# cumulative, so a ring that was already up while we walked would have spent ticks
# before the battle and "did it grow" would prove nothing.
wait 30
# BR_BOOT_MAP | TESTMON, male, Route 102 (0, 17), x=20 y=9 -- the middle grass patch
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
# walk the patch until something jumps out
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
wait 200
shot wild
# in a battle, and it is a wild one: BATTLE_TYPE_IS_MASTER only, no TRAINER (0x8)
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 4
# nothing has bled yet
expect u16 gBrRing+10 0
# now the fog closes somewhere else: RING phase 1, centre (25,12), r 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 01 19 0C 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 400
# it reached in
expectge u16 gBrRing+10 1
# but it did not end anybody mid-turn: still fighting, still in
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
expect u8 gBrRing+7 0
say fog in battle ok
