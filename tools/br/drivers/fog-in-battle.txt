# fog-in-battle: a wild fight is not somewhere to hide from the fog (POK-262). Kanto's
# rule, v0.3.1. Without it you step outside the ring, pick a fight with the grass, and
# the clock stops mattering.
#
# Since wild fights are published for spectators (2026-10-05 play-test), the fog in one
# is the engine's, at the end of each turn, as in a fight between contestants
# (fog-in-duel.txt): a replay is built from the fight's seed and its choices, and a bleed
# on the field's clock is neither, so the replay's mon stood at an HP the fighter's never
# had. Decided as the fight starts, so the ring closes first here and then we walk.
wait 30
# BR_BOOT_MAP | TESTMON, male, Route 102 (0, 17), x=20 y=9 -- the middle grass patch
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
# the fog closes somewhere else: RING phase 1, centre (25,12), r 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 01 19 0C 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 60
expect u8 gBrRing+5 1
# walk the patch until something jumps out
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
wait 200
shot wild
# in a battle, and it is a wild one: BATTLE_TYPE_IS_MASTER only, no TRAINER (0x8)
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 4
# and it knows it is in the fog
expect u8 gBrRing+31 1
# the field's clock has stopped: what bleeds from here is the engine's turn
poke u16 gBrRing+10 0
wait 300
expect u16 gBrRing+10 0
# one turn: past "Wild POOCHYENA appeared!" and "Go! TREECKO!", then FIGHT, the first move
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 300
shot menu
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 1500
shot fog-turn
# it reached in, and ended nobody
expectge u16 gBrRing+10 1
expect u8 gBrRing+7 0
say fog in battle ok
