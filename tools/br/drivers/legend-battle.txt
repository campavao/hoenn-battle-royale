# legend-battle: a legendary the match put down stands as its own overworld sprite and is
# fought, not picked up (POK-410, br_legend.c). Cam, 2026-10-18: "getting legendaries is a
# bit too easy ... you have to initiate a battle with them".
#
# Event 0 drops REGIROCK (401) under its legendary key 0xB000 one cell below the boot
# cell, which we face. It stands as OBJ_EVENT_GFX_REGIROCK (200), not an item ball (59).
# A is its cry and a REGI battle; the battle takes it off the ground for the room (the
# PICKUP) and grays the event's mark. Running puts it back where it stood, for everybody
# (a SPILL under the same key), and the mark is live again.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x1234
# EVENT 0 at ROUTE 116, legendary 0xB000; then its SPILL: seat 1, map 0:9, one mon, key
# 0xB000 at (12,17), the cell we face (the boot leaves us on (12,16) facing down), species 0x0191 at 20, no bag
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 20 08 05 00 00 00 00 1F 00 B0
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 0B 11 0E 00 00 01 00 09 01 00 B0 0C 00 11 00 91 01 14 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 20
# two rows: it, and the DAY CARE's chest on its own map (0x8E00, daycare-chest.txt)
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 2
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 1
expect u8 gBrEvents+17 0
# slot 2 (slot 1 is the map's own person): local id 0xC0, graphics 200, facing down
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x50 0xC0
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x4D 200
shot standing
drain gBrMailbox
tap A
wait 30
# still on the field for its cry: nothing has left the ground yet
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 2
waitfor u32 gMain+4 BattleMainCB2+1 600
# a REGI battle (BATTLE_TYPE_LEGENDARY | BATTLE_TYPE_REGI) against REGIROCK
expectbits u32 gBattleTypeFlags 0x6000 0x6000
# (row 0's kind: BR_LOOT_NONE; the count is only kept on the field)
expect u8 gBrLoot+0x0B 0
expectmsg 10 u16 1 0xB000
expect u8 gBrEvents+17 1
wait 300
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x58 401
shot battle
# out as a run would be (B_OUTCOME_RAN), the way ghost-loot-in-battle.txt ends a fight,
# on B: back on the field, A would be a second challenge of the one put back in front of us
tap A
wait 70
tap A
wait 70
tap A
wait 70
poke u8 gBattleOutcome 4
tap A
wait 70
poke u8 gBattleOutcome 4
tap A
wait 70
poke u8 gBattleOutcome 4
tap B
wait 70
poke u8 gBattleOutcome 4
tap B
wait 70
poke u8 gBattleOutcome 4
tap B
wait 70
poke u8 gBattleOutcome 4
tap B
wait 70
tap B
wait 70
tap B
wait 70
tap B
wait 70
tap B
wait 70
tap B
wait 70
tap B
wait 70
waitfor u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1 600
wait 60
# back where it stood, for everybody: our SPILL of key 0xB000 at (12,17), REGIROCK
expectmsg 11 u16 4 0xB000
expectmsg 11 u16 6 0x0C
expectmsg 11 u16 8 0x11
expectmsg 11 u16 10 401
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 2
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 1
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x4D 200
expect u8 gBrEvents+17 0
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
shot back
say legend battle ok
