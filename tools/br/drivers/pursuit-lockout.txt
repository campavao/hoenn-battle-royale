# pursuit-lockout: after we flee a seat, we do not turn and re-engage it (POK-231).
# A ghost is placed in our eyeline while seat 1 is our fledFrom: no challenge goes out.
# Once the lockout runs down, the same eyeline challenge fires -- the pursuer is fair
# game again (and until then it is free to keep chasing us).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# the eyeline only fires in a started match (gBrMatch.phase = BR_PHASE_PLAY)
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
# we fled seat 1: hold it off
poke u8 gBrEngage+6 1
poke u16 gBrEngage+8 200
# seat 1 at (15,16) facing WEST, in our eyeline (as in the engage driver)
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 0F 00 10 00 03 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 60
shot locked
expect u16 gBrEngage+4 0
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
# run the lockout down to zero; fledFrom clears and the eyeline challenge fires
poke u16 gBrEngage+8 3
wait 40
expect u8 gBrEngage+6 0xFF
expectge u16 gBrEngage+4 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
shot chased
say pursuit lockout ok
