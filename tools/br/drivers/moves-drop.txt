# moves-drop: a TM's move written over drops the TM back on the ground (Cam, 2026-10-13:
# "TMs drop back to the ground when the move is unlearned").
#
# TREECKO at 5 knows POUND and LEER. Four TMs go under our feet -- TOXIC, HIDDEN POWER,
# PROTECT, DOUBLE TEAM (294, 298, 305, 320), all four anybody can learn -- and one MOVES
# visit teaches them all: TOXIC and HIDDEN POWER into the empty slots, then PROTECT over
# TOXIC. TOXIC is not in TREECKO's learnset, so it could only have come from its TM, and
# the TM lands beside us as a one-item bag (BrLoot_DropItem): drawn as a ball, and named
# as the TM. Then DOUBLE TEAM over POUND, which TREECKO learns on its own: nothing drops,
# or every caught mon would be a TM machine.
#
# See moves-spend.txt for the loot and TM-pocket conventions, moves-stay.txt for the list
# handing itself back after a teach.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 5
pokebytes gBrLoot 01 00 0C 00 10 00 26 01 00 09 00 03 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
pokebytes gBrLoot+20 02 00 0C 00 10 00 2A 01 00 09 00 03 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
pokebytes gBrLoot+40 03 00 0C 00 10 00 31 01 00 09 00 03 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
pokebytes gBrLoot+60 04 00 0C 00 10 00 40 01 00 09 00 03 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
wait 60
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 90
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 4
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x690 294
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x694 298
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x698 305
# MOVES on the party screen: TOXIC, HIDDEN POWER, PROTECT, DOUBLE TEAM, CANCEL
tap START

wait 80
tap A
wait 40

wait 10

tap A

tap A

# TOXIC's TM is on the ground: the first free row, a bag (kind 2) holding just TM06, keyed
# in the released space under seat 0 (0x4000), one cell east of where we stand
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 2
