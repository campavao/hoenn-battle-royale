# moves-drop: a TM's move written over gives the TM back, into the bag (Cam, 2026-10-17:
# "make swapping moves among pokemon also easy"; it dropped on the ground before), so MOVES
# on a teammate offers it next.
#
# TREECKO at 5 knows POUND and LEER. Four TMs go under our feet -- TOXIC, HIDDEN POWER,
# PROTECT, DOUBLE TEAM (294, 298, 305, 320), all four anybody can learn -- and one MOVES
# visit teaches them all: TOXIC and HIDDEN POWER into the empty slots, then PROTECT over
# TOXIC. TOXIC is not in TREECKO's learnset, so it could only have come from its TM, and
# TM06 goes back in the bag, and nothing goes on the ground. Then DOUBLE TEAM over POUND,
# which TREECKO learns on its own: no TM for it, or every caught mon would be a TM machine.
#
# See moves-spend.txt for the loot and TM-pocket conventions, moves-stay.txt for the list
# handing itself back after a teach.
