# ring-rows: the ring's far row after a step down (POK-319, POK-329), the row half of
# ring-far-column.txt. The ring is 32 metatile rows, pos.y-4..pos.y+27, grid row pos.y+dy
# in tile rows (yTileOffset + 2*dy) & 63 of 64 (include/br/br_field.h, THE RING). A step
# down moves every row up one and leaves the new bottom row, pos.y+27, to be drawn into
# the slot the old top row had -- which is the new pos.y-5, the row the band's top shows
# until the step is done. So BrField_RedrawSlices only marks it and BrField_Tick draws it
# on the frame the step completes, not before (Cam's 2026-09-18 phone: "jittering on the
# top left, up, down, based on which way you're going").
#
# Rustboro City (21,25), landed, so the tile offsets are 0: the ring's top row, grid row
# 21 (map row 14), is in tile rows 56..57 -- BG2's tilemap (0x0600C800, the middle layer,
# 64 bytes a tile row) at 0x0600D600. One step down puts pos at (21,26) and the offset at
# 2, and (2 + 2*27) & 63 is 56 again: the far row, grid row 53 (map row 46), goes into
# that same slot. Tile columns 0..1 (u32 at +0) are the witness: 0xB33BB33B for the top
# row, 0x82E082E1 for the far one -- what a whole-map draw booted straight at (21,26) has
# at dy 27, tile rows 54..55 (0x0600D580), read off that boot. VRAM trails the buffers by
# a frame, so the mid-step read (the camera 12 pixels in) is the step's fifth frame.
# The parent's 256-row ring has no tile rows past 31: 0x0600D600 is not BG2's map at all.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 03 15 00 19 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
say rest
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 25
expect u32 0x0600D600 0xB33BB33B
expect u32 0x0600D640 0xB34BB34B
# Already facing south, so the step starts on the first frame DOWN is held.
hold DOWN 1
wait 4
say mid-step
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 26
expect u32 gFieldCamera+20 12
expect u32 0x0600D600 0xB33BB33B
wait 40
say after-a-step-down
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 26
expect u32 0x0600D600 0x82E082E1
expect u32 0x0600D640 0x82E082E2
say ok
