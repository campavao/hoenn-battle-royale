# ring-rows: the ring's sixteenth row after a step down (POK-319), the row half of
# ring-far-column.txt. The LCD sits at ring rows 5..24 at rest (BG VOFS 40), so the band
# past the picture shows rows 0..4 above it and 25..31 below, and every one of them has
# to hold the map. Emerald's slice redraw draws rows pos.y..pos.y+14 on a step and
# leaves the sixteenth stale; BrField_MarkFarRow notes it and BrField_Tick draws
# metatile row pos.y+15 into it on the frame the step completes, not before.
#
# From Littleroot (5,9) one step down puts pos at (5,10) with the ring's y tile offset
# at 2, so the sixteenth slot is tile rows (2 + 30) % 32 = 0..1 of BG2's tilemap
# (0x0600E000, the middle layer, 64 bytes a row). Tile columns 10..11 (u32 at +0x14)
# are the witness: plain path at rest (0x20032002), and 0x21FD21FC / 0x21FF21FE once the
# row is drawn -- what a whole-map draw booted straight at (5,10) has in tile rows
# 30..31 (u32 at 0x0600E794 and 0x0600E7D4), and not the plain path the stale slot held.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
say rest
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 9
expect u32 0x0600E014 0x20032002
expect u32 0x0600E054 0x20022003
# Already facing south, so the step starts at once and pos moves with its first frame.
# Four frames later the step is still under way and the band's top rows are still
# showing that slot: it must not have been drawn yet (Cam's 2026-09-18 phone jitter).
hold DOWN 1
wait 4
say mid-step
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 10
expect u32 0x0600E014 0x20032002
wait 40
say after-a-step-down
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 10
expect u32 0x0600E014 0x21FD21FC
expect u32 0x0600E054 0x21FF21FE
say ok
