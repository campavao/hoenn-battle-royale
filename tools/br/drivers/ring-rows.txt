# ring-rows: the ring's far row after a step down (POK-319, POK-329), the row half of
# ring-far-column.txt. The ring is 32 metatile rows, pos.y-9..pos.y+22 (BR_RING_ABOVE 9
# since POK-378), grid row pos.y+dy in tile rows (yTileOffset + 2*dy) & 63 of 64
# (include/br/br_field.h, THE RING). A step down moves every row up one and leaves the new
# bottom row, pos.y+22, to be drawn into the slot the old top row had -- which is the new
# pos.y-10, the row the band's top shows until the step is done. So BrField_RedrawSlices
# only marks it and BrField_Tick draws it on the frame the step completes, not before
# (Cam's 2026-09-18 phone: "jittering on the top left, up, down, based on which way you're
# going").
#
# Rustboro City (21,25), landed, so the tile offsets are 0: the ring's top row, grid row
# 16 (map row 9), is in tile rows 46..47 -- BG2's tilemap (0x0600C800, the middle layer,
# 64 bytes a tile row) at 0x0600D380. One step down puts pos at (21,26) and the offset at
# 2, and (2 + 2*22) & 63 is 46 again: the far row, grid row 48 (map row 41), goes into
# that same slot. Tile columns 0..1 (u32 at +0) are the witness: 0xB351B351 for the top
# row, 0x11161106 for the far one -- what a whole-map draw booted straight at (21,26) has
# at dy 22, tile rows 44..45 (0x0600D300), read off that boot. VRAM trails the buffers by
# a frame, so the mid-step read (the camera 12 pixels in) is the step's fifth frame.
# The parent's 256-row ring has no tile rows past 31: 0x0600D380 is not BG2's map at all.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 03 15 00 19 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
say rest
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 25
expect u32 0x0600D380 0xB351B351
expect u32 0x0600D3C0 0xB348B329
# Already facing south, so the step starts on the first frame DOWN is held.
hold DOWN 1
wait 4
say mid-step
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 26
expect u32 gFieldCamera+20 12
expect u32 0x0600D380 0xB351B351
wait 40
say after-a-step-down
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 26
expect u32 0x0600D380 0x11161106
expect u32 0x0600D3C0 0x02FA0EE8
say ok
