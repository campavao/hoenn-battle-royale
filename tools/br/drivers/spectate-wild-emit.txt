# spectate-wild-emit: a wild fight goes out to the room the way a link battle does
# (2026-10-05 play-test: "spectator mode can't see wild pokemon battles").
#
# Only a link battle's master and the proxy duel ever published; a fight the player's
# own ROM ran alone -- a wild POKeMON, a bot, a gym -- left whoever followed them looking
# at an empty field. Now it is published under the player's seat and BR_NO_SEAT (0xFF),
# with its own flags: no LINK, so the watcher replays a wild battle (spectate-wild.txt).
#
# The walk onto a patch is fog-in-battle.txt's.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
# BR_BOOT_MAP | TESTMON, male, Route 102 (0, 17), x=20 y=9 -- the middle grass patch
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
# a patch one step west, at (19,9): grass meets nothing on a step now (br_patch.h), and
# this boot has no match seed to deal the route's own
pokebytes gBrPatch+16 1A 00
pokebytes gBrPatch+32 10 00
poke u8 gBrPatch+4 1
tap LEFT
wait 4
tap LEFT
# the patch's battle opens: the transition and the mon sliding in
wait 600
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 4
# BSTART is out: seat 0 (this boot's) and nobody, the wild flags, the player's name and
# an empty one
expect u8 gBrSpectate+4 1
expectmsg 18 u16 0 0xFF00
expectmsg 18 u16 6 4
expectmsg 18 u8 10 0xBD
expectmsg 18 u8 18 0xFF
# FIGHT, the first move, and the turn goes out under the same id
hold A 4
wait 250
shot menu
tap A
wait 40
tap A
wait 400
shot turn
expectge u16 gBrSpectate 1
expectmsg 19 u16 0 0xFF00
say spectate wild emit ok
