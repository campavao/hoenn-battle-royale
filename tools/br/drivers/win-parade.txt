# Hall of Fame draws their parade from these rows, and with no personality and no OT ID
# on them every mon came out shiny (HBR-22). This TREECKO's personality is 0x0B00B17C,
# its OT ID 0xB2F90000: the visible half (0) goes, the hidden half stays home, and it is
# not shiny, so the flags are BR_MON_ROM_MOVES alone.

expectmsg 7 u8 57 2
