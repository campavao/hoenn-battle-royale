# national-dex: the routes draw on the whole dex (2026-10-05 play-test: "use the national
# dex to increase Pokemon variety"). Half of what a map's table picks is swapped, this
# match, for a species of its weight sharing one of its types (br_zone.c, BrZone_National).
#
# Route 102's grass under match seed 4: the WURMPLE the table picked is a KAKUNA (14)
# this match -- BUG, 205 to its 195. Read off the wild battler, gBattleMons[1].species.
#
# The walk used to be fog-in-battle.txt's, eight holds back and forth until something
# turned up, and which step that was rode on the RNG the VBlank turns every frame: any
# change that moved a lag frame (2026-10-06: the spectator camera, which runs nothing
# here) met a different mon or none. So the generator is set at the start of the walk
# that meets it: a walk right, out of the encounter immunity a map load gives, then
# gRngValue 3 and back left, the same KAKUNA on every build tried.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 4
hold RIGHT 60
poke u32 gRngValue 3
hold LEFT 60
wait 200
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 4
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x58 14
shot wild
say national dex ok
