# clock-in-box: under the page's windows (gBrHud.noBattleBox) the shot clock is in the message
# box, bottom right, in both menus (Cam, 11-04: "we've freed up the right-hand side"); with the
# ROM's own menus, at the top right as it was. It goes when the choice is made and leaves no
# hole in the box. BG0's tilemap is at screen block 24 (battle_bg.c), 32 cells a row: the cell
# at (25, 17) is 0x0600C472. The clock's first tile is CLK_BASEBLOCK, 0x110; B_WIN_MSG's there
# is 0x90 + 2 * 26 + 23 = 0xDB. At the top of the action menu's scroll, (26, 20) is 0x0600C534,
# in palette 5.

poke u8 gBrHud+0x17B 1

tap LEFT
