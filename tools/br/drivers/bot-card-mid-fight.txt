# bot-card-mid-fight: a bot's card that lands while a battle is running, or while a
# bot fight is fading in, is not staged (POK-330 #17).
#
# A card goes straight into gEnemyParty, and ParseTrainer zeroed and rebuilt it whenever
# one arrived. In a fight against another bot that is the enemy's team replaced under
# the battle, and gBrBotFight's seat, name and bag taken over by the newcomer, so the
# fight's report (party, spent, RESULT) goes out under the wrong bot. In a link battle it
# is the peer's team. SOLO never told its bots who was busy, so a second bot could stage
# one at any time; the page now does, and this is the ROM refusing it anyway.
#
# gEnemyParty+8 is the first mon's nickname, which is not encrypted: CreateMon names a
# card's mon after its species, so its first letter says which card built the party.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
#
# A link battle (netlink-loop.txt's, loopback on): gEnemyParty is the other side's team.
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 600
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
# Our own TREECKO, mirrored back as the peer's.
expect u8 gEnemyParty+8 0xCE
# MUDKIP (283 = 0x11B), from seat 30 (0x1E) calling itself RIV.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1E 03 CC C3 D0 01 1B 01 05 13 00 13 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 C7 CF BE C5 C3
mailsend gBrMailbox 23 gDecompressionBuffer 106
wait 10
expect u8 gEnemyParty+8 0xCE
expect u8 gBrBotFight+1 0
# Seat 1 goes out (out-peer.txt), which ends it.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 9 gDecompressionBuffer 1
wait 300
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
#
# A bot fight: bot-fight.txt's TREECKO card from seat 31, its ghost on our map, and its
# CHALLENGE.

mailsend gBrMailbox 23 gDecompressionBuffer 106
wait 10
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 01 00 09 14 00 08 00 01 01 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
# The MUDKIP card two frames behind it, while the fight is still fading in -- a second
# bot that spotted us on the same step (bot-two-challenges.txt). The staged party is
# already the fight's: nothing changes.
wait 2
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1E 03 CC C3 D0 01 1B 01 05 13 00 13 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 C7 CF BE C5 C3
