# bag-stale-pick: a pick whose bag never opened is let go at the next action menu
# (BrBag_Forget, from BrBattle_ShotReset). The page writes gBrBag as BAG goes down; if
# that bag never opens (the shot clock on the same frame, the battle over), a ball must
# not throw itself in the next bag the player opens by hand.
#
# The wild battle is run-wild-keeps-doll.txt's: ROUTE 102 under match seed 4.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 4
wait 30
tap LEFT
wait 4
tap LEFT
wait 250
tap LEFT
wait 250
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 4
wait 300
tap B
wait 300
tap B
wait 300
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
poke u32 *gSaveBlock2Ptr+0xAC 0
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x650 4
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x652 3
# a pick left waiting, then FIGHT and the first move instead of BAG
poke u8 gBagPosition+5 1
poke u16 gBrBag 4
tap A
wait 30
tap A
wait 900
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
expect u16 gBrBag 0
# BAG by hand: the bag stays open on the list, nothing thrown
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap A
wait 200
shot by-hand
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x652 3
say bag stale pick ok
