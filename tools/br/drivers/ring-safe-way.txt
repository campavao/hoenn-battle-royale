# ring-outside's start: Littleroot (section 4,11), a test Treecko, in a match.

# the count's turn is the first two seconds of every four (BR_HUD_ALT_FRAMES)

wait 30

poke u8 gBrHud+0x16 0
wait 4

pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 09 0C 02 FF 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 200

wait 4

say ring safe way ok
