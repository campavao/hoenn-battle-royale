# object-slots-near: when the view is crowded, a ghost close enough to engage gets an
# object before the loot's share does (POK-330 #48, the review).
#
# object-slots.txt's share gave loot up to BR_LOOT_SHARE (2) slots ahead of every ghost,
# however far the balls and however near the ghosts. A ghost with no object is neither
# drawn nor engaged (BrEngage_Tick skips it, and the lower seat is the one that
# challenges), so a trainer two steps away could stand there invisible and unchallenged
# while two balls across the screen were drawn. Now the share is what the ghosts within
# BR_SIGHT_RANGE (5) steps leave of it.
#
# Route 103 from (9,8), object-slots.txt's start: nothing else on the view, so the room
# is 16 - the player - BR_NPC_HEADROOM (3) = 12. Map coords below include MAP_OFFSET (7):
# we stand at (16,15).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 12 09 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 9
# Eleven ghosts within five steps: seats 1..5 at (11..15, 15) and 6..10 at (17..21, 15),
# one to five steps either side, and seat 11 five steps south at (16, 20). Seat 12 eight
# steps south at (16, 23), still in view.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 01 00 12 0B 00 0F 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 01 00 12 0C 00 0F 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 03 01 00 12 0D 00 0F 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 04 01 00 12 0E 00 0F 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 05 01 00 12 0F 00 0F 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 06 01 00 12 11 00 0F 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 07 01 00 12 12 00 0F 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 08 01 00 12 13 00 0F 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 09 01 00 12 14 00 0F 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 0A 01 00 12 15 00 0F 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 0B 01 00 12 10 00 14 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 0C 01 00 12 10 00 17 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
# ...and a spill of two balls from seat 20, thirteen and fourteen steps off at (24, 20)
# and (25, 20): seat, map 0:18, count 2, then key, x, y, species, level a row.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 14 00 12 02 00 14 18 00 14 00 15 01 05 01 14 19 00 14 00 15 01 05 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 11 gDecompressionBuffer 23
wait 30
# Twelve slots: the eleven near ghosts, and one ball in the one that is left. Seat 11 was
# the one the old share dropped -- five steps away, the lower seats ahead of it on the
# tie -- with seat 12, so that both balls could be up.
expectne u8 gBrSeats+25 0xFF
expectne u8 gBrSeats+105 0xFF
expectne u8 gBrSeats+169 0xFF
expectne u8 gBrSeats+185 0xFF
expect u8 gBrSeats+201 0xFF
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 2
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 1
shot crowd
# Seat 11 walks off past engage range, to (16, 22): now only ten are near, and the second
# ball has its slot back ahead of it.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 0B 01 00 12 10 00 16 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
wait 30
expect u8 gBrSeats+185 0xFF
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 2
shot ball-back
say object slots near ok
