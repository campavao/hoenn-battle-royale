# legend-denied: two trainers pressed A on the same legendary within one round trip
# (POK-410 review: "two trainers can both catch it"). Each ROM asks the host first, and the
# host answers the first ask with its PICKUP naming that trainer, the later one with the
# same PICKUP. We are seat 2 and seat 1 asked first: the answer names seat 1, so no battle
# starts, the box says somebody got there first, and it is off our ground with our party
# as it was.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMySeat 2
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x1234
# EVENT 0 at ROUTE 116, legendary 0xB000; then its SPILL: REGIROCK at (12,17), the cell we face
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 20 08 05 00 00 00 00 1F 00 B0
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 0B 11 0E 00 00 01 00 09 01 00 B0 0C 00 11 00 91 01 14 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 20
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 1
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x4D 200
drain gBrMailbox
tap A
wait 30
expectmsg 10 u16 1 0xB800
# the host's answer: seat 1's PICKUP of 0xB000
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 0A 0B 08 00 00 01 00 B0 00 00 00 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 90
shot box
wait 110
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 0
expect u8 gBrEvents+17 1
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
shot denied
tap A
wait 30
tap B
wait 60
# no battle came of it, and we never claimed it ourselves
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
say legend denied ok
