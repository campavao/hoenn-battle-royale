# world-grid: the whole window's anchor (the one-layer prototype, Cam 2026-10-20). Every
# field frame the ROM publishes gBrWorld: the scroll it wrote the BGs with, the grid pixel
# of the LCD's top-left, and where the core reads the map grid, the layout and whether
# the field is up. The core draws BG1..3 past the LCD from those, so the anchor has to be
# the camera's own: standing, the LCD's top-left is (pos.x*16, pos.y*16 + 40) on the
# backup grid (MAP_OFFSET 7 metatiles in, the player 7 across and 4.5 down the LCD) on
# either ring, and a step moves it 16. The pointers are the build's own symbols.
# checked-by: tools/br/world-grid.py
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u32 gBrWorld+0x10 gBackupMapLayout
expect u32 gBrWorld+0x14 gMain+4
expect u32 gBrWorld+0x18 CB2_Overworld+1
expectsame u32 gBrWorld+0x0C gMapHeader
expectsame u32 *(gBrWorld+0x14) gBrWorld+0x18
say tall
dump gBrWorld 28
dump *gSaveBlock1Ptr 4
hold RIGHT 16
wait 30
say tall-right
dump gBrWorld 28
dump *gSaveBlock1Ptr 4
pokebytes gBrFieldShown 28 00 28 00 80 00 80 00
hold LEFT 16
wait 30
say wide
dump gBrWorld 28
dump *gSaveBlock1Ptr 4
hold DOWN 16
wait 30
say wide-down
dump gBrWorld 28
dump *gSaveBlock1Ptr 4
say ok
