# world-grid: the whole window's anchor (the one-layer prototype, Cam 2026-10-20). Every
# field frame the ROM publishes gBrWorld: the scroll it wrote the BGs with and the grid
# pixel of the LCD's top-left (the grid, layout and callback are the page's symbols).
# The core draws BG1..3 past the LCD from those, so the anchor has to be the camera's
# own: standing, the LCD's top-left is (pos.x*16, pos.y*16 + 40) on the backup grid
# (MAP_OFFSET 7 metatiles in, the player 7 across and 4.5 down the LCD) on either ring,
# and a step moves it 16.
# checked-by: tools/br/world-grid.py
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# On the field: the callback the core checks (gMain.callback2) is CB2_Overworld.
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
say tall
dump gBrWorld 8
dump *gSaveBlock1Ptr 4
hold RIGHT 16
wait 30
say tall-right
dump gBrWorld 8
dump *gSaveBlock1Ptr 4
pokebytes gBrFieldShown 28 00 28 00 80 00 80 00
hold LEFT 16
wait 30
say wide
dump gBrWorld 8
dump *gSaveBlock1Ptr 4
hold DOWN 16
wait 30
say wide-down
dump gBrWorld 8
dump *gSaveBlock1Ptr 4
say ok
