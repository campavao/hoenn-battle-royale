# story-regi-fly: ANCIENT TOMB's sealed chamber opens on A at its wall with a Flying-type
# in the party (br_story.c, HasFlier), as its braille asks for FLY.
#
# FLAG_SYS_REGISTEEL_PUZZLE_COMPLETED (0x8B2) is gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1386 bit 2. Booted
# with the test SWELLOW (BR_BOOT_FLAG_TESTFLY) under the wall at (8,21) on ANCIENT TOMB
# (24:68). story-regi-nofly is the same wall with no flier.
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 8
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 41 00 18 44 08 00 15 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 24
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 68
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1386 0x04 0x00
hold UP 20
wait 30
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 21
tap A
wait 30
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1386 0x04 0x04
hold UP 12
wait 150
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 11
shot chamber
say story regi fly ok
