# challenge-gate: a CHALLENGE starts a fight only in the match proper, and only from a
# trainer standing on our map (POK-330 #24).
#
# The relay passes on whatever a member sends, so anybody in the room can name anybody
# in a CHALLENGE at any time -- and HandleChallenge took every one: in the lobby, in the
# Safari opening, after we were out, from a seat on the far side of Hoenn or from one it
# had never heard of. Our own eyeline refuses all of those (CanEngage, and the same-map
# test), so the answering side now asks the same things of what it can see.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# Seat 2 on our map, out of our eyeline: (20,8) facing SOUTH. Seat 4 on another map
# (group 0, number 10). Seat 5 is never placed at all.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 01 00 09 14 00 08 00 01 01 02
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 04 01 00 0A 0A 00 0A 00 01 01 04
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeats+32 1
expect u8 gBrSeats+64 1
# The lobby (phase NONE): CHALLENGE 2 -> 0.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 30
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
expect u8 gBrNetlink+16 0xFF
# The Safari opening.
poke u8 gBrMatch 1
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 30
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
# Out of the match.
poke u8 gBrMatch 3
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 30
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
# The match proper, from a seat on another map, and from one with no roster row.
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 04 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 05 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 30
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
expect u8 gBrNetlink+16 0xFF
shot refused
# And the one that is an engage: in the match, from seat 2 on this map.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 10
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink+1 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink+2 2
shot accepted
# Seat 2 goes out and the session closes (out-peer.txt), so nothing is left running.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02
mailsend gBrMailbox 9 gDecompressionBuffer 1
wait 300
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
say challenge gate ok
