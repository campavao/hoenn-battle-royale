# royale-ball-catch: a ROYALE BALL (POK-408, br_items.h) thrown on a battle's first turn
# is 4x the POKe BALL: a full-health ROUTE 102 mon (catch rate 200 or more) is caught on
# the throw whatever the roll. The retail POKe BALL under the same seed (gRngValue 1, poked
# mid-throw) breaks free.
#
# ROUTE 102 under match seed 4, as bag-pick-ball.txt; the boot's five balls.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 4
wait 30
tap LEFT
wait 4
tap LEFT
wait 250
tap LEFT
wait 250
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 4
wait 300
tap B
wait 300
tap B
wait 300
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
# BAG, on the POKe BALLS pocket
poke u8 gBagPosition+5 1
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap A
wait 120
shot bag
tap A
wait 30
tap A
wait 40
poke u32 gRngValue 1
wait 20
shot thrown
wait 330
shot landed
wait 270
shot result
# Through "Gotcha!" and back to the field: the catch is in the party. (A POKe BALL under
# seed 1 breaks free on that throw.)
hold A 4
wait 200
tap A
wait 200
tap A
wait 300
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 2
say royale ball ok
