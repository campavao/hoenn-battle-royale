# fog-woods-fade-in: walking into PETALBURG WOODS in the fog, the woods fade in (HBR-26,
# Cam's 2026-10-20 report: the map black under the HUD for the rest of the match).
#
# The woods' own weather is SHADE, which fades the screen in through the weather's
# palettes; the ring then puts the fog back, and a change of weather mid fade-in drops
# that fade where it is (Task_WeatherMain), at black. BrRing_Tick now waits for the
# fade-in to end before it re-applies the weather.
#
# On ROUTE 104 (0:19) at (10,39), under the woods' south door, with the ring on
# SLATEPORT CITY (8,10) and r 2: the route and the woods are both outside.
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 8
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 13 0A 00 27 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
# RING from seat 0: phase 1 at (8,10), r 2, no place.
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 01 08 0A 02 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 120
expect u8 gBrRing 1
expect u8 gWeather+0x6d0 6
hold UP 24
wait 240
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 24
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 11
# The fog again, its palette work idle, and BG palette 0 lit (colour 1, not black).
expect u8 gWeather+0x6d0 6
expect u8 gWeather+0x6c6 3
expectne u16 0x05000002 0
shot woods
say fog woods fade-in ok
