# catch-pages: what a catch says, and how many times it stops to say it (POK-266,
# Kanto v0.45.0 -- the catch confirmation is one page). Two of the three pages Emerald
# would show are already gone: the Pokedex is filled at boot (POK-256), so "data was
# added" never fires, and the nickname prompt went with it. This is the driver that says
# so out loud, and `caught` is the shot a player would read.
#
# Driving a battle: tap rather than hold; B advances a box without choosing anything,
# A on the Safari menu throws a ball (POK-269). A Safari mon flees when it feels like
# it, so this has four goes at it.

hold DOWN 140
hold LEFT 100
# Two patches either side of where that leaves us, (7,23) and (8,23) on this Safari map
# (wild header 0x48): a step in grass meets nothing now (br_patch.h). Before each walk
# below they grow back -- the map's byte of gBrPatchTaken cleared -- so every walk meets
# the next mon one step along.
pokebytes gBrPatch+16 0E 00 0F 00
pokebytes gBrPatch+32 1E 00 1E 00
poke u8 gBrPatch+4 2
# and the mon they deal: the battle draws from gRngValue, which the walk leaves wherever

hold LEFT 70
