# story-regi-wait: ISLAND CAVE's sealed chamber opens by itself once the ring has moved
# on a phase from the one its event dropped on (br_story.c, BrStory_Tick): its braille
# asks you to wait, and a match is the waiting.
#
# FLAG_SYS_BRAILLE_REGICE_COMPLETED (0x8B1) is gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1386 bit 1. On ISLAND
# CAVE (24:67) at (8,21), event 1's REGICE lands in the chamber (key 0xB010 at (8,7),
# (15,14) with MAP_OFFSET). Event 1 drops on phase 4; the door waits for phase 5.
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 8
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 18 43 08 00 15 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 67
poke u8 gBrRing+1 4
# SPILL: seat 0 on 24:67, one mon (key 0xB010, (15,14), REGICE 402, level 30), no bag
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 18 43 01 10 B0 0F 00 0E 00 92 01 1E 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 11 gDecompressionBuffer 14
wait 30
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1386 0x02 0x00
poke u8 gBrRing+1 5
wait 10
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1386 0x02 0x02
shot opened
hold UP 12
wait 150
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 11
say story regi wait ok
