# battle-hurry: gBrBattle.hurry (PAGE WRITES, not over a link) takes the transition off the
# hidden field and runs the intro at BR_INTRO_SPEED, so a wild battle is at its first menu
# in under half the frames (Cam, 2026-11-01: "there is always a delay before a battle").
# The same step as menu-byte.txt, which at normal speed is still in the intro at this
# point. Then the fight is an ordinary fight: the flag is gone and a move goes in.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrHud+0x17B 1
pokebytes gBrPatch+16 1A 00
pokebytes gBrPatch+32 10 00
poke u8 gBrPatch+4 1
poke u8 gBrBattle+13 1
tap LEFT
wait 4
tap LEFT
wait 250
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
shot mid
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
expect u8 gBrBattle+13 0
shot menu
expect u16 gBattle_BG0_Y 0
tap A
wait 30
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 2
tap A
wait 700
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
shot next-turn
say battle hurry ok
