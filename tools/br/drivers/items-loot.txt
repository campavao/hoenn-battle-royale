# items-loot: our own ground loot takes the item rework too (br_loot.c Take, by way of
# BrItems_Pickup): a dealt PROTEIN ball is used on the spot, on the whole party, and a
# dealt POKe BALL is six of them, said as "6 POKe BALLS".
#
# LITTLEROOT (map 0:9), the boot at (5,8); the pieces at (13,16) in loot cells, one RIGHT.
wait 30
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# the bag's key to 0, so the pockets read plain; the boot's 5 POKe BALLs to 0
poke u32 *gSaveBlock2Ptr+0xAC 0
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x650 4
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x652 0
expect u16 gPlayerParty+0x5A 9
# a PROTEIN (64) ball, keyed the way the Zone deals one
pokebytes gBrLoot 00 8F 0D 00 10 00 40 00 00 09 00 03 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
wait 30
tap RIGHT
wait 20
tap A
wait 30
shot protein
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 1
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0
expect u16 gPlayerParty+0x5A 12
# a POKe BALL (4) in the same cell, and the same press
pokebytes gBrLoot 01 8F 0D 00 10 00 04 00 00 09 00 03 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
wait 100
tap A
wait 30
shot balls
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 2
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x650 4
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x652 6
say items loot ok
