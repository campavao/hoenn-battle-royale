# story-clash: SOOTOPOLIS's story turns its weather to the clash while it is live, rain
# and drought by turns (WEATHER_ABNORMAL, 15), and back to the map's own sun (2) once
# its legendary is taken (br_story.c, Clash). A fight in the city starts in whichever
# it is: battle_util.c's switch-in weather reads the field's (DOWNPOUR or DROUGHT).
#
# In SOOTOPOLIS CITY (0:7) at (31,35), on the land in front of the CAVE OF ORIGIN.
# SaveBlock1's weather is +0x2E.
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 8
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 07 1F 00 23 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 7
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x2E 2
shot sun
# EVENT 0 at SOOTOPOLIS CITY (0x0E), legendary 0xB000
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 00 0E 00 B0
mailsend gBrMailbox 32 gDecompressionBuffer 5
wait 10
expect u8 gBrEvents+16 1
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x2E 15
wait 60
shot clash1
wait 300
shot clash2
wait 600
shot clash3
wait 600
shot clash
# Seat 3 takes the legendary: the clash is over
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 03 00 B0 00 00 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 10 gDecompressionBuffer 8
wait 10
expect u8 gBrEvents+17 1
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x2E 2
wait 600
shot after
say story clash ok
