# briney-ferry: MR. BRINEY is ashore wherever his boat is, and sails (2026-10-07 play-test).
#
# Cam on Route 109's beach: "the ship guy is not present, he should be here and able to
# give you rides". The boot's hide sweep took him from all three of his stops. Now each
# stop's map, loaded with him not at sea, puts him there (br_boot.c BrBoot_PlaceBriney),
# and his errands are run, so he goes straight to "where are we bound?".
#
# Booted on ROUTE 109 (map 0:24) at (21,23), a step north of where he stands (21,24) with
# the boat at (21,26). VAR_BRINEY_LOCATION is SaveBlock1 + 0x139C + 2*(0x4096 - 0x4000) =
# +0x14C8; the hide flags are at +0x1270: 0x2E4..0x2E8 are bits 4..7 of +0x12CC and bit 0
# of +0x12CD.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 18 15 00 17 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 24
# He is at Route 109, and nowhere else: 109's man and boat shown, Dewford's hidden.
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x14C8 3
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x12CC 0xB0 0x90
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x12CD 0x01 0x00
shot ashore
# Face him and talk: "where are we bound?" with DEWFORD and CANCEL.
hold DOWN 2
wait 10
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 60
shot where-bound
# The cursor starts on CANCEL; up to DEWFORD.
tap UP
wait 10
tap A
wait 30
# "DEWFORD it is, then!" and "Anchors aweigh!", a page each, each printed before its A.
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
# The voyage: he boards, the boat runs south off Route 109 and round to Dewford.
wait 400
shot voyage
wait 1200
shot ashore-dewford
# "Ahoy! We've hit land in DEWFORD!" and its second page; the var comes back after them.
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
shot landed
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 11
# Ashore again: the trip's 0 is gone (pret puts back what it was, which only ever
# meant "not at sea" in a game past the story -- the flags say where he is).
expectne u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x14C8 0
# Dewford's man and boat shown; Route 109's hidden behind us.
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x12CC 0xB0 0x20
expectbits u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x12CD 0x01 0x01
say briney ferry ok
