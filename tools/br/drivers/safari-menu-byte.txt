# safari-menu-byte: the Safari's controller says 3 on BALL/POKeBLOCK/GO NEAR/RUN and 0
# once a choice is made (menu-byte.txt is the ordinary battle). The opening is where a
# phone spends its first two minutes, so the tap-to-choose has to work here too.
#
# The walk into the grass is catch-pages.txt's.
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x00000C00
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 02 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gBrMatch 1
poke u8 gBrMatch+1 1
poke u16 gBrMatch+12 240
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 0
hold DOWN 140
hold LEFT 100
hold RIGHT 70
hold LEFT 70
hold RIGHT 70
hold LEFT 70
wait 40
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
shot safari-menu
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 3
expect u8 gActionSelectionCursor 0
tap DOWN
wait 6
expect u8 gActionSelectionCursor 2
tap UP
wait 6
# BALL: the choice leaves through SafariBufferExecCompleted, which clears it
tap A
wait 10
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 0
say safari menu byte ok
