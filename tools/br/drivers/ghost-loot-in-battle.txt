# ghost-loot-in-battle: a ghost's PLACE and a piece's PICKUP heard in the middle of a
# battle leave the object table alone until the field is back, and the loot on the map
# is still ours after the battle (POK-328's audit).
#
# npcout-in-battle.txt is the trainers' half. A ghost is the more common case: a PLACE
# comes every time anybody changes maps, and it despawned the ghost under whatever the
# battle had put in its old sprite slot. The loot was safe from that only because its
# tick forgot every object id off the field ("the object table is gone with the map") --
# which a battle does not do: the piece stayed drawn by an object nothing owned, so a
# pickup never took it off the map, and the next piece to want its local id could not
# spawn at all.
#
# Route 102, as npcout-in-battle.txt: under CALVIN, facing up. Seat 2's ghost three
# tiles east and a ball one tile west, both in view; CALVIN and RICK hold slots 1 and 2,
# so the ghost is slot 3 (local id 0xCA, sprite 4) and the ball slot 4 (0xC0, sprite 5).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 21 00 0F 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrMatch+1 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 03 19 0C 3C 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
# PLACE seat 2 on 0:17 at (43,22): type 2, framed len 14 = hdr(0B 00 00) + 11
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 02 0E 0B 00 00 02 01 00 11 2B 00 16 00 01 01 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 2
# SPILL from seat 1: one mon, key 0x0101, at (39,22), species 25 at 5, no bag
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 0B 11 0E 00 00 01 00 11 01 01 01 27 00 16 00 19 00 05 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 30
