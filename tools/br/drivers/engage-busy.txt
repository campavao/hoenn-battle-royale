# engage-busy: a seat in a battle is left alone. Seat 1 reports BUSY battle, then is
# placed in our eyeline: no challenge. BUSY map lifts it: the challenge goes out.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# the eyeline only fires in a started match (gBrMatch.phase = BR_PHASE_PLAY)
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
# BUSY: type 17, framed len 5 = hdr(02 00 00) + seat 01, kind 02 (battle)
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 11 05 02 00 00 01 02
# PLACE seat 1 at (15,16) facing WEST on Littleroot, three tiles east of us
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 0F 00 10 00 03 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeatBusy+1 2
expect u8 gBrSeats+16 1
expect u16 gBrEngage+4 0
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
# BUSY map: they are back on the map
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 11 05 02 00 00 01 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeatBusy+1 0
expect u16 gBrEngage+4 1
# The challenge parks for BR_ENGAGE_WAIT frames first, in case the seat is a bot whose
# card is still on its way (bot-spotted.txt); nothing answers for a person.
wait 120
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
say engage busy ok
