# trainer-drop: a beaten route trainer leaves one of their team on the ground where
# they stood (POK-232), and leaves the map for the rest of the match. The play-test
# says it does not happen -- "an NPC trainer didn't drop their Pokemon after defeat" --
# and trainer-despawn.txt only ever poked the despawn list, so the real path (win a
# trainer battle, look at the tile) has never been driven. This is that path.
#
# gym-rung.txt's own opening: Rustboro Gym, one of Roxanne's students up the hall.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 0B 03 05 00 0F 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# A started match at rung 30, so both sides of the fight stand at 30 (POK-225/234).
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrMatch+1 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 03 19 0C 3C 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
expect u8 gBrLevels 30
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 0
hold UP 120
wait 30
tap A
wait 120
tap A
wait 240
shot trainer
# Through the intro, and then the fight is called: B_OUTCOME_WON, which the engine
# reads at the next turn boundary (br_match.c's own note about a poked outcome). What
# is on trial here is what a won trainer battle leaves behind, not whether a Treecko
# can win one -- and a press that lands on a box is a box advanced, which is what the
# rest of these are for.
poke u8 gBattleOutcome 1
tap A

poke u8 gBattleOutcome 1

tap A

tap A

# in front of us is how loot is picked up (POK-232) and the ball is the whole point.

# One of their team is on the ground where they stood...
expectge u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 1
expectge u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 1
# ...and they are gone from the map for the rest of the match.
expectge u8 gBrLoot+0xA3 1
shot ball
# And it is a real piece of loot: one press, facing it, and it is ours.
tap A
wait 90

say trainer drop ok
