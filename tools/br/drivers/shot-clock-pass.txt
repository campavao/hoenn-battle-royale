# shot-clock-pass: in a fight nobody else is waiting on, the clock spends the turn
# (POK-313). Kanto, lib/bots.lua: "the player who does not pick in time is not penalised
# beyond the turn -- their mon does nothing while the bot's moves, and the menu comes back
# with a fresh clock. The PvP clock forfeits; this one only spends the turn."
#
# shot-clock.txt is the PvP half. This is run-bot.txt's staged bot fight, and then nothing
# is pressed for thirty seconds.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
# The bot is a SURSKIT at 5: its learnset has nothing but BUBBLE until 7, and the ROM
# gives a bot's mon its learnset's moves, not the card's (POK-330 #50), so the bot has
# nothing else to pick and the turn below does not ride on where the RNG happens to be
# when the fight starts.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 03 BC C9 CE 01 37 01 05 13 00 13 00 00 91 00 1E 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 CE CC BF C5 C9
mailsend gBrMailbox 23 gDecompressionBuffer 106
wait 10
# Seat 31's ghost on our map (out of our eyeline), then its CHALLENGE: a challenge
# comes from a trainer standing on our map (POK-330 #24).
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 01 00 09 14 00 08 00 01 01 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 300
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 200
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
expect u16 gBrBattle+2 0
shot menu
# gBattleMons[0] is ours, gBattleMons[1] (+0x58) theirs: POUND's PP at +0x24, HP at
# +0x28. Their trainer AI can only pick BUBBLE, which is what makes this readable: HP
# off us is a turn THEY took, and HP still on them is one we did not answer. Both set to
# 15 here, so the reading does not ride on either mon's IVs.
expect u8 gBattleMons+0x24 35
poke u16 gBattleMons+0x28 15
poke u16 gBattleMons+0x80 15
wait 1900
# the clock fired once...
expect u16 gBrBattle+2 1
wait 400
shot after
# ...their move landed on us and ours never swung: our HP down, POUND's PP untouched,
# and their HP where it was
expectle u16 gBattleMons+0x28 14
expect u8 gBattleMons+0x24 35
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x80 15
# and the menu is back, with a fresh clock, for the second turn
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
expectle u16 gBrBattle 500
say shot clock pass ok
