# loot-label: the ticker names the piece in front of you before you take it (POK-289).
#
# Kanto's rule, from its README: "face a piece (or stand on it) and the ticker in the
# top-left corner names it -- the Pokemon with its party icon, or whose bag it is -- and
# A takes it." BrHud_Hold and BrHud_Release have existed since the HUD went in and were
# called by nothing, so the only thing a pile ever said was the 90-frame box AFTER the
# press: you could not tell a NUGGET from a MASTER BALL from somebody's SWAMPERT until
# you had already taken one, which with a full party is a decision you cannot undo.
#
# The boot's warp lands a tile south of the cell it names (the way a door exit does), so
# the player stands on Littleroot (5,9) facing south -- (12,16) outer, facing (12,17).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# nothing in reach, nothing held
expect u8 gBrHud+0x0C 0
# a MUDKIP ball on the cell we face. SPECIES_MUDKIP is 283 (0x011B) -- not 258, which is
# an empty slot whose name is "?", and which is exactly what the ticker said when this
# driver had the wrong number in it. Key 1, (12,17), map 0:9, level 5, kind 1
# (BR_LOOT_MON), objId BR_NO_OBJ.
pokebytes gBrLoot 01 00 0C 00 11 00 1B 01 00 09 05 01 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
wait 30
shot named
# the held line is up, and it is the species name: six letters starting with M (0xC7 in
# the Gen 3 charmap, where A is 0xBB)
expect u8 gBrHud+0x0C 1
expect u8 gBrHud+0x19 6
expect u8 gBrHud+0x1A 0xC7
# take it, and the label comes off with the piece
tap A
wait 20
shot took
expect u8 gBrHud+0x0C 0
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 1
say loot label ok
