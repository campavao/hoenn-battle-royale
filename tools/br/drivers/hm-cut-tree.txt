# hm-cut-tree: a CUT tree comes down as you walk into it, when somebody in the party could
# learn CUT (Cam's 10-19 play-test, #7: "we got rid of all the cuttable trees and boulders,
# but that could be a cool way to reintroduce them"). No "would you like to CUT it?", no
# cut-in (br_hm.c, BrHm_WalkInto).
#
# ROUTE 104 (map 0:19): the tree at (35,22). The boot's TREECKO could learn CUT. We boot
# on (35,23), under it.
wait 30
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 13 23 00 17 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 35
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 23
shot before
hold UP 16
wait 120
shot cut
# down, and the field is ours again; one more step goes where it stood
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
hold UP 16
wait 30
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 22
shot through
say hm cut tree ok
