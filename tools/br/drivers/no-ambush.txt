# no-ambush: Emerald's own route trainers do not start a fight on sight during a match
# (POK-259). Route 102's BUG CATCHER RICK stands at (25,15) with MOVEMENT_TYPE_FACE_UP
# and sight 2, so the cells at (25,13) and (25,14) are inside his eyeline. We boot two
# tiles above and walk down through it.
#
# No A is pressed here on purpose. The rule is "only their own A-press starts their
# fight", so a driver that walks past and then taps A is testing the opposite rule --
# which is exactly what the first draft of this did, and it failed for the right reason.
#
# Its control is ambush-control.txt: the same walk with no match running, where he does
# see us and the battle starts. One without the other proves nothing.
wait 30
# BR_BOOT_MAP | TESTMON, male, Route 102 (group 0, num 17), x=25, y=11
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 19 00 0B 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# the match is under way
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrMatch+1 1
expect u8 gBrMatch 2
hold DOWN 40
wait 400
shot walked
# nothing grabbed us on the way past
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 0
say no ambush ok
