# hud-vram-safari: the Safari's START menu over the HUD (POK-329). hud-vram.txt has the
# model and the five-row menu out in Hoenn; the Zone's is four rows (safari-menu.txt),
# 0x139..0x17E, with the balls window beside it from tile 0x008. It covers the corner's
# tiles and the box's first forty-three. Every frame through its opening and closing is
# watched, the box up the whole time, and tools/br/hud-vram.py checks every cell naming
# a shared tile, and that BG1..3 show the field's own tilemaps throughout.
# checked-by: tools/br/hud-vram.py
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x00000C00
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 82 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gBrMatch 1
poke u8 gBrHud+0x00 7
poke u16 gBrHud+0x02 125
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0F 15 12 00 00 01 01 0F C7 BB D3 00 C1 C9 CE 00 BC CC BF C8 BE BB C8
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
pokebytes gBrHud+0x14C 00 05 C2 BF C6 C6 C9 FF
poke u8 gBrHud+0x178 255
poke u8 gBrHud+0x0D 4
wait 4
poke u8 gBrHud+0x0B 0
expect u8 gBrHud+0x09 7
shot hud-up
say safari-menu-open
watch gMain+4 4
watch gBrHud 16
watch 0x0600F800 2048
watch 0x0600A720 6368
watch windows 0
watch 0x0400000A 6
watch 0x0600C800 2048
watch 0x0600D800 2048
watch 0x0600E800 2048
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 2048
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 2048
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 2048
wait 2
tap START
wait 14
unwatch
say safari-menu-up
# four rows: the menu is rows 1..10 and its frame's foot row 11, 0x21B at (22,11), whole
# where it runs into the box's rows; the box is down, its first cell off
expectbits u16 0x0600FAEC 0x3FF 0x21B
expect u16 0x0600FAC2 0
expect u8 gBrHud+0x09 0
shot menu
say safari-menu-close
watch gMain+4 4
watch gBrHud 16
watch 0x0600F800 2048
watch 0x0600A720 6368
watch windows 0
watch 0x0400000A 6
watch 0x0600C800 2048
watch 0x0600D800 2048
watch 0x0600E800 2048
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 2048
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 2048
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 2048
wait 2
tap B
wait 10
unwatch
say safari-menu-closed
expect u8 gBrHud+0x09 7
expect u16 0x0600F868 0xF139
expect u16 0x0600FAC2 0xF154
shot hud-back
say hud vram safari ok
