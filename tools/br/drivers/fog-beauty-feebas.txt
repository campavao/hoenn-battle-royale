# fog-beauty-feebas: beauty rises as the fog closes (Cam, 2026-11-10), so FEEBAS can
# become MILOTIC (EVO_BEAUTY 170) the way a contest-fed one would. Beauty goes up 60 a
# ring: 60 at rung 15, 120 at rung 30, 180 at rung 50 -- the first lift past 170. A
# FEEBAS never evolved at all before, because nothing in a match ever raised it.
#
# Beauty is encrypted on a party mon; tools/br/fog-friendship.py decrypts the dumps.
# checked-by: tools/br/fog-friendship.py
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 0
# SPILL from seat 2: one FEEBAS (328 = 0x148) at level 5 on the cell we face
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0B 11 0E 00 00 02 00 09 01 00 02 0C 00 10 00 48 01 05 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 20
tap A
wait 30
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
# F E E B A S
expect u8 gPlayerParty+8 0xC0
expect u8 gPlayerParty+9 0xBF
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 5
say stage 0 rung 5
dump gPlayerParty 100
# ring 1 first, as a real match goes: tier 0, nothing warmer yet
poke u8 gBrRing+1 1
poke u8 gBrRing 1
wait 200
say stage 5 ring 1
dump gPlayerParty 100
poke u8 gBrRing+1 2
wait 200
expect u8 gBrLevels 15
say stage 1 rung 15
dump gPlayerParty 100
poke u8 gBrRing+1 3
wait 200
expect u8 gBrLevels 30
# still a FEEBAS: beauty 120 is short of 170
expect u8 gPlayerParty+8 0xC0
say stage 2 rung 30
dump gPlayerParty 100
poke u8 gBrRing+1 4
wait 200
expect u8 gBrLevels 50
# M I L O T I C
expect u8 gPlayerParty+8 0xC7
expect u8 gPlayerParty+9 0xC3
expect u8 gPlayerParty+10 0xC6
expect u8 gPlayerParty+11 0xC9
say stage 3 rung 50
dump gPlayerParty 100
say fog beauty feebas ok
