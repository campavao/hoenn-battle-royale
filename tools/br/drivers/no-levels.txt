# no-levels: no level number on any screen during a round (POK-266, Kanto v0.37.0). The
# rung is shared, so a number that is the same for everybody is noise -- and reading an
# opponent by their team rather than by a number is the point.
#
# The party screen is the one a player opens most, so that is the one this looks at:
# START, POKEMON, and a shot with a match running. `levels-shown.txt` is the control,
# the same screen with no match, where the Lv is where it always was.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
tap START
wait 40
tap A
wait 150
shot party
say no levels ok
