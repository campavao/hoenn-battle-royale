# fog-in-duel: the fog reaches into a fight between contestants (2026-10-05 play-test:
# "players not taking fog damage if in a battle"). It used to be the half of
# fog-in-battle.txt that said it did not (POK-262, Kanto v0.3.1); Cam reversed it.
#
# Not on the field's clock: in a link battle only the challenger's ROM runs the engine,
# so the engine does it, at the end of each turn, to whoever is on the field, down to 1
# HP (br_ring.c, BrRing_FogEndTurn). Whether the fight is in the fog is decided once, as
# it starts, so the ring closes first here and then the challenge comes.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
# the fog closes somewhere else: RING phase 1, centre (25,12), r 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 01 19 0C 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 60
expect u8 gBrRing+5 1
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 0F 00 10 00 03 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 900
# in the duel, and it knows it is in the fog
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
expect u8 gBrRing+31 1
# the field's clock has stopped: what bleeds from here is the engine's turn
poke u16 gBrRing+10 0
wait 300
expect u16 gBrRing+10 0
# one turn: FIGHT, the first move
tap A
wait 30
tap A
wait 1500
shot fog-turn
# it reached in, and ended nobody
expectge u16 gBrRing+10 1
expect u8 gBattleOutcome 0
expect u8 gBrRing+7 0
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
say fog in duel ok
