# cable-car-rides: the cable car takes you up MT. CHIMNEY in a match (POK-425).
#
# The attendant at each station is a BEAUTY with no flag, so the match's strip
# (br_strip.c) took her with the towns' people and the ride was gone. Jagged Pass only
# goes down without an ACRO BIKE, so she is the way up.
#
# In ROUTE 112 CABLE CAR STATION (19:0) at (6,7), south of the attendant at (6,6). The
# strip marks a map's people as it loads, so the match starts first and then we walk out
# to ROUTE 112 and back in.
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 8
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 13 00 06 00 07 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 19
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 0
hold DOWN 80
wait 200
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
hold UP 20
wait 200
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 19
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 0
hold UP 90
wait 20
shot at-the-desk
# "The cable car is ready to go up. Would you like to be on it?" YES.
tap UP
wait 10
tap A
wait 60
shot ready-to-go
tap A
wait 60
# "Please step this way."
hold A 4
wait 60
shot step-this-way
hold A 4
wait 120
shot boarding
# The ride itself, then the walk out at the top.
wait 400
shot riding
wait 1100
shot arrived
wait 600
shot top
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 19
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 1
