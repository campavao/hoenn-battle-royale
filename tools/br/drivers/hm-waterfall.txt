# hm-waterfall: up a waterfall with no HM and no prompt (2026-10-13, "get rid of the
# need for HMs"). br_hm.c's BrHm_WalkInto: surfing north into a waterfall climbs it.
#
# ROUTE 119 (map 0:34). The shore at (16,29) faces a pool at x 17-19; the falls are
# x 17-19, y 25-28, with more river above at y 22-24.
wait 30
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 22 10 00 1D 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 34
shot booted
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0 16
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 29
# East into the pool: surfing with no prompt.
hold RIGHT 24
wait 150
expectbits u8 gPlayerAvatar 0x08 0x08
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0 17
# North into the falls: no "use WATERFALL?", the climb runs to the top.
hold UP 24
wait 300
shot climbed
# At the top (y 24), still surfing, and the field is the player's again. Without the
# hook the step lands on the falls and the current pushes it back to y 29.
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0 17
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 24
expectbits u8 gPlayerAvatar 0x08 0x08
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
