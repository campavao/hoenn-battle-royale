# learn-ask: a lift's fifth move is asked about, as the cartridge asks (Cam, 2026-10-18,
# POK-400: "show the normal learn/replace prompt"). It used to swap in BrLevels' own
# pick of the best four without a word. The TREECKO lifted 5 -> 30 (one-clock.txt's
# ring) evolves to GROVYLE and fills its two empty slots with ABSORB and QUICK ATTACK
# as before; FURY CUTTER, PURSUIT, SCREECH and LEAF BLADE find four moves there, so the
# party screen asks about each in learnset order when START's POKeMON opens it:
# FURY CUTTER taken over POUND, the other three declined, then back to the field.
# gPartyMenu: data[0] (+14) the move asked about, data[1] (+16) 1 = ask the next after.

# the question waits (Cam, 2026-10-18: "it can wait"): the ticker says so, the field
# stays ours -- a step west and back -- and START's POKeMON asks it
shot ticker-says
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0 5
hold LEFT 20
wait 20
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0 3
hold RIGHT 20
wait 20
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0 5
tap START
wait 30
tap A
wait 200
# No "wants to learn... delete an older move?" and no YES/NO (Cam's 2026-10-19 play-test:
# teaching needs no confirmations): POKeMON opens straight on the summary screen's
# "forget which", FURY CUTTER listed fifth.

expect u16 gPartyMenu+16 1

shot learned-fury-cutter

tap A
wait 200

shot pursuit

tap B
wait 150
tap A
wait 200

tap B
wait 150
tap A
wait 150
# back to START, as the party screen would have gone
shot back-on-start

wait 30

# the field is ours again: the party screen let go, and a step west takes
