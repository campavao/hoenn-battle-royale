# house-gift: somebody at home (POK-379, br_balls.c). One house in eight has a person in
# it beside the cache, the same ones for every ROM, with a Pokemon. This one gives it:
# he turns to us, says his line, then "received BULBASAUR!" with the fanfare, and the
# PICKUP of his key takes him off every map.
#
# house-cache's boot: PETALBURG CITY's HOUSE1 (map 8:2), sHouses[33]. Seed 0x1086 has
# somebody home there (33 % 8 is the seed's eighth) giving a BULBASAUR. The house's own
# people only go at a map load once the match is on, so out of the door and back in.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 08 02 03 00 06 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x1086
wait 30
hold DOWN 90
wait 200
hold UP 40
wait 200
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
# the cache on the table, (10,12), and him at its west end, (8,11): key
# 0x8C21, a BULBASAUR (1) at the rung (level 0), standing as a person
expect u16 gBrLoot 0x8D21
expect u16 gBrLoot+20 0x8C21
expect u16 gBrLoot+22 8
expect u16 gBrLoot+24 11
expect u16 gBrLoot+26 1
expect u8 gBrLoot+30 0
expect u8 gBrLoot+31 1
expectne u8 gBrLoot+32 0xFF
# up a step, left past the table to (1,6), up beside him, and face him
hold UP 16
wait 20
hold LEFT 16
wait 20
hold LEFT 16
wait 20
hold UP 34
wait 20
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 1
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 5
hold UP 1
wait 20
shot home
tap A
wait 120
shot talks
# his line is up and he still has it
expect u8 gBrLoot+31 1
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
tap A
wait 40
# He waits on the host first (POK-379, as the event trader does): our PICKUP of his
# claim key, 0x8C40 above his, and nothing given yet
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
expectmsg 10 u16 1 0x8C61
expect u8 gBrLoot+31 1
# ...and the host's PICKUP of his key naming us (seat 0) is the yes: he is still standing
# for GiftTake, which gives him and sends his key as ever
expect u8 gBrMySeat 0
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 21 8C 00 00 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 10 gDecompressionBuffer 8
wait 40
shot given
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 2
expectmsg 10 u16 1 0x8C21
wait 200
tap A
wait 30
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
expect u8 gBrLoot+31 0
# ...and for good: out (down past the table to the door) and back in, and the cache is
# back but he is not
hold DOWN 40
wait 20
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 7
hold RIGHT 40
wait 20
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr 3
hold DOWN 90
wait 200
expectne u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 2
hold UP 40
wait 200
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 2
expect u16 gBrLoot 0x8D21
expect u8 gBrLoot+31 0
shot gone
say house gift ok
