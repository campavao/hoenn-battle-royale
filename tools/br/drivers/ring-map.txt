# ring-map: the fog, on the map, from the START menu mid-match (POK-263). Until this
# the ring was only ever a FOG! flash once you were already in it.
#
# The fog is drawn as a purple overlay over everything outside the ring (POK-277) --
# blended on BG1 rather than outlined in sprites, which is what the play-test asked for
# and what `shot ring` below is the record of. VRAM says which cells it covers: the
# expects after the shot read BG1's tilemap, the fog's palette and the blend registers
# (br_map.c's BrMap_ShadeFog / BrMap_BlendFog, which POK-331 #24 moved out of
# region_map.c without changing a write).
#
# The menu in a match is POKEMON / BAG / MAP / <name> / EXIT (start-menu.txt pins the
# first two), so MAP is two below the top with a party in the bag.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# a match, dropped, with a ring around Rustboro-ish: centre (4,5), radius 4
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrRing 1
poke u8 gBrRing+1 1
poke u8 gBrRing+2 4
poke u8 gBrRing+3 5
poke u8 gBrRing+4 4
tap START
wait 40
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap A
wait 240
shot ring
# BG1's tilemap is screen block 30 (0x0600F000), 32 cells a row, and the region-map grid
# starts at its cell (1,2). The fog is one tile of its own after the frame's last
# (0x15 here) in palette 10: 0xA015. Inside the ring the frame's own cell stays:
# (4,5) the centre, and (8,5), four cells out on a radius of 4 -- the edge is inside,
# the test is <=. One further, (9,5), is fog, and so is the far corner (27,14).
expect u16 0x0600F1CA 0x1000
expect u16 0x0600F1D2 0x1000
expect u16 0x0600F1D4 0xA015
expect u16 0x0600F438 0xA015
# the fog's colour, RGB(13,3,22), in palette 10...
expect u16 0x05000140 0x586D
expect u16 0x0500015E 0x586D
# ...and the blend: BG1 over BG2 and the backdrop, 10/16 fog over 6/16 map
expect u16 0x04000050 0x2442
expect u16 0x04000052 0x060A
# the map is up and it is a look, not a pick: the drop is not running
expect u8 gBrMap 1
expect u8 gBrPick 0
# and B puts us back on the field
tap B
wait 200
expect u8 gBrMap 0
say ring map ok
