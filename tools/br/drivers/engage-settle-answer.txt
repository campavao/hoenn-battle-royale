# engage-settle-answer: the challenged side freezes when the challenge lands, and starts
# its link battle only once the challenger's ghost has walked the steps the wire gave it
# (BrEngage_Settle, Cam's play-test 2026-10-05). It used to start the link on the frame
# the CHALLENGE arrived, with the challenger's ghost still somewhere else on the screen.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
# Seat 2 at (20,13) facing WEST, three rows north of us and out of our eyeline.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 01 00 09 14 00 0D 00 03 01 02
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeats+32 1
# Three steps WEST, to (17,13), and the CHALLENGE 2 -> 0 right behind them, the order
# the wire delivers them in.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 03 13 00 0D 00 00 09
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 8
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 03 12 00 0D 00 00 09
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 8
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 03 11 00 0D 00 00 09
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 8
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 00 01 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 10
# Settling: frozen, the ghost still walking, no link yet.
expect u8 gBrEngage+11 2
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
expect u16 gBrSeats+36 17
hold LEFT 20
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+2 12
shot settling
wait 40
# The ghost is on (17,13) and the link is open, as link id 1.
expect u8 gBrEngage+11 0xFF
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink+1 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink+2 2
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+2 12
shot started
# Seat 2 goes out and the session closes (out-peer.txt), so nothing is left running.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02
mailsend gBrMailbox 9 gDecompressionBuffer 1
wait 300
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
say engage settle answer ok
