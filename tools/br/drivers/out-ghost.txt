# out-ghost: an eliminated seat's ghost leaves the map, and stays gone (POK-330 #5).
#
# The page has always pushed every `out` into every ROM, and no handler listened: the
# fallen kept standing where they fell, in everybody's eyeline, and could still be
# challenged. Nor is one removal enough on its own -- an eliminated trainer spectates,
# its ROM follows whoever it watches, and its page keeps forwarding where it walks, so
# the next PLACE or STEP from that seat would put the ghost straight back.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# Seat 1 on Littleroot at (15,16) facing WEST: three tiles east of us, engage.txt's
# cell, in our eyeline. Still the lobby (phase NONE), so nobody challenges anybody.
# PLACE: seat, hasMap, group, num, x u16, y u16, dir, 1, skin
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 01 00 09 0F 00 10 00 03 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeats+16 1
expectne u8 gBrSeats+25 0xFF
shot standing
# OUT: seat 1.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 9 gDecompressionBuffer 1
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeats+16 0
expect u8 gBrSeats+25 0xFF
shot gone
# Their page goes on forwarding where their ROM is: a PLACE on the same cell, and a STEP
# (seat, dir, x u16, y u16, group, num) one tile north of it. Neither brings them back.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 01 00 09 0F 00 10 00 03 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 02 0F 00 0F 00 00 09
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 8
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeats+16 0
expect u8 gBrSeats+25 0xFF
# The match proper, with that cell still in our eyeline: nothing there to challenge.
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
wait 150
expect u16 gBrEngage+4 0
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
# Somebody still in it is untouched: seat 2 on the same cell is seen and challenged.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 01 00 09 0F 00 10 00 03 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeats+32 1
expect u16 gBrEngage+4 1
expect u8 gBrEngage+7 2
shot still-in-it
say out ghost ok
