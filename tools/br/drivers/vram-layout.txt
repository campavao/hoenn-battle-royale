# vram-layout: the field's BG1..3 tilemaps are at screen blocks 27, 25 and 29, not pret's
# 29, 28 and 30 (POK-329). Two blocks a layer, so each can grow to 256x512 in place, and
# BG2's map at block 25 (0x0600C800) is where BG0's text tiles end: tile 0x240, the
# HUD's ceiling (br_hud.h). BrField_InitRingBgs sets them every time the field builds
# its BGs -- a map load and the way back from a battle both -- and every tilemap copy
# follows the config, so the three maps in VRAM are the field's buffers wherever they
# are (hud-vram.py checks that, frame by frame, through every window over the HUD).
#
# BGxCNT's map base is bits 8..12: 27 is 0x1B00, 25 0x1900, 29 0x1D00.
#
# Route 102 under CALVIN (npcout-in-battle.txt's boot): a map load, then his fight, won,
# and back.

wait 400

say after-a-map-load

expectbits u16 0x0400000C 0x1F00 0x1900
expectbits u16 0x0400000E 0x1F00 0x1D00
