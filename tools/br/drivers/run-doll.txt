# run-doll: RUN spends a POKe DOLL for a guaranteed getaway (POK-231, POK-293).
# Loopback engage battle; a doll is poked into the bag, then we pick RUN. Without a doll
# RUN against a trainer fails outright (run-roll), so this is the one attempt that gets
# away: runRolls and runEscapes both 1, the outcome RAN. (Loopback leaves the link up; a
# real peer tears the battle down.)
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# the eyeline only fires in a started match (gBrMatch.phase = BR_PHASE_PLAY)
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 0F 00 10 00 03 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 900
expect u16 gBrBattle+4 0
# a POKe DOLL in the bag: zero the save encryption key, then POKe DOLL (item 0x50) x1 in slot 0
poke u32 *gSaveBlock2Ptr+0xAC 0
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0x50
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x562 1
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap RIGHT
wait 10
shot run
tap A
wait 120
shot fled
# The doll is the whole mechanism now (POK-293), so this is the ONE attempt that ever
# gets away: attempted once, escaped once, outcome RAN (4) rather than a loss, and the
# doll gone from the bag.
expect u16 gBrBattle+4 1
expect u16 gBrBattle+6 1
expect u8 gBattleOutcome 4
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0
# ...and the turn a spectator is sent says so (POK-330 #12): RUN (3) with its kind after
# it, BR_RUN_DOLL (1), for us and for the loopback's mirror. Without the kind the replay
# ran it as nothing to run with, printed "Can't escape!", and waited on a turn that was
# never coming.
expectmsg 19 u16 0 0x0100
expectmsg 19 u8 2 0
expectmsg 19 u8 3 2
expectmsg 19 u8 4 3
expectmsg 19 u8 5 1
expectmsg 19 u8 6 1
expectmsg 19 u8 8 3
expectmsg 19 u8 9 1
say run doll ok
