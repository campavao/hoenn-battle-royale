# stall-reset: a turn the bag's clock spent in one link fight is not spent again in the
# next one (POK-331 #11).
#
# pvp-bag-clock.txt is the clock over the bag: past thirty seconds BrBattle_TickStall
# presses B and sets `stalled`, and the action menu it backs out to times out at once.
# A fight torn down by Abandon (the peer out, the hello watchdog) while the bag was still
# closing never got back to that menu, so `stalled` outlived it -- and the next link
# fight's very first choice was taken by the clock: a forfeit, and the match with it.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
# out-peer.txt's fight: we challenge seat 1, loopback on, up to the first choice.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 600
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 200
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
# BAG, and pvp-bag-clock.txt's thirty seconds in it skipped to their last frame.
tap RIGHT
wait 30
tap A
wait 150
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 3
poke u16 gBrBattle+10 1799
wait 3
# The clock ran out in the bag: B is pressed, the turn is spent, and the menu that
# would spend it is not back yet.
expect u8 gBrBattle+9 1
expect u16 gBrBattle+2 0
# ...and seat 1 goes out while the bag is closing. The fight is ours.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 9 gDecompressionBuffer 1
wait 150
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
expect u8 gBrNetlink+11 1
expect u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 gBrBattle+9 0
expect u16 gBrBattle+10 0
shot back
# The next link fight, with seat 3: its first action menu is a choice, not a forfeit.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 03 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 600
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink+2 3
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 200
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
expect u8 gBattleOutcome 0
expect u16 gBrBattle+2 0
shot next-fight
# Seat 3 goes out too, so nothing is left running.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 03
mailsend gBrMailbox 9 gDecompressionBuffer 1
wait 150
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
say stall reset ok
