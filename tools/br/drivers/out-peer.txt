# out-peer: the seat we are fighting goes out, and the fight is ours (POK-330 #5).
#
# The hello watchdog (netlink-silent.txt) only covers a peer that never said a word. One
# that answered the start exchange and then closed its tab left the survivor at the
# action menu waiting for blocks that were not coming -- and everybody watching the
# fight waiting with them, because only a fighter's RESULT lets a replay go. The host
# says `out` for a seat the relay has stopped seeing; now the ROM listens.
#
# netlink-loop.txt's session: we challenge seat 1 with loopback on, so the fight is real
# up to the first choice. Then seat 1 goes out.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
# CHALLENGE: challenger 00 (us), opponent 01, nonce 0
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 600
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
expect u8 gWirelessCommType 5
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 200
# STATE_WAIT_ACTION_CHOSEN: mid-fight, nothing decided.
expect u8 gBattleCommunication 2
expect u8 gBattleOutcome 0
shot menu
# OUT: seat 1.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 9 gDecompressionBuffer 1
wait 120
# The session is closed, the fight went down as won (lastOutcome = B_OUTCOME_WON, the
# outcome CB2_BrReturnFromBattle sent as our RESULT), and we are still in the match.
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
expect u8 gWirelessCommType 0
expect u8 gBrNetlink+11 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink+20 0
expect u8 gBrMatch 2
shot back
# On our own feet: a step is taken, which is the overworld's callback1 and its input.
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+4 16
hold DOWN 60
wait 20
expectge u16 gBrOwnPos+4 17
# A challenge still waiting for us to leave a menu goes with its challenger. Seat 2 is
# placed out of our eyeline; with the START menu open, its CHALLENGE and its OUT land in
# the same frame, and the menu is not closed for a fight that is not coming.
tap START
wait 40
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 01 00 09 14 00 08 00 01 01 02
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02
mailsend gBrMailbox 9 gDecompressionBuffer 1
wait 30
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
expect u8 gBrNetlink+16 0xFF
expect u8 gBrSeats+32 0
shot menu-stays
say out peer ok
