# hud-vram-mart: a mart's clerk over the HUD (POK-329). hud-vram.txt has the model;
# this is Oldale's Mart (map 2:4, the clerk at (1,3), as mart-no-stones.txt), where the
# clerk talks in window 0 -- the ticker's tiles and the box's last forty-eight -- and
# the BUY / SELL / SEE YA multichoice is its own window from tile 0x008. The mart's
# lines after the first are not the field message box at all (DisplayItemMessageOnField),
# which is why the HUD reads window 0's first cell as well as the message box's mode.
# Every frame from A to the choice, and from SEE YA to the HUD coming back, is watched,
# and tools/br/hud-vram.py checks every cell naming a shared tile, and that BG1..3 show
# the field's own tilemaps throughout.
# checked-by: tools/br/hud-vram.py
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 02 04 01 00 04 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 700
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 2
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 4
poke u8 gBrHud+0x00 7
poke u16 gBrHud+0x02 125
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0F 15 12 00 00 01 00 0F C7 BB D3 00 C1 C9 CE 00 BC CC BF C8 BE BB C8
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
pokebytes gBrHud+0x14C 00 05 C2 BF C6 C6 C9 FF
poke u8 gBrHud+0x178 255
poke u8 gBrHud+0x0D 4
hold UP 10
wait 20
poke u8 gBrHud+0x178 255
poke u8 gBrHud+0x0B 0
wait 2
expect u8 gBrHud+0x09 7
shot hud-up
say mart-greet
watch gMain+4 4
watch gBrHud 16
watch 0x0600F800 2048
watch 0x0600A720 6368
watch windows 0
watch 0x0400000A 6
watch 0x0600C800 4096
watch 0x0600D800 4096
watch 0x0600E800 4096
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4096
wait 2
tap A
wait 60
unwatch
# her greeting waits for A, and the choice comes up with the next line
say mart-ask
watch gMain+4 4
watch gBrHud 16
watch 0x0600F800 2048
watch 0x0600A720 6368
watch windows 0
watch 0x0400000A 6
watch 0x0600C800 4096
watch 0x0600D800 4096
watch 0x0600E800 4096
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4096
wait 2
tap A
wait 60
unwatch
say mart-choice
# the clerk's box is up, the choice's first cell (2,1) is its own tile 0x008, and the
# ticker and the box are down
expectbits u16 0x0600FBC4 0x3FF 0x194
expectbits u16 0x0600F844 0x3FF 0x008
expectbits u8 gBrHud+0x09 6 0
shot choice
# SEE YA is the third row
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap DOWN
wait 10
say mart-leave
watch gMain+4 4
watch gBrHud 16
watch 0x0600F800 2048
watch 0x0600A720 6368
watch windows 0
watch 0x0400000A 6
watch 0x0600C800 4096
watch 0x0600D800 4096
watch 0x0600E800 4096
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4096
wait 2
tap A
wait 60
unwatch
say mart-bye
# the ticker's line and the box ran out behind her: a line and the box again for the
# way back
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 0F 15 12 00 00 01 00 0F C7 BB D3 00 C1 C9 CE 00 BC CC BF C8 BE BB C8
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
pokebytes gBrHud+0x14C 00 05 C2 BF C6 C6 C9 FF
poke u8 gBrHud+0x178 255
poke u8 gBrHud+0x0D 4
tap A
wait 10
say mart-gone
watch gMain+4 4
watch gBrHud 16
watch 0x0600F800 2048
watch 0x0600A720 6368
watch windows 0
watch 0x0400000A 6
watch 0x0600C800 4096
watch 0x0600D800 4096
watch 0x0600E800 4096
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4096
wait 40
unwatch
say mart-after
expect u8 gBrHud+0x09 7
expect u16 0x0600FAC2 0xF154
expect u16 0x0600FC42 0xF1C8
shot hud-back
say hud vram mart ok
