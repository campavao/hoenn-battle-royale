# menu-byte: gBrBattle.menu says which choice menu is up (the page's tap-to-choose,
# Cam's phone UI of 2026-09-18). 0 out of a menu, 1 on FIGHT/BAG/POKeMON/RUN, 2 on the
# four moves, back to 1 after B, 0 the moment a choice is made. The Safari's own menu
# is 3 (safari-menu-byte.txt).
#
# The page walks the cursor with D-pad taps and confirms with A through the same
# handlers a thumb drives, so what it needs from the ROM is only this byte and the two
# cursors -- both exported to br-symbols.json now.
wait 30
# BR_BOOT_MAP | TESTMON, male, Route 102 (0, 17), x=20 y=9 -- the middle grass patch
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 0
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
wait 240
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
# through "Wild X appeared!" and "Go! Y!" to the menu
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 90
# the action menu is open: 1
shot action
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
expect u8 gActionSelectionCursor 0
# the cursor moves the way the page will move it: RIGHT is bit 0, DOWN is bit 1
tap RIGHT
wait 6
expect u8 gActionSelectionCursor 1
tap DOWN
wait 6
expect u8 gActionSelectionCursor 3
tap UP
wait 6
tap LEFT
wait 6
expect u8 gActionSelectionCursor 0
# FIGHT: the move menu is 2
tap A
wait 30
shot moves
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 2
# ...and B is the action menu again
tap B
wait 30
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
# choose a move: the byte drops to 0 the frame the choice leaves the controller
tap A
wait 30
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 2
tap A
wait 10
shot chosen
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 0
say menu byte ok
