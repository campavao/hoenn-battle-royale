# ferry-fog: aboard the SS TIDAL you are where the boat is, not in the fog (PR #76 review).
#
# The boat's three maps have no section of their own: MAPSEC_DYNAMIC, whose region-map
# entry is all zeroes, so the ring measured them as the top-left corner (0,0) and a ride
# was fog, FOG! and the bleed from the gangway to the landing. Emerald's own region map
# knows better (region_map.c, GetSSTidalLocation): Slateport while boarding, then Routes
# 134, 133 and 132 by the step count, 131 halfway, Lilycove when it has made land.
#
# In SLATEPORT HARBOR (9:9) at (8,11), with the ring on SLATEPORT CITY (8,10) and r 2:
# the harbour and ROUTE 134 inside, the corner far outside.
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 8
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 09 09 08 00 0B 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
# RING from seat 0: phase 1 at (8,10), r 2, no place.
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 01 08 0A 02 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 60
expect u8 gBrRing 1
expect u8 gBrRing+5 0
# "May I see your ticket?", "...flashed the ticket. Where to?", LILYCOVE, YES.
tap UP
wait 10
tap A
wait 60
hold A 4
wait 60
tap A
wait 40
tap UP
wait 10
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
hold A 4
wait 60
tap A
wait 500
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 25
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 41
tap B
wait 60
tap B
wait 60
tap B
wait 60
tap B
wait 30
# Aboard at DEPART_SLATEPORT with no steps yet: ROUTE 134, inside. A bleed comes every
# few seconds outside; ten seconds aboard and none.
poke u16 gBrRing+10 0
wait 600
shot aboard
expect u8 gBrRing+5 0
expect u16 gBrRing+10 0
# ...and past the ring the voyage is in it. VAR_CRUISE_STEP_COUNT (0x404A, at
# *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1430) at 150 is ROUTE 132 (15,10), seven sections east of the centre.
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1430 150
wait 120
expect u8 gBrRing+5 1
# Made land in LILYCOVE (VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE 4, at +0x1504): Lilycove, also outside.
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1504 4
wait 60
expect u8 gBrRing+5 1
# Boarding at Slateport again (1): inside.
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x1504 1
wait 60
expect u8 gBrRing+5 0
