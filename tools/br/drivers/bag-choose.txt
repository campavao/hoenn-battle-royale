# bag-choose: a trainer's team lies in their bag (Cam, 2026-10-27, HBR-82: "we should
# just keep the Pokemon in the bag. Instead when clicking on a bag I guess it should give
# options for POKEMON and BAG. Pokemon brings you to this new trade screen. Bag brings
# you to an easy to pick up list w/ a quick action to take all").
#
# Seat 3's SPILL with TORCHIC (0x118), ZIGZAGOON (0x120) and the bag all on the cell we
# face, (12,17), south of where we stand: one object, the bag, with the balls under it.
# A asks POKeMON or BAG; POKeMON opens the swap screen on the two balls; BAG takes the
# bag and lists its GIVE.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
# SPILL: type 11, framed len 38 = hdr(23 00 00) + seat 03, group 00, num 09, count 02,
# rows (key, x, y, species, level), then hasBag 01, key 0x03FF, (12,17), no items,
# money 100, no name
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0B 26 23 00 00 03 00 09 02 00 03 0C 00 11 00 18 01 05 01 03 0C 00 11 00 20 01 05 01 FF 03 0C 00 11 00 00 64 00 00 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
# three rows, one object: the bag (row 2) is the cell's head
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 3
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 1
expect u8 gBrLoot+12 0xFF
expect u8 gBrLoot+32 0xFF
expectne u8 gBrLoot+52 0xFF
# and the ticker says "{A} Take the BAG": "the" at +0x22
expect u8 gBrHud+0x0C 1
expect u16 gBrHud+0x22 0xDCE8
shot packed
tap A
wait 40
shot chooser
# nothing taken yet: the box is asking
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 0
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 3
# POKeMON: the swap screen, the drop side the two balls
tap A
wait 150
shot swap
expectne u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
# A picks TORCHIC, START moves it over, B is done
tap A
wait 10
tap START
wait 30
shot moved
tap B
wait 150
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 2
# TORCHIC's ball is taken; ZIGZAGOON still lies in the bag, and the bag is still the head
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 1
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 2
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 1
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 0
expect u16 gBrLoot+26 0x120
expectne u8 gBrLoot+52 0xFF
shot after-swap
# BAG: DOWN, A. The bag is taken (its PICKUP out, the cash in), and the list waits for
# the GIVE.
tap A
wait 40
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap A
wait 20
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 2
expect u8 gBrLoot+51 0
expectmsg 10 u16 1 0x03FF
# GIVE [count 2] (ORAN BERRY x2) (NUGGET x1)
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 8B 00 02 6E 00 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 30 gDecompressionBuffer 7
wait 40
shot list
# nothing in the pockets until a row is picked
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x790 0
# DOWN to ORAN BERRY, A: the berries are in, the row is gone
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap A
wait 20
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x790 139
shot took-one
# B: the NUGGET goes back down as the bag it was, under its key on its cell, solid (it
# opens its list again), and with ZIGZAGOON under it the cell is packed again
tap B
wait 30
expectmsg 11 u8 3 0
expectmsg 11 u8 4 1
expectmsg 11 u16 5 0x03FF
expectmsg 11 u16 7 12
expectmsg 11 u16 9 17
expectmsg 11 u8 11 1
expectmsg 11 u16 12 110
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 2
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 1
expect u8 gBrLoot+53 0
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
shot left-behind
say bag choose ok
