# drop-swap: A on a ball of a drop with another in reach opens the TRADE menu's look,
# our party left and the drop right (Cam, 2026-10-27: "you could see your inventory on
# the left, and the lootable one on the right and transfer between em"; its side titled
# BAG). One of the drop goes to the party and leaves the ground, one of ours goes over and lands beside us as a
# released ball, and the rest never moved.
#
# spill-pickup's SPILL with three species: MUDKIP (0x11B) on the cell we face, TORCHIC
# (0x118) and ZIGZAGOON (0x120) beside it, and the bag. Our party is a TREECKO.

wait 120

# the screen is up, the cursor on the drop's MUDKIP, the balls still on the ground
expectne u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 4
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 0
# A picks MUDKIP, LEFT to TREECKO (past the party's empty cell) and A picks it,
# START moves both across at once (Cam, 2026-10-27: "we should be able to move multiple
# at a time")

wait 10
tap A
wait 10

wait 30
