# tm-teach: a TM off the ground goes straight to the teach screen (Cam, 2026-10-14:
# "moves that are picked up should immediately go into the teach screen"). br_loot.c's
# Take queues it and BrMoves_Tick opens the bag's own TM party screen once it is in the
# bag and the field is ours; teaching spends it and the screen goes back to the field.
#
# TREECKO at 5 knows POUND and LEER. A TM06 TOXIC (294) under our feet, as moves-spend
# puts it (see there for the loot and TM-pocket conventions).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 5
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x690 0
pokebytes gBrLoot 01 00 0C 00 10 00 26 01 00 09 00 03 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
wait 60
tap A
wait 150
shot teach
# taken, in the bag, and the party screen is up on its own
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 1
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x690 294
expectne u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
# TREECKO: "TREECKO learned TOXIC!", and A past it
tap A
wait 120
shot learned
tap A
wait 120
# spent, and back on the field with the controls ours
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x690 0
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
shot field
hold RIGHT 20
wait 30
expectne u16 gBrOwnPos+2 12
say tm teach ok
