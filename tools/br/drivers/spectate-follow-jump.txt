# spectate-follow-jump: following a seat on our own map but past the object box jumps the
# camera there, with no warp (HBR-42, 10-20 play-test: "swapping between players should be
# instant"). FollowTick used to DoWarp to the map we were already on -- a fade to black, the
# map's music and SE_EXIT, a second of black -- because no ghost had spawned out there.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 9
# seat 1 placed in LITTLEROOT's far corner (19, 19) -- 26, 26 with MAP_OFFSET -- facing
# south, fourteen tiles east and eleven south of us: past the box
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 1A 00 1A 00 01 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 10
expect u8 gBrSeats+16 1
expect u8 gBrSeats+25 0xFF
shot before
# FOLLOW seat 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 14 04 01 00 00 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 4
# still on LITTLEROOT, no warp latched, and the view is on their cell already
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 9
expect u8 gBrSpectate+14 0
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0 19
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 19
shot jumped
# their ghost spawns in the box that came with the view, and the camera rides it
wait 20
expectge u8 gBrSpectate+9 1
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 9
shot following
say spectate follow jump ok
