# spill-own-items: a trainer who goes out drops their items in the bag, not just the money
# (HBR-87). Cam's rule (HBR-82): A on a beaten trainer's bag offers BAG, a list of what
# they carried with Take all. The page holds a bag's rows for the match off its SPILL and
# GIVEs them to whoever takes it (match/session.ts giveBag), so the rows have to be in the
# spill: SpillOwn wrote itemCount 0 and every human's bag was money alone. Bots' bags,
# filled by the host page, always carried theirs.
#
# fog-out.txt's walk-out: one TREECKO, finished by the fog. Key zeroed so the quantities
# read as poked: ITEMS POTION x3 and ANTIDOTE x1, POKe BALLS the dealt POKe BALL x5,
# BERRIES ORAN BERRY x2, and a TM01 the bag leaves out (live TMs are HBR-86).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
poke u32 *gSaveBlock2Ptr+0xAC 0
pokebytes *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0D 00 03 00 0E 00 01 00
pokebytes *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x650 04 00 05 00
pokebytes *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x690 21 01 01 00
pokebytes *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x790 8B 00 02 00
poke u16 gPlayerParty+0x56 2
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 01 19 0C 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
waitfor u16 gPlayerParty+0x56 0 300
wait 30
expect u8 gBrMatch 3
# the spill: seat, map, one mon (9 bytes), hasBag at 13, the bag's key and cell, then
# itemCount at 20 and its rows from 21, pocket by pocket: POTION x3, ANTIDOTE x1,
# POKe BALL x5, ORAN BERRY x2, then the money. Before, data+20 was 0 and the money stood
# at 21.
expectmsg 11 u8 3 1
expectmsg 11 u8 13 1
expectmsg 11 u8 20 4
expectmsg 11 u16 21 13
expectmsg 11 u8 23 3
expectmsg 11 u16 24 14
expectmsg 11 u8 26 1
expectmsg 11 u16 27 4
expectmsg 11 u8 29 5
expectmsg 11 u16 30 139
expectmsg 11 u8 32 2
say spill own items ok
