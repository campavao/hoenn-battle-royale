# spike-wild: spike B's page asks the hidden ROM for the wild battle a marked spot on
# another map would start (br_wild.h). The ROM stands in Littleroot, as spike B parks it
# (web/spike-b/rom.ts); the ask names ROUTE 102 (0:17), and the battle comes off Route
# 102's land table, not Littleroot's (which has none). The backdrop is the spot's own
# (BrWild_Environment): grass for tall grass, the pond for Route 102's pond. It ends
# with a run (a Poke Doll in the bag, run-wild-keeps-doll.txt), answers DONE with the
# outcome, and a second ask starts a second battle from the field it came back to.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 D3 C9 CF FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 4
wait 2
# map 0:17, a tall grass spot (MB_TALL_GRASS 0x02), then ASKED
pokebytes gBrWildAsk+1 00 11 02
poke u8 gBrWildAsk 1
wait 10
expect u8 gBrWildAsk 2
expectne u16 gBrWildAsk+4 0
expectne u8 gBrWildAsk+6 0
wait 600
shot wild
expect u16 gBattleTypeFlags 4
# BATTLE_ENVIRONMENT_GRASS (0): the spot's own backdrop
expect u8 gBattleEnvironment 0
tap B
wait 300
tap B
wait 300
poke u32 *gSaveBlock2Ptr+0xAC 0
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0x50
poke u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x562 1
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap A
wait 240
shot ran
tap A
wait 300
# DONE, B_OUTCOME_RAN
expect u8 gBrWildAsk 3
expect u8 gBrWildAsk+7 4
# the same again on its pond (MB_POND_WATER 0x10): 0:17 has a water table too
pokebytes gBrWildAsk+1 00 11 10
poke u8 gBrWildAsk 1
wait 10
expect u8 gBrWildAsk 2
wait 600
shot water
# BATTLE_ENVIRONMENT_POND (5)
expect u8 gBattleEnvironment 5
say spike wild ok
