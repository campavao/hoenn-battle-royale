# skin-spectate: a watched fight draws both fighters in their own looks (POK-411).
# spectate-watch.txt's BSTART, with seat numbers riding in the gender bytes: side 0 is
# seat 0 (BEAUTY, skin 5, female), side 1 is seat 1 (HIKER, skin 4).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
pokebytes gBrSeats 01 05
pokebytes gBrSeats+16 01 04
# genders: (seat + 1) << 1 | female
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 78 56 34 12 0E 00 00 00 BD BB C7 FF 00 00 00 00 BD BB C8 FF 00 00 00 00 03 04 01
copy gDecompressionBuffer+29 gPlayerParty 100
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer+129 01
copy gDecompressionBuffer+130 gPlayerParty 100
mailsend gBrMailbox 18 gDecompressionBuffer 230
wait 10
expect u8 gBrSpectate+5 1
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 00 03 00 00 01 01 03 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 12
wait 300
shot intro
wait 100
shot sent
wait 800
expect u32 gBattleTypeFlags 0x8300000C
expect u8 gBattleOutcome 0
shot battle
say skin spectate ok
