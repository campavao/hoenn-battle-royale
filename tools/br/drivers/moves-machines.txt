# moves-machines: MOVES is one screen, and the machines in the bag are on it (POK-279).
#
# Play-test: "I had a Wingull and when I clicked on MOVES from the Party screen, it
# kicked me out and said there were no moves. If I went to my bag manually though and
# used FLY, it was available to teach." Both true -- the relearner only ever offers
# level-up moves, and a machine used to be taught from the bag -- and the first answer
# to that was a line pointing at the bag. Cam's call was that MOVES should BE the screen.
#
# So this is the same L5 mon that had nothing before: the relearner has no level-up move
# to offer it at that level, and the list is therefore machines or nothing. It opens.
#
# moves-row.txt is the other half: a mon that HAS level-up moves still gets them, with
# the machines appended after.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 5
tap START
wait 40
tap A
wait 80
tap A
wait 40
shot menu
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap A
wait 240
# The relearner is up on a mon the old gate would have turned away, and every row on it
# is a machine from the bag: GiveTheRunOfHoenn deals the eight HMs and nothing else.
shot machines
expect u8 gBrCatch+2 0xFF
say moves machines ok
