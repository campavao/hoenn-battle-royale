# emote-busy: what everybody else is doing, over their head (POK-266, Kanto v0.29.0).
# The engage already refuses a trainer who is in a menu or a battle (POK-230) and
# nothing showed it, so a trainer standing still in a fight looked exactly like one
# standing still waiting to take yours.
#
# A ghost two tiles east, then BUSY says it is fighting. The bubble fires on a three
# second timer, so the shot is taken after one.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# seat 1 placed two tiles east of us, facing west
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 0E 00 0F 00 03 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
expect u8 gBrSeats+16 1
# BUSY: type 17 (0x11), framed len 5 = hdr(02 00 00) + seat 01, kind 02 (BR_BUSY_BATTLE)
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 11 05 02 00 00 01 02
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeatBusy+1 2
# The bubble fires on the message, not on the three-second repeat, so this frame has
# it: the moment somebody steps into a fight is the moment it is worth showing.
wait 20
shot bang
# it goes quiet again when they do
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 11 05 02 00 00 01 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeatBusy+1 0
wait 220
shot quiet
say emote busy ok
