# emote-busy: what everybody else is doing, over their head (POK-266, Kanto v0.29.0).
# The engage already refuses a trainer who is in a menu or a battle (POK-230) and
# nothing showed it, so a trainer standing still in a fight looked exactly like one
# standing still waiting to take yours.
#
# And the mark stays up for as long as they are busy (2026-10-05 play-test: "it pops up,
# fades out, then pops back up after a few seconds -- it should persist until the user is
# done with their action"). pret's icon is gone when its 60-frame animation ends; a busy
# ghost's is held on its last frame until BUSY says otherwise.
#
# A ghost two tiles east, then BUSY says it is in a menu, then a fight, then neither.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# seat 1 placed two tiles east of us, facing west
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 0E 00 0F 00 03 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
expect u8 gBrSeats+16 1
# BUSY: type 17 (0x11), framed len 5 = hdr(02 00 00) + seat 01, kind 01 (BR_BUSY_MENU)
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 11 05 02 00 00 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeatBusy+1 1
# The mark fires on the message, and is still up four seconds on: well past pret's 60
# frames and past the old three-second repeat that used to bring it back.
expectge u8 gBrGhostMark+1 1
wait 240
shot question
expectge u8 gBrGhostMark+1 1
# into a fight: the "?" comes down and a "!" goes up in its place
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 11 05 02 00 00 01 02
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 60
expect u8 gBrSeatBusy+1 2
expectge u8 gBrGhostMark+1 1
shot bang
# ...and it goes when they are done
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x10D8 11 05 02 00 00 01 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 4
wait 20
expect u8 gBrSeatBusy+1 0
expect u8 gBrGhostMark+1 0
wait 120
shot quiet
say emote busy ok
