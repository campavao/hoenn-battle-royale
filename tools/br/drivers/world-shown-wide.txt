# left and right, include/br/br_field.h). Rustboro City (21,11), pret's box being the
# columns 2 past the LCD's left and 2 past the view's right (21-2 .. 21+17, object
# coords). Seats 15 and 16 are put on our row 10 columns left of the box's left and 2
# right of its right: with nothing shown past the view the ROM gives them no object; once
# the page says it shows the wide view's 128 columns each side the box reaches 8 columns
# further left and 7 further right, and both seats get one. Then the page says nothing
# again, the seats still there, and the box lets them go.
