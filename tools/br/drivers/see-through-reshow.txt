# see-through-reshow: shutting a menu in a see-through battle comes straight back. pret's
# way back is a hardware fade, which dims the key off pure blue along with everything else,
# and the page keys only pure blue: a blue battle for the length of the fade, every time a
# BAG or the POKeMON shut (Cam, 2026-11-01 video, 6:15, "a blue screen that comes up").
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrSeeThrough 1
poke u8 gBrBattle+14 1
pokebytes gBrPatch+16 1A 00
pokebytes gBrPatch+32 10 00
poke u8 gBrPatch+4 1
tap LEFT
wait 4
tap LEFT
wait 840
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
# BAG. With the fade it took 74 frames to come back from B, and 57 without.
tap RIGHT
wait 6
tap A
wait 90
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 0
tap B
waitfor u8 gBrBattle+8 1 65
# the battle's back, and no fade over it
expectbits u16 gPaletteFade+6 0x8000 0
shot back
say see-through reshow ok
