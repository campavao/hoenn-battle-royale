# objects-no-template: a ghost or a ball the match puts on a map has no template in the
# map's object table (SpawnSpecialObjectEventParameterized keeps its template on the
# stack), and two engine lookups read one anyway, through NULL.
#
#   - RemoveObjectEventByLocalIdAndMap sets the object's flag, read from NULL+0x14. On the
#     modern build UBFIX returns 0 there; on agbcc -- the release -- it read the BIOS's
#     open bus, 0xC002 under mGBA, and FlagSet(0xC002) set bit 2 of the byte 4 KB past the
#     special flags (gWeather's, on this layout) every time a ghost moved away or a ball
#     was taken.
#   - An A press on one looked up its script at NULL+0x10 and ran whatever that was --
#     which open bus it read depends on the last IRQ or SWI, so on most presses it was
#     harmless and on some it was not: loot-full-pocket.txt's press on a ball warped the
#     modern build into BRENDAN's house once POK-328 moved a lag frame.
#
# sSpecialFlags is static; it sits 0x24 past gSpecialVar_0x8000 in event_data.c's EWRAM,
# so the byte FlagSet(0xC002) writes is gSpecialVar_0x8000 + 0x24 + (0xC002-0x4000)/8.
# What is there differs between the builds (gWeather on agbcc, the map buffer on modern),
# so it is copied aside -- into the pad of loot row 7, which nothing reads -- and held to
# that copy.
#
# Littleroot, where loot-full-pocket.txt boots: facing south at (12,16), with a match on
# (PLAY) so nobody walks up to talk.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
copy gBrLoot+0x99 gSpecialVar_0x8000+0x1024 1
# PLACE seat 2 on 0:9 at (15,16), three tiles east, in view: it gets an object
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 02 01 00 09 0F 00 10 00 01 01 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
expectne u8 gBrSeats+0x29 0xFF
# ...and away to Route 101: the ghost is taken off the map, and no flag goes with it.
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 02 0E 0B 00 00 02 01 00 10 0A 00 0A 00 01 01 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 10
expect u8 gBrSeats+0x29 0xFF
expectsame u8 gSpecialVar_0x8000+0x1024 gBrLoot+0x99
shot ghost-gone
# A ball one tile west, from seat 1: SPILL, one mon, key 0x0101 at (11,16), species 25 at 5.
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 0B 11 0E 00 00 01 00 09 01 01 01 0B 00 10 00 19 00 05 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 30
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA1 1
# Turn to it (a hold of 8 frames is a turn) and press A: the ball is ours -- there is room
# in the party -- and taken off the map by our own ROM, still with no flag, and the press
# ran no script: the field is still up and we are still in Littleroot.
hold LEFT 8
wait 20
tap A
wait 60
shot taken
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 1
expect u8 gBrLoot+0x0B 0
expectsame u8 gSpecialVar_0x8000+0x1024 gBrLoot+0x99
expect u32 gMain+4 CB2_Overworld+1
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 9
say objects no template ok
