# shuffle-vofs: a cave trainer's SHUFFLE transition scrolls BG1..3 by the camera's whole
# value, bit 8 and all (POK-329). The ring's BGs are 512 rows (include/br/br_field.h), so
# VOFS is 9 bits there. Shuffle_Init filled the HBlank's line buffer with pret's memset,
# a byte fill, so every line read (cameraY & 0xFF) * 0x101: 256 rows off whenever the
# camera's bit 8 is set. For the Init frame and the one after it -- until Shuffle_End's
# first DMA -- the LCD showed the ring 16 metatiles away. Ripple and Wave store u16s.
#
# A cave's trainer fight is the SHUFFLE when our lead's level is the higher
# (GetTrainerBattleTransition). Rusturf Tunnel: HIKER MIKE stands at (32,13) facing left
# with sight 3; we boot at (30,16), three rows below his line, and walk up into it. A map
# load puts the camera at 40 (the standing pan and its 8), each step up takes 16 off, so
# three steps up leave it at -8: 0x1F8 in nine bits, bit 8 set, bit 0 clear -- the byte
# fill reads 0x0F8. gSpriteCoordOffsetY is the camera's negation (field_camera.c).
#
# tools/br/shuffle-vofs.py reads every frame of the line buffer from a sentinel on:
# the first frame it changes is the Init's fill, and every line must be the camera; each
# frame after, while the field is still up, within the wobble Shuffle_End adds.
# checked-by: tools/br/shuffle-vofs.py
wait 30
# BR_BOOT_MAP | TESTMON, male, Rusturf Tunnel (group 24, num 4), x=30, y=16
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 18 04 1E 00 10 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 24
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 4
expect u16 gSpriteCoordOffsetY 0xFFD8
# our Treecko at 100, so MIKE's lead is the lower level and the fight is the SHUFFLE
poke u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 100
# a turn, then up until he sees us at (30,13): his eyeline stops the walk there
hold UP 60
wait 60
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 13
expect u16 gSpriteCoordOffsetY 8
say rest
dump gSpriteCoordOffsetY 2
shot spotted
# a sentinel in the first and last lines of the buffer the HBlank reads
poke u16 gScanlineEffectRegBuffers+0x780 0x5A5A
poke u16 gScanlineEffectRegBuffers+0x8BE 0x5A5A
say watch
watch gMain+4 4
watch gScanlineEffectRegBuffers+0x780 320
# his lines, then the transition
wait 240
tap A
wait 120
tap A
wait 120
tap A
wait 120
tap A
wait 300
unwatch
shot fight
expectbits u16 gBattleTypeFlags 8 8
say shuffle vofs ok
