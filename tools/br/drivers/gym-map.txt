# gym-map: the leaders on the MAP, grayed once beaten (2026-10-05 play-test: "don't
# announce gym leader defeats in the bottom banner -- show gym leader icons on the map,
# grayed out when defeated").
#
# Each leader is a 16x16 head over their town, the top of their own standing frame. A
# leader somebody else beat arrives as NPCOUT (POK-287), the same message that takes the
# sprite off every map; one we beat ourselves goes through BrGym_Beaten. TATE&LIZA are
# two sprites, and beating either is beating the gym.
#
# The MAP is two below the top of START in a match (ring-map.txt).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 gBrGymsBeaten 0
# NPCOUT: seat 3 beat ROXANNE (RUSTBORO GYM, 11:3, local id 1); seat 5 beat LIZA
# (MOSSDEEP GYM, 14:0, local id 9)
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 1F 07 04 00 00 03 0B 03 01
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 1F 07 04 00 00 05 0E 00 09
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 10
expect u8 gBrGymsBeaten 0x41
# ...and the fog taking a gym is not beating it
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 1F 07 04 00 00 FF 0A 00 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 10
expect u8 gBrGymsBeaten 0x41
tap START
wait 40
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap A
wait 240
shot gyms
say gym map ok
