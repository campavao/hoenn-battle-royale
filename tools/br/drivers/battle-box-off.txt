# battle-box-off: gBrHud.noBattleBox (PAGE WRITES, a phone's page sets it with noCorner)
# keeps the battle's own FIGHT/BAG box and move box off the screen, under the page's big
# windows (Cam, 2026-10-15): bg0 stays on the message box, with "What will X do?" in it,
# while the menus still take their keys -- the page's windows choose through them
# (web/src/touch.ts chooseBattle). Its text turns its own pages. Back to 0, the boxes are
# back.
wait 30
# BR_BOOT_MAP | TESTMON, male, Route 102 (0, 17), x=20 y=9 -- the middle grass patch
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrHud+0x17B 1
# a patch one step west, as menu-byte.txt has it
pokebytes gBrPatch+16 1A 00
pokebytes gBrPatch+32 10 00
poke u8 gBrPatch+4 1
tap LEFT
wait 4
tap LEFT
wait 600
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
# No A through "Wild X appeared!" and "Go! Y!": on a phone the battle's text runs on by
# itself (BrBattle_PhoneAutoScroll), as it does over a link.
wait 240
# the action menu takes keys, and the screen shows the message box, not the menu's
shot action-off
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
expect u16 gBattle_BG0_Y 0
tap RIGHT
wait 6
expect u8 gActionSelectionCursor 1
tap LEFT
wait 6
expect u8 gActionSelectionCursor 0
# FIGHT: the move menu, off the screen too
tap A
wait 30
shot moves-off
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 2
expect u16 gBattle_BG0_Y 0
# the page lets go mid-menu (the phone turned on its side): the move box is back at once
poke u8 gBrHud+0x17B 0
wait 4
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 2
expect u16 gBattle_BG0_Y 320
# ...and B: the action menu that comes back is the ROM's own again
tap B
wait 30
shot action-on
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
expect u16 gBattle_BG0_Y 160
# and taken again mid-menu, off at once
poke u8 gBrHud+0x17B 1
wait 4
expect u16 gBattle_BG0_Y 0
poke u8 gBrHud+0x17B 0
tap A
wait 30
shot moves-on
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 2
expect u16 gBattle_BG0_Y 320
say battle box off ok
