# netlink-loop: a CHALLENGE opens the netlink session and starts a link battle; with
# loopback on, our own blocks come back as the peer's and the battle-start exchange
# (header, party in three blocks, seed) completes into the battle screen.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
# CHALLENGE: type 5, framed len 7 = hdr(04 00 00) + challenger 00, opponent 01, nonce 00 00
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 05 07 04 00 00 00 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 6
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink+1 0
expect u8 gBrNetlink+2 1
expect u8 gWirelessCommType 5
wait 600
# five blocks went out: header, party x3, rng seed
expectge u16 gBrNetlink+12 5
expectge u16 gBrMailbox+0x08 6
shot battle
say netlink loop ok
