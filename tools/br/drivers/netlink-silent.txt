# netlink-silent: a link battle with nobody on the other end. engage.txt is this driver
# with loopback on, so our own blocks come back as the peer's and a real fight starts;
# with it off, nothing ever answers -- which is what a peer closing its tab in the
# handshake looks like, and what a challenge to a seat with no ROM behind it used to
# look like (bot-spotted.txt).
#
# There was no way out of that. The battle waits for blocks that are not coming, on a
# black screen, with the controls locked, for the rest of the match: the play-test's own
# freeze, and the only thing a player could do about it was reload.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
# No `poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1` here: no loopback, no peer.
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 0F 00 10 00 03 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
expect u16 gBrEngage+4 1
# BR_ENGAGE_WAIT, and then the link battle starts on faith, the way it does against a
# person (engage.txt).
wait 120
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
expect u8 gWirelessCommType 5
# Nothing has been heard from them at all.
expect u16 gBrNetlink+14 0
shot waiting
# BR_NETLINK_HELLO is ten seconds. Nothing is pressed: a battle nobody can finish is
# not something to press through.
wait 700
# The session is closed and we are back in the overworld, on our own feet.
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
expect u8 gWirelessCommType 0
shot back
# ...and their seat is on the fleer's lockout, because their ghost is still standing
# in our eyeline and the engage would challenge it again the moment the grace was up.
expect u8 gBrEngage+6 1
# The field is our own again: a step is taken and the START menu opens, which between
# them are the overworld's callback1, its input and its windows.
expect u16 gBrOwnPos+4 16
hold DOWN 60
wait 20
expectge u16 gBrOwnPos+4 17
tap START
wait 60
shot menu
tap B
wait 30
#
# ...and it does so in every fight, not just the first after boot (POK-330 #7). The
# count that tells the watchdog the peer has answered was never reset, so once any fight
# had heard a single block, the next peer that went silent in the handshake froze us
# again, the same black screen, for the rest of the match.
#
# One fight that hears from its peer: seat 2 (placed well out of our eyeline) challenges
# us, and one block comes back from it -- a zero header, which is enough to move the
# start exchange on to the parties. Then seat 2 goes out, and the fight is ours
# (out-peer.txt).
# PLACE seat 2 at (20,8) facing SOUTH; CHALLENGE 2 -> 0
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 01 00 09 14 00 08 00 01 01 02
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 300
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink+2 2
# BT from seat 2: seat, seq u16 = 1, len u16 = 4, four zero bytes
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 01 00 04 00 00 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 6 gDecompressionBuffer 9
wait 30
expect u16 gBrNetlink+14 1
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02
mailsend gBrMailbox 9 gDecompressionBuffer 1
wait 200
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
# The next challenge is from somebody who says nothing at all: seat 3, on this map, out
# of our eyeline. The watchdog must see it for what it is.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 03 01 00 09 14 00 0A 00 01 01 03
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 03 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 30
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink+2 3
wait 700
expect u8 gBrNetlink 0
expect u8 gWirelessCommType 0
# B_OUTCOME_FORFEITED: nobody fought, and nothing was heard from this one.
expect u8 gBrNetlink+11 9
expect u16 gBrNetlink+14 0
shot back-again
say netlink silent ok
