# engage-menu: a menu is not a hiding place. With the START menu open a CHALLENGE
# from seat 1 lands; the menu closes on its own and the link battle starts (loopback
# so the start exchange completes into the VS screen).
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# the eyeline only fires in a started match (gBrMatch.phase = BR_PHASE_PLAY)
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
tap START
wait 40
shot menu
# Seat 1's ghost on our map, out of our eyeline -- a challenge comes from a trainer
# standing on our map (POK-330 #24) -- then its CHALLENGE: challenger 01, opponent 00
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 01 00 09 14 00 08 00 01 01 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 6
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink+1 1
expect u8 gBrNetlink+16 0xFF
wait 20
shot closed
wait 700
expect u8 gWirelessCommType 5
shot vs
say engage from a menu ok
