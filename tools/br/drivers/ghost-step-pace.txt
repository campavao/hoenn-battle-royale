# ghost-step-pace: a ghost walks at the pace its STEP says (POK-376). Inferred from the
# backlog alone, a runner's ghost walked its first two steps and a cyclist's took four to
# find the bike's pace -- a lurch at the start of every run, and the camera of whoever
# watched lurched with it. Byte 8 of a STEP is the pace its sender took it at (br_wire.h).
#
# Seat 1 (ghost-walk.txt's PLACE) steps east once at each pace, each step alone after a
# rest: the very first walk is the one asked for. Then an older sender's 8-byte STEP,
# which says nothing, is walked at the backlog's pace as before.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 02 0E 0B 00 00 01 01 00 09 0E 00 0F 00 04 01 01
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 4
# its object is gObjectEvents[2]: currentCoords.x at 2 * 0x24 + 0x10, movementActionId at + 0x1C
expect u8 gBrSeats+25 2
expect u16 gObjectEvents+0x58 14
# running: walk_fast, MOVEMENT_ACTION_WALK_FAST_RIGHT
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 04 0F 00 0F 00 00 09 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 9
wait 3
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x64 0x18
expect u16 gObjectEvents+0x58 15
wait 60
# the Mach Bike flat out: walk_faster, MOVEMENT_ACTION_WALK_FASTER_RIGHT
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 04 10 00 0F 00 00 09 02
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 9
wait 3
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x64 0x30
expect u16 gObjectEvents+0x58 16
wait 60
# off the bike and walking: straight back to walk_normal, MOVEMENT_ACTION_WALK_NORMAL_RIGHT
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 04 11 00 0F 00 00 09 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 9
wait 3
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x64 0x0B
expect u16 gObjectEvents+0x58 17
wait 60
# an 8-byte STEP: no word on its pace, and a lone step after a rest is a walk
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 01 03 10 00 0F 00 00 09
mailsend gBrMailbox 3 gDecompressionBuffer 8
wait 3
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x64 0x0A
expect u16 gObjectEvents+0x58 16
wait 60
shot rest
expect u8 gBrSeats+26 0
say ghost step pace ok
