# event-map: an event's mark on the MAP (speed-up phase 6), gray once its legendary is
# taken.
#
# The host drops an event as ordinary SPILLs and one EVENT that says where (br_event.c):
# n, the section, and the legendary's key. A PICKUP of that key from anybody grays the
# mark; a PICKUP of another piece does not. Another match's marks are forgotten when its
# seed is not ours.
#
# The MAP is two below the top of START in a match (ring-map.txt).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x1234
expect u8 gBrEvents+16 0
# EVENT 0 at ROUTE 116 (0x1F), legendary 0xB000; EVENT 1 at FORTREE CITY (0x0B), 0xB010
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 20 08 05 00 00 00 00 1F 00 B0
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 20 08 05 00 00 00 01 0B 10 B0
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 10
expect u8 gBrEvents+16 3
expect u32 gBrEvents 0x1234
expect u8 gBrEvents+12 0x1F
expect u8 gBrEvents+13 0x0B
expect u16 gBrEvents+4 0xB000
expect u16 gBrEvents+6 0xB010
expect u8 gBrEvents+17 0
# A PICKUP of event 1's second piece (0xB011) is not its legendary...
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1098 0A 0B 08 00 00 03 11 B0 00 00 00 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
wait 10
expect u8 gBrEvents+17 0
# ...and seat 3 taking event 0's legendary grays its mark
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x10D8 0A 0B 08 00 00 03 00 B0 00 00 00 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 4
wait 10
expect u8 gBrEvents+17 1
# an out-of-range n and a section past MAPSEC_NONE are dropped
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1118 20 08 05 00 00 00 04 1F 40 B0
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1158 20 08 05 00 00 00 02 D5 20 B0
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 6
wait 10
expect u8 gBrEvents+16 3
tap START
wait 40
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap A
wait 240
shot events
tap B
wait 120
# A new match: the old marks go with the first EVENT of the new one
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x5678
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1198 20 08 05 00 00 00 02 22 20 B0
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 7
wait 10
expect u32 gBrEvents 0x5678
expect u8 gBrEvents+16 4
expect u8 gBrEvents+17 0
say event map ok
