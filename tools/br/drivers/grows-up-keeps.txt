# grows-up-keeps: the rung teaches moves, and never takes back one the player chose
# (POK-290). Cam's rule, from Kanto: "if a user assigned a move, we never overwrite that
# move. But if they didn't assign a move, then we can overwrite it."
#
# This is the sharp end of it. TOXIC is a TM move, so it is not in TREECKO's learnset at
# all and LearnLevelOf scores it 0 -- the FIRST thing an automatic learn would take. It
# survives only because teaching it from MOVES marked the slot.
#
# grows-up.txt is the other half: the same lift with nothing chosen, where POUND and LEER
# are replaced wholesale. See moves-spend.txt for the loot and TM-pocket conventions.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 5
# TM06 TOXIC (294) on the tile under us, taken with one press
pokebytes gBrLoot 01 00 0C 00 10 00 26 01 00 09 00 03 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
wait 60
tap A
wait 90
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B0 294
# MOVES on the party screen, four down to TOXIC (CUT, STRENGTH, FLASH, ROCK SMASH, TOXIC)
tap START
wait 40
tap A
wait 80
tap A
wait 40
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap A
wait 240
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap DOWN
wait 20
shot on-toxic
tap A
wait 150
tap A
wait 150
# taught, and the TM is spent
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x6B0 0
shot taught
# Out of MOVES and back to the field. The cursor is on CANCEL after the teach (the list
# lost a row and the window closed up under it, POK-301); A there asks "give up trying to
# teach a new move?" and YES is the answer. Then the party screen and the start menu,
# each with its own fade.
tap A
wait 150
tap A
wait 200
tap B
wait 150
tap B
wait 200
shot back-outside
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA2 1
# ...and now seventy levels at once
poke u8 gBrRing+1 5
poke u8 gBrRing 1
wait 120
expect u8 gPlayerParty+0x54 75
shot grown
# Into a fight, where gBattleMons is decrypted: species at +0, the four moves at +0x0C.
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 03 BC C9 CE 01 15 01 05 13 00 13 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 CE CC BF C5 C9
mailsend gBrMailbox 23 gDecompressionBuffer 106
wait 10
# Seat 31's ghost on our map (out of our eyeline), then its CHALLENGE: a challenge
# comes from a trainer standing on our map (POK-330 #24).
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 01 00 09 14 00 08 00 01 01 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 600
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 90
tap A
wait 300
shot fight
# SCEPTILE, two evolutions up from the TREECKO that was taught the move...
expect u16 gBattleMons 279
# ...and TOXIC (92) is still in slot 2, the slot GiveMoveToMon put it in at level 5 when
# POUND and LEER held the first two. The three around it are the best SCEPTILE's own
# learnset has to go with it (POK-311) -- AGILITY (97), SLAM (21), LEAF BLADE (348) -- so
# the rung filled everything it was allowed to and stopped at the one it was not. A TOXIC
# nobody chose is only worth 42 to the rule and PURSUIT would have had its slot: that is
# grows-up.txt's set, and the difference between the two is the mark.
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x10 92
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x0C 97
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x0E 21
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x12 348
say grows up keeps ok
