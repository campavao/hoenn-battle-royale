# fog-out-menu: the fog finishes the team while the START menu is up, and the player
# opens POKeMON from it. The party screen resets every task on its way in, the fog's
# OUT task with them -- and with nothing else to send it, the player was never out and
# the match never ended (review of the 10-14 batch). Back on the field the task starts
# again: told, then out. fog-out.txt's setup.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
# TREECKO on 4 of 20: one bleed leaves 2, the next one finishes it
poke u16 gPlayerParty+0x56 4
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 01 19 0C 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 60
expect u8 gBrRing+5 1
shot hp-in-corner
waitfor u16 gPlayerParty+0x56 2 300
# the menu is up when the last bleed lands
tap START
wait 30
waitfor u16 gPlayerParty+0x56 0 300
wait 30
expect u8 gBrRing+7 1
expect u8 gBrMatch 2
# POKeMON, the top row: its screen resets the tasks
tap A
wait 120
tap B
wait 120
# back on the field with the menu: close it, and the fog says its piece
tap B
wait 90
expect u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u32 gStringVar4 0xBFBFCCCE

tap A

tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
