# drop-timeout: the other half of POK-223. A trainer who never touches the map still
# lands: twenty seconds in, the section under the cursor is the choice and `pick` goes
# out on its own. Nobody is kept waiting by a tab nobody is looking at.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 82 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0E 15 12 00 00 78 56 34 12 01 78 00 78 00 00 00 00 19 0A 00 28 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 4
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 0D 06 03 00 00 00 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 200
expect u8 gBrPick 1
# the clock is running and nobody is pressing anything
expectle u16 gBrPick+8 1200
wait 1260
# it chose on its own: the map is gone and we are waiting on the host's cell
shot timedout
expect u8 gBrPick 1
# ...which arrives, and the drop happens like any other

poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 3
