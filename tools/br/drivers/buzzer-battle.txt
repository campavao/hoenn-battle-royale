# buzzer-battle: the opening's buzzer closes a catch that is still running (POK-261,
# Kanto v0.27.0). Without it the opening ends for everybody standing on a map and waits
# for whoever is in a battle -- with the ring already closing outside and the drop they
# are owed still to come.
#
# Seed 0x0C00 lands this ROM in the Zone's SOUTHWEST area at (8,5); the grass is five
# steps south and two west. Holding A gets through the encounter's intro -- the message
# box waits for a press, which is what POK-269 turned out to be.
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x00000C00
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 02 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gBrMatch 1
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 2
# the opening is running with two minutes on it, so the buzzer is ours to fire
poke u8 gBrMatch+1 1
poke u16 gBrMatch+12 120
# into the grass
hold DOWN 140
hold LEFT 100
hold RIGHT 60
hold LEFT 60
hold RIGHT 60
# B, not A, and tapped rather than held. A held button is ONE press and every box on
# the way in waits for its own -- but A on the Safari's action menu throws a ball, so a
# run of them catches the thing instead of leaving it there for the buzzer. B advances
# a message box just as well and chooses nothing.
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
shot in-battle
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
# the fog goes up while we are still in there
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 01 19 0C 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
# The getaway has a box of its own -- "Got away safely!" waits like every other one.
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 30
wait 200
shot after
# The buzzer pressed RUN for us and the opening is over. gBattlersCount is NOT the test
# -- it keeps its last value after a battle ends -- so the phase is: the ROM was in the
# Zone with a battle running, and it is out of the opening now. Nothing was caught (B
# chooses nothing), so that means OUT.
expectge u8 gBrMatch 2
# and the fog it left into is really up
expect u8 gBrRing 1
say buzzer battle ok
