# house-cache: a house has a ball in it (Cam, 2026-10-13: "houses get item caches"), the
# same one for every ROM, and the first to take it takes it for everybody.
#
# PETALBURG CITY's HOUSE1 (map 8:2) is sHouses[33]. Under seed 0x12345678 its cache is
# TM24 THUNDERBOLT (312), a gold one (br_balls.c, CacheItem). The boot names (3,6) and
# leaves us just inside the door.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 08 02 03 00 06 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 0x12345678
wait 30
expect u16 gBrLoot 0x8D21
expect u16 gBrLoot+6 312
expect u8 gBrLoot+8 8
expect u8 gBrLoot+9 2
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 3
# on the table (Cam's 2026-10-19 play-test, #8): (3,5), its south-east corner
expect u16 gBrLoot+2 10
expect u16 gBrLoot+4 12
shot inside
# Out of the door: the cache stays behind, out of the table. (Row 1 is the DAY CARE's
# chest, which is always there.)
hold DOWN 90
wait 200
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 0
shot outside
# ...and back in: it is put down again, where it was
hold UP 40
wait 200
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 3
expect u16 gBrLoot 0x8D21
expect u16 gBrLoot+2 10
shot back-in
# Somebody else takes it -- seat 1, PICKUP of key 0x8D21 -- and it goes from here too
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0A 0B 08 00 00 01 21 8D 00 00 00 00 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 20
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 0
# ...and for good: out and back in, and there is nothing
hold DOWN 90
wait 200
hold UP 40
wait 200
expect u8 gBrLoot+11 0
shot gone
say house cache ok
