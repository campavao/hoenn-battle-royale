# seam-midframe: gBrMidFrame says when a VBlank lands mid main-loop iteration
# (2026-10-07 play-test: "flashing happening as I move between routes").
#
# Crossing a map seam loads the next map inside CameraMove, and the load runs past a
# VBlank or two. The page reads RAM at each one, and there the struct is half-written:
# the map is already the new one, the position not yet stepped onto it (pret sets it
# from the connection, loads the map, then adds the step). The page drew those frames a
# whole tile off. gBrMidFrame is 1 from BrFrame to BrFrameEnd, and the page holds its
# last whole frame while it is.
#
# Booted in MAUVILLE CITY (0:2) at (16,16); DOWN walks out the south gap into ROUTE 110
# (0:25). The run is deterministic: the two torn frames follow 25 frames of DOWN.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 02 10 00 10 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 2
expect u8 gBrMidFrame 0
hold DOWN 25
# Mid-load: ROUTE 110 already, y still -1 (the connection's, before the step's +1).
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 25
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 0xFFFF
expect u8 gBrMidFrame 1
hold DOWN 2
# The iteration finished: on its first row, and whole again.
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+2 0
expect u8 gBrMidFrame 0
hold DOWN 30
expect u8 gBrMidFrame 0
say seam midframe ok
