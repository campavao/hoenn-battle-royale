# npcout-in-battle: an NPCOUT heard in the middle of a trainer battle leaves the object
# table alone until the field is back (POK-328).
#
# A battle keeps the map's object events and rebuilds their sprites on the way back, so
# while it is up an object's spriteId is an old number and the sprite under it is the
# battle's. The fog's sweep of the map we are fighting on (seat 0xFF), or a peer who beat
# somebody on it, used to go straight to RemoveObjectEvent -> DestroySprite, and took
# sprites 1 and 2 -- the foe's healthbox -- out from under this fight. That is the lead
# for Cam's black screen after a Mossdeep Gym trainer: he had dropped into the fog.
#
# Route 102: CALVIN (local id 2) stands at (33,14) with sight 3; booting at (33,15) puts
# us under him, facing up. RICK (local id 3) is in view eight tiles west. Both are in the
# object table, CALVIN in slot 1 on sprite 1 and RICK in slot 2 on sprite 2.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 21 00 0F 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# A started match at rung 30, as trainer-drop.txt has it.

poke u8 gBrMatch+1 1
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0C 0A 07 00 00 00 03 19 0C 3C 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 30
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x2C 2
expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x50 3
expect u8 gBrLoot+0xA3 0
# Talk to CALVIN, then through his challenge and the intro to the action menu.

expect u8 gObjectEvents+0x4C 2
expectbits u8 gSprites+0x82 1 1
expectbits u8 gSprites+0xC6 1 1
# NPCOUT x2, the fog's (seat FF): type 31, framed len 7 = hdr(04 00 00) + FF 00 11 <id>
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 1F 07 04 00 00 FF 00 11 02

# byte 0), and the battle's sprites 1 and 2 still in use (Sprite.inUse, bit 0 of +0x3E).
# The old ROM had cleared all four.

# Held, CALVIN was still there when the battle ended, so his team is on the ground where
# he stood. Taken off a map mid-fight the old way, BrLoot_TrainerBeaten found no object
# and dropped nothing.
expectge u8 gBrLoot+0xA0 1
# ...he is remembered as beaten (the sweep keeps him off this map), and not counted twice
# for it: he was already on his way.
expect u8 gBrDespawned 0

# ball, which is drawn where he stood (local id 0xC0 is the first piece of loot).
