# bot-held-item: a bot's mon fights with what it holds (Cam's 10-19 play-test, #22: "it
# would be cool to also see them apply those items, if this Pelipper was holding
# Leftovers"). The page gives a LEFTOVERS a bot picked up to a mon (web/src/bots/bag.ts,
# equip) and the card's PackedMon carries it at +24; BrBot_BuildMon puts it on, and
# Emerald's own end-of-turn line plays.
#
# trainer-ball-refused.txt's fight, the bot's TREECKO on 10 of 19 HP holding LEFTOVERS
# (200, row byte 24 = card byte 30).
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 03 BC C9 CE 01 15 01 05 0A 00 13 00 00 01 00 23 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 C8 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 05 CE CC BF C5 C9
mailsend gBrMailbox 23 gDecompressionBuffer 106
wait 10
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 01 00 09 14 00 08 00 01 01 0F
mailsend gBrMailbox 2 gDecompressionBuffer 11
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 1F 00 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 5 gDecompressionBuffer 4
wait 400
# the intro's lines scroll themselves; their length follows TEXT (HBR-39), so wait for
# the action menu rather than pressing through them on fixed frames
tap A
wait 100
waitfor u8 gBrBattle+8 1 900
# the foe holds it
expect u16 gBattleMons+0x86 200
# FIGHT, POUND; the turn runs to its end and LEFTOVERS gives a little back
tap A
waitfor u8 gBrBattle+8 2 120
tap A
# POUND's damage is a roll, so the check is Emerald's own line at the end of the turn:
# "Foe TREECKO's LEFTOVERS / restored its HP a little!" ("FTOV" at byte 16)
waitfor u32 gDisplayedStringBattle+16 0xD0C9CEC0 900
wait 60
expectle u16 gBattleMons+0x80 9
shot leftovers
say bot held item ok
