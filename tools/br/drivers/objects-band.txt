# objects-band: which object sprites the ROM shows past the LCD (POK-319). The band the
# core draws is 40 rows above, 56 below and 16 columns right of the LCD, and a sprite is
# visible there iff BrField_OffScreen says so -- keyed on its TOP row, because the OAM's
# 8-bit y would put a top below -40 at the band's bottom. tools/br/objects-band.py reads
# these dumps and checks every active object's sprite.invisible against that rule.
# Littleroot (8,9): the town's one outdoor NPC then stands at screen (240, 184) -- 24
# rows below the LCD and on the band's right edge, past the LCD both ways.
# checked-by: tools/br/objects-band.py
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 08 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
say pos
dump *gSaveBlock1Ptr 4
say coord-offset
dump gSpriteCoordOffsetX 2
dump gSpriteCoordOffsetY 2
say objects
dump gObjectEvents 576
say sprites
dump gSprites 4352
say ok
