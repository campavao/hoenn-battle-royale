# story-clash-link: a link battle in SOOTOPOLIS while its story is live starts in the
# clash's weather, which every ROM works out the same way: from the match seed (odd here,
# GROUDON's DROUGHT, 12), not from a task of its own (PR #38 review: WEATHER_ABNORMAL's
# local turn would have put the two GBAs of one link battle in rain and sun). With the
# ring on, which is who applies it (br_ring.c, ApplyWeather). The battle is netlink-loop's
# loopback: our own blocks come back as the peer's.
#
# In SOOTOPOLIS CITY (0:7) at (31,35). SaveBlock1's weather is +0x2E.
drain gBrMailbox
wait 30
poke u32 gBrMatch+8 9
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 07 1F 00 23 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+4 0
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+5 7
# RING phase 1 round the whole map (r 30): the city is inside
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 00 00 1E 00 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 12 gDecompressionBuffer 7
wait 10
expect u8 gBrRing 1
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x2E 2
# EVENT 0 at SOOTOPOLIS CITY (0x0E), legendary 0xB000
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 00 0E 00 B0
mailsend gBrMailbox 32 gDecompressionBuffer 5
wait 10
expect u8 gBrEvents+16 1
expect u8 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x2E 12
wait 300
shot drought
poke u8 gBrNetlink+8 1
# CHALLENGE: type 5, framed len 7 = hdr(04 00 00) + challenger 00, opponent 01, nonce 00 00
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 05 07 04 00 00 00 01 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 6
expect u8 gBrNetlink 1
wait 900
shot battle
expect u8 gWirelessCommType 5
expectbits u16 gBattleWeather 0x60 0x60
say story clash link ok
