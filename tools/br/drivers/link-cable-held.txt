# link-cable-held: the LINK CABLE on a trade evolution that wants a held item
# (EVO_TRADE_ITEM): an ONIX with nothing held gets NO USE and the cable is kept; given a
# METAL COAT it becomes a STEELIX, and the coat is spent with the cable, as the trade
# would spend it (br_items.c, BrItems_CableTarget).
#
# Our own ONIX (95) is put on our tile as a ball keyed to our seat (no trade on the way
# in), then a LINK CABLE (52) and a METAL COAT (199) by GIVE. LITTLEROOT (map 0:9).
wait 30
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 01 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
pokebytes gBrLoot 01 00 0C 00 10 00 5F 00 00 09 1E 01 FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
wait 60
tap A
wait 60
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 02 34 00 01 C7 00 01
mailsend gBrMailbox 30 gDecompressionBuffer 7
wait 60
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 52
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x564 199
# BAG > LINK CABLE > USE > ONIX: holding nothing, so NO USE, and the cable stays.
tap START
wait 40
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap A
wait 120
tap A
wait 30
tap A
wait 60
shot no-use
tap A
wait 60
shot no-effect
hold A 4
wait 60
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 52
expect u8 gPlayerParty+8 0xC9
# back in the BAG: down to the METAL COAT, GIVE it to the ONIX
tap DOWN
wait 20
tap A
wait 30
shot coat-menu
# USE GIVE / TOSS CANCEL: GIVE is to the right
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap A
wait 90
shot give-pick
tap A
wait 60
shot given
hold A 4
wait 90
shot after-give
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x564 0
# the coat is gone from the list, so the cursor is on CLOSE BAG: up to the LINK CABLE,
# USE, the ONIX -- now holding the coat
tap UP
wait 20
tap A
wait 30
tap A
wait 60
shot able
tap A
wait 120
shot evolving
wait 900
shot evolved
hold A 4
wait 120
hold A 4
wait 120
hold A 4
wait 120
shot back
# STEELIX: S T E E L I X
expect u8 gPlayerParty+8 0xCD
expect u8 gPlayerParty+9 0xCE
expect u8 gPlayerParty+10 0xBF
expect u8 gPlayerParty+11 0xBF
expect u8 gPlayerParty+12 0xC6
expect u8 gPlayerParty+13 0xC3
expect u8 gPlayerParty+14 0xD2
# the cable is spent, and the coat did not come back to the bag
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x560 0
expect u16 *gSaveBlock1Ptr+0x564 0
say link cable held ok
