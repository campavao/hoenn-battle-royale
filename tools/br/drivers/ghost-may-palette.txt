# play-test: "may sometimes has her hat go black from time to time"). BRENDAN and MAY
# both draw from the player's palette slot, 0, and whichever was loaded last coloured
# both. A ghost of the other gender now wears the RIVAL graphic -- the same picture --
# from slot 10, and one of ours the player's own.

# we are BRENDAN

# seat 1, skin 1 (MAY), one tile west; seat 2, skin 0 (BRENDAN), one tile east

poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 90

expect u8 gBrSeats+41 3

# BRENDAN as the player's own, 0
