# pace: the host's match options reach every ROM in the room (POK-241). START's pace
# byte is bit 0 "the host said something", bit 1 battle animations, bits 2..3 the text
# speed -- and a zero byte leaves this save's own options alone, which is what an older
# page sends.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
# the save's defaults before anybody says otherwise
expect u8 gBrMatch+6 0
# START with pace = 1 | 0<<1 | 2<<2 = 0x09: animations OFF, text speed FAST
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0E 15 12 00 00 78 56 34 12 01 78 00 78 00 09 00 00 19 0A 00 28 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
wait 20
expect u8 gBrMatch+6 0x09
expect u8 *gSaveBlock2Ptr+0x14 0x02
# ...and with the animations bit set, they come back on
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 0E 15 12 00 00 78 56 34 12 01 78 00 78 00 03 00 00 19 0A 00 28 00 00
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
wait 20
expect u8 gBrMatch+6 0x03
say pace ok
