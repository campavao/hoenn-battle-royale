# run-chance: RUN's window says the odds of the next RUN (Cam, 2026-11-01), and they are
# the roll's own: speed * 128 / foe's speed + 30 per try, in a u8, over 256. The number is
# worked out as the menu opens, and RUN while trapped brings the menu back with no roll and
# no turn spent, so that is how each case below gets a fresh one. gBattleMons[0] is ours,
# +6 speed, +0x50 status2 (1 << 26 ESCAPE_PREVENTION); gBattleStruct+0x6C is runTries.
wait 30
# BR_BOOT_MAP | TESTMON, male, Route 102 (0, 17), x=20 y=9 -- the middle grass patch
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 11 14 00 09 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
poke u8 gBrMatch 2
pokebytes gBrPatch+16 1A 00
pokebytes gBrPatch+32 10 00
poke u8 gBrPatch+4 1
tap LEFT
wait 4
tap LEFT
wait 600
expectge u8 gBattlersCount 2
# 30 against 47: 81 of 256
poke u16 gBattleMons+6 30
poke u16 gBattleMons+0x5E 47
poke u16 gBattleMons+0x28 999
tap B
wait 30
tap B
wait 200
tap B
wait 60
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
expect u8 gBrBattle+12 32
shot odds-32

# RUN from 1 against 255 rolls 0 and cannot get away: a real try, spent, moves the odds
poke u16 gBattleMons+6 1
poke u16 gBattleMons+0x5E 255
poke u8 gBrHud+0x17B 1
tap DOWN
wait 10
tap RIGHT
wait 10
tap A
wait 900
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
expect u8 *gBattleStruct+0x6C 1
# 30 of 256
expect u8 gBrBattle+12 12
# the sixth try off a speed just under the foe's: 127 + 150 wraps in pret's u8, to 21 of 256
poke u16 gBattleMons+0x28 999
poke u16 gBattleMons+6 127
poke u16 gBattleMons+0x5E 128
poke u8 *gBattleStruct+0x6C 5
poke u32 gBattleMons+0x50 0x04000000
tap A
wait 20
poke u32 gBattleMons+0x50 0
wait 300
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
expect u8 *gBattleStruct+0x6C 5
expect u8 gBrBattle+12 8
# trapped: 0, whatever the speeds, and the tap still says why
poke u16 gBattleMons+6 200
poke u16 gBattleMons+0x5E 10
poke u32 gBattleMons+0x50 0x04000000
tap A
wait 300
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
expect u8 gBrBattle+12 0
poke u8 gBrHud+0x17B 0
wait 4
shot odds-0
poke u8 gBrHud+0x17B 1
# faster: certain, and it is
tap A
wait 20
poke u32 gBattleMons+0x50 0
wait 300
expect u8 gBrBattle+8 1
expect u8 gBrBattle+12 100
poke u8 gBrHud+0x17B 0
wait 4
shot odds-100
poke u8 gBrHud+0x17B 1
tap A
wait 120
expect u8 gBattleOutcome 4
say run chance ok
