# spectate-late-join: a watcher handed a fight joined late gets every turn of it while
# its field is still fading out, and the replay plays every one (2026-10-13 play-test:
# "battles don't finish"). Those turns were held in 64 bytes, about six turns' worth;
# the rest were dropped mid-run, and the replay played six turns and then waited, for
# good, on bytes that had gone. Now they are held per battler on the heap.
#
# spectate-watch.txt's fight, and the page's cache of it: twelve TURNs, one message a
# turn as the fighter sent them, all arriving the frames after the BSTART. Both pick
# move slot 1 (30 PP), which knocks nobody out, so the fight outlasts the twelve.
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 00 09 05 00 08 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 78 56 34 12 0E 00 00 00 BD BB C7 FF 00 00 00 00 BD BB C8 FF 00 00 00 00 00 01 01
copy gDecompressionBuffer+29 gPlayerParty 100
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer+129 01
copy gDecompressionBuffer+130 gPlayerParty 100
mailsend gBrMailbox 18 gDecompressionBuffer 230
wait 4
expect u8 gBrSpectate+5 1
# battle 0x0100; battler 0 picks FIGHT, move 1, at 1, then the 00 its engine wrote at the
# turn's end (no fog, BrRing_FogEndTurn); battler 1 the same at 0
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 00 04 00 01 01 00 01 03 00 01 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
wait 10
expectge u16 gBrSpectate 12
# twelve turns at 1x (the page runs them at 8x): both mons used the move twelve times,
# and the replay is still in the fight, a turn behind, waiting on the thirteenth
wait 13000
expect u8 gBrSpectate+5 1
expect u8 gBattleOutcome 0
expect u8 gBattleMons+0x25 18
expect u8 gBattleMons+0x7D 18
shot twelve
# ...which, when it comes, is the thirteenth: the held turns left the record in step.
# (The battle has used gDecompressionBuffer since, so the turn is written out again.)
pokebytes gDecompressionBuffer 00 01 00 04 00 01 01 00 01 03 00 01 00
mailsend gBrMailbox 19 gDecompressionBuffer 13
wait 2500
expect u8 gBattleMons+0x25 17
expect u8 gBattleMons+0x7D 17
shot thirteen
say spectate late join ok
