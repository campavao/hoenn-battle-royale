# hud-vram-centre: the nurse's question over the HUD (POK-329). hud-vram.txt has the
# model; this is Oldale's Centre (map 2:2), where the nurse's message box takes the
# ticker's tiles and the box's last forty-eight, and her YES/NO -- (21,9) 5x4 from tile
# 0x125, framed from (20,8) -- sits where the bottom box's frame would be. Every frame
# from A to the question, through YES, and from her last line to the HUD coming back is
# watched, and tools/br/hud-vram.py checks every cell naming a shared tile, and that
# BG1..3 show the field's own tilemaps throughout.
# checked-by: tools/br/hud-vram.py
wait 30
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x2018 81 00 02 02 07 00 04 00 BD BB C7 FF
wait 400
expect u8 gPlayerPartyCount 1
poke u16 gPlayerParty+0x56 1
poke u8 gBrHud+0x00 7
poke u16 gBrHud+0x02 125
# a kill line on the ticker (TICKER, as hud.txt) and a box of our own: "HELLO", 255
# frames, dirty
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1018 0F 15 12 00 00 01 00 0F C7 BB D3 00 C1 C9 CE 00 BC CC BF C8 BE BB C8
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 1
pokebytes gBrHud+0x14C 00 05 C2 BF C6 C6 C9 FF
poke u8 gBrHud+0x178 255
poke u8 gBrHud+0x0D 4
hold UP 6
wait 10
poke u8 gBrHud+0x178 255
poke u8 gBrHud+0x0B 0
wait 2
expect u8 gBrHud+0x09 7
expect u16 0x0600F868 0xF139
expect u16 0x0600FAC2 0xF154
expect u16 0x0600FC42 0xF1C8
shot hud-up
say nurse-ask
watch gMain+4 4
watch gBrHud 16
watch 0x0600F800 2048
watch 0x0600A720 6368
watch windows 0
watch 0x0400000A 6
watch 0x0600C800 4096
watch 0x0600D800 4096
watch 0x0600E800 4096
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4096
wait 2
tap A
wait 70
unwatch
say nurse-asking
# her message box is up (its first cell (2,15) is window 0's 0x194), the box is down,
# and the YES/NO's first cell (21,9) is its own 0x125
expectbits u16 0x0600FBC4 0x3FF 0x194
expectbits u8 gBrHud+0x09 6 0
expectbits u16 0x0600FA6A 0x3FF 0x125
shot question
say nurse-yes
watch gMain+4 4
watch gBrHud 16
watch 0x0600F800 2048
watch 0x0600A720 6368
watch windows 0
watch 0x0400000A 6
watch 0x0600C800 4096
watch 0x0600D800 4096
watch 0x0600E800 4096
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4096
wait 2
tap A
wait 30
unwatch
say nurse-healing
wait 400
expectge u16 gPlayerParty+0x56 15
# the ticker's line and the box ran out behind her: a line and the box again for the
# way back
pokebytes gBrMailbox+0x1058 0F 15 12 00 00 01 00 0F C7 BB D3 00 C1 C9 CE 00 BC CC BF C8 BE BB C8
poke u16 gBrMailbox+0x0C 2
pokebytes gBrHud+0x14C 00 05 C2 BF C6 C6 C9 FF
poke u8 gBrHud+0x178 255
poke u8 gBrHud+0x0D 4
wait 2
say nurse-done
watch gMain+4 4
watch gBrHud 16
watch 0x0600F800 2048
watch 0x0600A720 6368
watch windows 0
watch 0x0400000A 6
watch 0x0600C800 4096
watch 0x0600D800 4096
watch 0x0600E800 4096
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4
watch gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg1 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg2 4096
watch *gOverworldTilemapBuffer_Bg3 4096
wait 2
tap A
wait 40
unwatch
say nurse-gone
expect u8 gBrHud+0x09 7
expect u16 0x0600FAC2 0xF154
expect u16 0x0600FC42 0xF1C8
shot hud-back
say hud vram centre ok
